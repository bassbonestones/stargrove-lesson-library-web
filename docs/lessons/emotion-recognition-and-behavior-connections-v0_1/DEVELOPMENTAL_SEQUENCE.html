PK3 responsive opportunities

PK3 E3-1: Welcome voluntary meaningful expression in comfortable everyday activity; first observe ENTRY and offer words only as options. No private disclosure required. PK3 E3-2: MODEL a contextual feeling in a benign fictional story or truthful adult example; invite recognition of varied emotional meanings, not face stereotypes. PK3 E3-3: DURING ordinary play, support the child's expressive channel. Home language, sign, gaze, AAC and gesture can convey meaning. Maintain neutral access support; record adult-supplied content honestly. PK3 E3-4: At EXIT and across available examples, distinguish recognition, expression and range. No fixed quota of contexts, days or labels. Fictional expression is not falsely called autobiographical. A partial example is useful, not whole-target mastery. REVISIT on a later date only when actually observed; NOT_OBSERVED until then.

PK4 responsive opportunities

PK4 E4-1: ENTRY may reveal a beginning connection in a familiar activity: a volunteered pleased feeling followed by showing work, with child meaning attributable. PK4 E4-2: MODEL the difference between reported feeling and guessed interpretation. One teacher can state that quietness has many explanations without another adult. PK4 E4-3: DURING, explore different possible actions as enriching teaching, not compulsory enumeration for every learner. Safe choices are not one rigid culturally universal response. PK4 E4-4: If someone freely communicates a feeling, respect it; do not require a real peer, partner confession or self-disclosure when a fictional learning route fits. PK4 E4-5: EXIT can use familiar or different context. Credit beginning meaningful connections without requiring formal causal speech, novelty or an uncertainty qualifier. REVISIT is genuinely later observation, NOT_OBSERVED until it occurs; continued familiar play is valid.

Delivery and practical boundaries

ADULT-LED uses one adult and one child with familiar art/book materials and accessible communication. CO-PLAY is responsive shared activity, not a testing drill. CLASSROOM keeps a teacher supervising visible normal activity; observe one or two willing children while others continue familiar choices, rotate opportunities over time, and stop assessment if the room needs attention. No group chorus or peer answer is individual proof. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED and not required for these human paths.

All methods permit stopping, privacy, culturally varied expression and disability access. No induced distress, forced facial performance or care withholding.
