Choose a mechanism, not a compulsory ladder

Method 01 welcomes genuine voluntary expression during familiar art/reading. Its facilitator guide gives one-adult and class logistics. Method 02 (not yet in reviewed library) uses fictional story alternatives. Its guide (not yet in reviewed library) is historical and requires separate sibling consistency review before qualification. Method 03 (not yet in reviewed library) concerns consenting real-human communication. Its guide (not yet in reviewed library) likewise remains unqualified. A fictional character is not a real partner; observing a real partner does not prove the target child's own private feeling. This overview is not a fourth counted lesson. DOT19 repairs do not qualify sibling lessons.

Shared evidence boundaries

ENTRY listens before supplying meaning. MODEL illustrates a truthful adult feeling or clearly fictional example. One educator can describe alternatives; no second adult is a general prerequisite. DURING welcomes child-controlled accessible practice. EXIT notices the child's contribution in familiar or different material. Familiarity is not a failure. Adult answer supply cannot be credited as child understanding; support duration and disability assistance alone do not establish answer supply. Unknown meaning stays uncertain. REVISIT means a genuinely later date; same-day work is practice, not later retention. No later observation is invented.

PK3 concerns recognition AND expression of varied emotions, not a fixed number of contexts/days or English labels. Preserve partial evidence. First-person disclosure is optional. Fictional role expression can be described honestly as such, not falsely as a personal feeling. PK4 begins to connect emotion and behavior: accessible brief contextual meaning may count without a formal explanation, two alternatives or an uncertainty phrase. Teaching alternatives protects against stereotypes but is not an extra source gate.

Practical safeguards

Use normal child-chosen art or books, usual communication aids and a visible supervised pause space. Stop at refusal, fatigue, distress or supervision needs. Supply requested care immediately. Never induce negative emotions, force facial imitation, infer feelings from appearance, or demand a public disclosure. Validate feelings while preventing harm. Follow existing safeguarding procedures for concerns; keep identifying information confidential.

CO-PLAY and ADULT-LED can be ordinary responsive interaction. CLASSROOM uses manageable visible normal activities and a few optional individual opportunities over time, never choral mastery.
