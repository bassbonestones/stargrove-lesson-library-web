1 · Play a safe pretend adventure

PK4.VIII.C.1: learner actually CREATES OR RECREATES a STORY, MOOD OR EXPERIENCE through a meaningful personally enacted or child-directed DRAMATIC REPRESENTATION. One type is enough; pictures and passive listening alone are not source evidence.

What safe familiar or invented character/action will the learner choose and enact? What role does their personal gesture or voice represent?

Meaningful dramatic action

Invite voluntary pretend play; offer a brief model if helpful, even before a child chooses a role. Observe meaningful enactment in a familiar or new scene; retain access supports and record adult help. Re-creation is valid. Later return is unobserved until it occurs.

Open creative invitation:

“What would you like to pretend is happening? Can you show that story, mood OR experience in your own safe way? What did your action represent?”

Original image is only a scene prompt. The child must actually portray a role/action; don't count watching an animation or pointing to static star. No child dramatization observed by these original art prompts.
