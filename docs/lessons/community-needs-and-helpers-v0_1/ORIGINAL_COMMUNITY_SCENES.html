Food and nourishment

Fictional people need nourishment. Many different meals/ingredients can provide it; do not require a specific culture's preferred meal or brand.

Appropriate clothing

Different weather calls for suitable coverings and comfort. Many types of clothing are possible; do not grade brand, fashion or household resources.

⌂

Safe shelter

People need a safe place for shelter. Many forms of housing and shared safe spaces exist; no family form is more worthy.

Who Helps, and What Do They Do?

ADULT TEACHING EXAMPLES WITH ANSWERS. Not an independent-check page • page 2 / 3

PK4.VII.B.3 discusses roles and responsibilities of FAMILY, SCHOOL and COMMUNITY helpers. Naming a career title or matching uniform isn't a full discussion. Roles vary by family/community; avoid gender stereotypes and treating any occupation as necessarily trustworthy to a child.

Family roles

A caregiver, relative or another trusted family helper may prepare a safe space, share supplies or support a child. Households differ.

School roles

A school custodian keeps learning spaces clean; a teacher supports learning. Actual actions matter, not a uniform.

Community roles

A librarian helps find information; sanitation workers help maintain shared spaces. Other roles also help.

First-party fictional task cards

Different adult helpers organize safe rainwear for imaginary visitors.

A fictional shared classroom needs materials safely put away.

Adults remain responsible for child safety. Children should consult an identified trusted adult in actual safety concerns; role names alone are not permission to approach strangers. No real pupils' family details are collected.
