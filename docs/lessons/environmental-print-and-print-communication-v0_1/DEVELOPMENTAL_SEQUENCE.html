Environmental print: responsive teaching options

PK3.III.A.3 — “Child notices and connects meaning to environmental print.” PK4.III.A.3 — “Child recognizes that all print carries meaning and serves as a means for communication.” 2022 TEA Guidelines, printed/PDF page 30, original two-column page visually/textually compared by DOT22 on November 13, 2026. Research-cell SHA PK3 5c999b709441527cc98e41703b2825b9d2a8d2e4031d563ecb233a3fb1045304; PK4 29a9e338951074869f4e6c39ed8c7e00c1bf080d17689b6e3519b6d59bd41b27.

These local options support planning, not a required developmental ladder or diagnostic sequence. Familiar meaningful labels, child-origin questions about writing and pointing to words during read-aloud are legitimate. No independent decoding, novel-context quota or automatic rejection of modeled/familiar materials. One event supports a limited inference, not universal print mastery.

  • PK3 P3-1: notice or ask about writing in a familiar place, with read-aloud access.
  • PK3 P3-2: connect a label's words with its real object/place using action or language.
  • PK3 P3-3: explore a genuine notice/invitation when such a message is useful.
  • PK3 P3-4: optionally explore a different environment; do not erase familiar-context evidence.
  • PK4 P4-1: recognize a label can tell someone something, including a purposeful question about its message.
  • PK4 P4-2: compare real print purposes over suitable opportunities, not a same-session quota.
  • PK4 P4-3: consider a real or potential reader without requiring peer interaction.
  • PK4 P4-4: recognize print as meaning-bearing with adult reading access, not independent decoding.
  • PK4 P4-5: extend toward varied print uses; one label does not prove universal mastery.

ENTRY / MODEL / DURING / EXIT / REVISIT

ENTRY can be spontaneous print interest or model-first teaching. MODEL can be one adult using the same real label; record it as adult teaching. DURING allows child-owned meaningful use/questions with neutral reading access. EXIT is a modest observation of the actual child action and help, not necessarily novel. A transfer claim needs an actual relevant change, not a new calendar date alone. REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED until it occurs; familiar later use can inform recognition without claiming transfer or durable mastery.

ADULT-LED and CO-PLAY use the truthful label in the child's environment. CLASSROOM observations occur within one teacher's normal supervised activity, with missed opportunities deferred and no chorus-as-individual credit. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. No classroom effectiveness or mode-parity claims.

Scope of this correction
