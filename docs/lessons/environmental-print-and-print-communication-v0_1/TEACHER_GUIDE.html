Family overview: environmental print

This is an uncounted objective-level overview, not a fourth lesson. Current DOT22 candidate: Real place labels with its companion.

PK3.III.A.3: Child notices and connects meaning to environmental print. PK4.III.A.3: Child recognizes that all print carries meaning and serves as a means for communication. Original TEA 2022 printed/PDF page 30 was visually and textually checked by DOT22. Questions about text and following words during read-aloud are legitimate emergent behaviors. One label is a focused opportunity toward broad communication recognition, not universal mastery.

For Lesson 01 use one safe actual object/place, a truthful readable label, paper/marker/tape and optional private notes. Print packet page 1 has child labels; page 2 contains optional messages; page 3 is adult-only guidance. No pictures or full storybook are supplied. Handwriting and familiar home-language labels are valid. Adult owns cutting, safety and supervision; no extra adult, forced decoding, unfamiliar context or genre quota.

ENTRY may be child interest or model-first support. MODEL is one adult visibly using print. DURING invites child action/questions and responsive support. EXIT notes actual print-related meaning and help on familiar or changed print. Neutral reading is access; giving the concept answer is teaching. Transfer claims require relevant changed context; REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED until a genuine later opportunity. No mandatory calendar-day barrier or automatic retention proof.

Teach one child by following their chosen familiar place. Teach a class through ordinary supervised activities, observing a few individuals while others play/read or watch; defer when safety/access prevents an observation. Never score chorus, peer or adult action as individual understanding. Keep notes private and modest, without diagnosis or public rankings. Established accessible print/communication supports should preserve meaning; inaccessible print is not child failure.

Historical siblings, not qualified by DOT22

Lesson 02 centers genuine notices/invitations and recipients; Lesson 03 centers comparisons across print functions. Their original files are preserved for future separate repair/review, not endorsed instructional prerequisites. Known legacy constraints include compulsory novel EXIT, implied second-adult modeling and overstated supplied materials. Current Lesson 01 does not depend on those files. Shared resource/helper corrections must be considered when those siblings are reviewed; neither acceptance nor field validity is conferred.

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. Current human CO-PLAY/ADULT-LED/CLASSROOM paths are written design only. No observed children, external educator validation, physical/tactile printing or four-mode empirical parity.

Separate instructional faults from reported evidence

The synthetic helper keeps two axes: reported child source-action evidence and adult instructional/access faults. An unnecessary decoding demand must be repaired, but does not erase meaningful print understanding a child actually showed. A prohibited safety duty stops the task even if meaningful child action was reported; never interpret preserved evidence as permission to continue unsafe/coercive instruction. If print access was not established, preserve observations but mark the print inference not assessable, not child inability. The helper stores reported facts and never certifies a task or child. Its explicit perceptible-print field admits established accessible literacy in context, including nonvisual literacy; legacy visible-print names must not impose a visual-only requirement. Audio-only naming or arbitrary texture is not automatically equivalent writing.
