Three distinct original physical teaching contexts with conservative safety boundaries

A — genuine flat-ground running and ground-level jumping; PK4 isolated balance check. A trusted adult uses suitable unobstructed level age-safe space, footwear/surface checked and age-appropriate close supervision; no public road or race. PK3 separately demonstrates actual basic RUNNING and JUMPING in place/from level ground (not off height). Adults observe real locomotor mechanics/skill ability rather than app animations or isolated short step substitutes. This covers two of four required PK3 actions, not the entire outcome; genuine climbing AND pedaling needed elsewhere before whole-source mastery claim. PK4 has a DISTINCT isolated coordination/balance exercise on stable level floor: purposeful gentle weight-shift/reach and return to comfortable centered stance, not a required run-jump-sequence or one-leg stunt. Accessible individualized alternatives invite learning but do not mislabel omitted physical skills as observed mastery.

B — genuine low child-rated climbing; PK4 isolated control. Adult selects appropriate professionally made low child-rated play structure suitable for learner and follows actual equipment guidance, closely supervises; no furniture climbing, slippery/high/insecure props or homemade elevated hazards. PK3 actually demonstrates basic safe CLIMBING using hands/feet on appropriately low stable suitable apparatus; this is separate third skill. PK4 separately demonstrates genuine coordination/balance in isolation on FLAT stable ground before/after apparatus, such as small balanced reach and recovering stable stance; do not convert a climbing sequence into same isolated balance evidence without authentic separate task.

C — actual supervised age-appropriate PEDALING; PK4 isolated coordination/balance. Suitable approved child-sized stable tricycle/pedal apparatus set up on flat private clear traffic-free ground with protective equipment appropriate to manufacturer/local rules, direct trusted adult oversight and no street riding. PK3 physically demonstrates basic PEDALING with own alternating leg drive, not just an adult pushing pedal vehicle or tapping virtual pedals. PK4 separately shows isolated body coordination/balance on stable ground or suitable safely stationary position; not inferred solely from pedaled travel speed. Completing all four basic PK3 running/jumping/climbing/pedaling must be supported by real distinct observations for “masters” claim, not one cute sequence combining pictures.

Keep these physical contexts safe and low-risk, adult-controlled setup/age-appropriate oversight essential. Use mobility/sensory/vision/hearing/home-language adaptations and nonjudgmental observed evidence. If any true skill is not performed or safe to offer, mark NOT_OBSERVED rather than asserting full printed mastery, and coordinate appropriate individualized practice with qualified educators.
