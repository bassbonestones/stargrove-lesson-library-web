Exactly which distinct practical mechanism to teach

Produce independently chosen amounts by placing safe distinct physical objects into a blank space.

Original authored method's concrete learner actions and feedback model

  • Original material: ORIGINAL_MAKE_MATS.html; large safe washable objects, or child-chosen marks/stickers/digital dots, with adult handling any cutting; blank entry/practice/exit workspaces and an adult-private per-quantity observation. Printable layout contains no sample preconstructed answer pattern or numeral-number line visible to child. No small beads, choking hazards or demanded paper-pencil skills.
  • Prerequisites: Child can communicate a response or reliable intentional choice in some accessible mode, with meaningful opportunity to practice an amount; no prerequisite handwriting or printed numeral recognition. Clarify number word in child's home language/sign/AAC and provide a neutral accessible way to request repetition. Child may count while constructing; method of verifying the amount is not substituted for making it.

Two fundamentally distinct skills: Adult asks “make four,” child places four large blocks in their chosen arrangement = child represents. Adult already placed four blocks, child correctly says “four” = adult-built set with child counting/cardinality, not independent production. Child taps a picture labelled 4 that causes the app to auto-fill four objects = app produced representation, not child. A child who places no pieces after hearing zero might be responding correctly or refusing; assess zero via explicit intentional communication (“zero means none; this mat should stay empty”) and a changed later task, not silence.
