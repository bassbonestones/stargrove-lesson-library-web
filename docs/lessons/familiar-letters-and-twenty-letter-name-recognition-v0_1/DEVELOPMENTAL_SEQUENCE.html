Spanish learners may learn sounds before names. Uppercase may be recognized earlier. A never-taught glyph, new room, different case or fixed calendar deadline is not a source requirement.

Progressions are options, not a staircase every child must pass

PK3 L3-1: Notice a personally meaningful familiar initial or print letter. Observe before helping if useful; teach immediately when requested. PK3 L3-2: Recognize some frequent letters in books, labels or names. A known word/logo is not proof of all its letter identities. PK3 L3-3: Offer gentle alphabet-song/play. Document actual participation separately from recognition, and preserve voluntary nonspoken participation without falsely claiming literal singing. PK3 L3-4: Explore another familiar context when interesting. Same-card recognition remains valid; broader transfer is an additional observation, not a condition for earlier evidence. PK4 L4-1: Recognize and name a familiar displayed letter in either case. A spoken, signed or AAC name can count when the learner chooses it and the identity is verified. PK4 L4-2: Add identities as interest and readiness permit. Count A/a once; don't count repeated displays as new letters. PK4 L4-3: Find familiar letters in meaningful print without demanding word reading. PK4 L4-4: Notice names versus sounds and related case forms. A sound-only response is useful related knowledge; both-case mastery is not required. PK4 L4-5: Build a documented repertoire of at least twenty different recognized-and-named identities over manageable observations. No one-session quota, fixed exact set, new-font test, writing or pronunciation norm is added.

ENTRY, MODEL, DURING, EXIT, REVISIT

ENTRY invites a child-chosen meaningful letter and records only what actually occurs. MODEL can teach that same letter or a different one; the adult's answer is not the child's evidence. DURING offers contingent comparison, gentle letter play and repeated meaningful encounters. EXIT is a willing uncued response to an accessible familiar glyph; it need not be novel. Distinguish immediate echo from later recognition of something taught. REVISIT is an actually later occasion, possibly in the same context; optional variation explores transfer. All actual child outcomes and REVISIT are NOT_OBSERVED in these authored files.

Teach with large dark letters, only a few visible choices, familiar communication, home language and comfortable pacing. Do not withhold access assistance to make a check look independent. Keep actual response, access help, answer cues and uncertainty in separate plain-language fields. PK3 song/play and recognition remain separate, so one initial is not a complete outcome claim. PK4 records glyph/case and name per identity, not presentations. Twenty documented names do not demonstrate permanent mastery, later retention or readiness.

Methods and scope

Method 01 uses personally meaningful initials or chosen familiar print. It is the current DOT25 candidate, not accepted. Method 02 uses environmental signs, and Method 03 explores varied letter displays. Their historical files have not received this full applied examination. Source-level novelty and case corrections here apply to the family, but do not qualify or endorse those sibling plans. They require their own substantive reviews before acceptance. No sibling is counted anew.

ADULT-LED and CO-PLAY: one adult follows the child's choice, models responsively, distinguishes teaching and uncued evidence, and returns later without pressure. CLASSROOM: one teacher uses a supervised shared print exploration with meaningful materials for others while observing willing focal learners only when safe; chorus is not individual evidence. SOLO is NOT IMPLEMENTED; hypothetical digital use and four-mode efficacy are not established. All human routes are written designs, not field validation. Optional print galleries and private blank notes are resources, not required worksheets, child data or proof of accessible physical production.
