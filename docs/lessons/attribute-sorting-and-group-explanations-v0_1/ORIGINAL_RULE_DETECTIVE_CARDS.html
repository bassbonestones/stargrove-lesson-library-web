01 · One set, different defensible rules

Teacher model: compare criteria, not a state-mandated re-sort count · Original E2.2 V.E.1 teaching materials · NOT a child's independent result

FOUR ORIGINAL SCHEMATIC PIECES

By SHAPE, two circles can go together despite different colors, and two squares go together. By COLOR, two red pieces belong together despite different shapes, and two blue pieces belong together.

The properties of these objects remain the same; what changes is the sorting RULE. PK3: first master one meaningful same/different sort; changing rule is an OPTIONAL scaffold. PK4: group and describe similarities/differences under a coherent criterion. Do not mark an independently consistent alternate answer incorrect.

Teacher-only example language

“If I choose shape, these are alike because both have curved outlines. The other group differs because its outlines have corners. When I choose color instead, each group shares a color—even though the shapes differ.”
