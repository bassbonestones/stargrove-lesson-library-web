Original shared Child Behaviors permit prompted labels and character feelings. These local teaching options are not published stage prerequisites or an individual pass/fail developmental scale. A meaningful familiar or neutrally prompted contribution counts with its support recorded. No word-count, new-context, spontaneous initiation or simultaneous two-mode quota. Broad verbal/nonverbal teaching must not be misrepresented as both modes demonstrated in every event.

PK3 meaning options

PK3 F3-1 — Meet a comfortable basic word

ENTRY asks what happy might mean in Ari's original garden story. MODEL explains glad/happy using a separate adult story. DURING accepts synonyms and accessible choices, explains ambiguity. EXIT records actual meaning in the same or another understood context, not echo alone. REVISIT records later meaning only when observed.

PK3 F3-2 — Contrast meaningful words

Offer comfortable happy/sad story meanings without inducing either. Explain how someone may feel differently. Contrast is a learning opportunity, not a mandatory two-word test. Record actual semantic response and answer help.

PK3 F3-3 — Connect words to context

Explore a familiar story, play observation or child-selected example. Child may use established home language/sign/AAC; no reading requirement. A child’s own emotion report is optional, not necessary to show word familiarity.

PK3 F3-4 — Broaden when useful

Return to varied benign stories and synonyms if ready. Optional context variation can explore transfer, never invalidate familiar meaning. No requirement to teach fear to a child who finds it unwelcome.

PK4 communication options

PK4 F4-1 — Language-bearing meaning

Invite a comfortable optional message using speech, home language, sign or selected AAC. A prompted but owned message is meaningful; adult-dictated content is not independent evidence.

PK4 F4-2 — Nonverbal meaning

Attend to a child’s intentional gesture, expression, action or established accessible symbol meaning. Do not assume a face reveals a private feeling. Clarify respectfully if needed; accept uncertainty.

PK4 F4-3 — Diverse channels over time

Offer verbal and nonverbal opportunities without simultaneous performance requirements. Record channels actually present, and do not claim the full outcome from only one bounded event. Permanent access aids remain available.

PK4 F4-4 — Clarify when genuinely useful

One adult can explain a hypothetical misunderstanding and model respectful clarification without a second person. Respond to an actual child message, ask once if helpful, and accept no further disclosure. No engineered misunderstanding or new listener requirement.

PK4 F4-5 — Generalize optionally

A naturally different context may offer further evidence. Familiar meaningful contexts remain valid. A character-feeling message may contribute to source communication but is never relabeled as the learner's own feeling. A care request alone is not proof of a specific emotion.

Sequence, support and routes

ENTRY invites, MODEL explains, DURING teaches, EXIT observes bounded meaning, REVISIT is an actually later opportunity. Current learner events are NOT_OBSERVED. Neutral prompts and supported learning are preserved with provenance. Unknown/inaccessible opportunity is not diagnosis; known safety issues remain recorded even without a target response. No rigid lesson order, timing or age-exclusion rule.

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. CO-PLAY permits responsive caregiver participation. ADULT-LED is one adult with one child using the complete Lesson01. CLASSROOM uses one teacher's supervised shared area, ordinary meaningful activities and fair focal observations across later opportunities, never public compulsory emotion reporting or choral individual credit. Stop for comfort, care, privacy, fatigue or choice. No external field outcomes, measured parity, physical print or tactile accessibility claims.
