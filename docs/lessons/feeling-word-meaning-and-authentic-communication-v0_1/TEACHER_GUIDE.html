Objective educator guide — Basic feeling words and communication

This shared guide is not a counted lesson or sibling qualification. DOT26 repairs the source interpretation and common teaching boundaries; Lessons 02/03 still require their own comprehensive reviews.

Published target and distinctions

2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines, Social and Emotional Development, printed/PDF page 13: - PK3.I.B.2.b: “Child is familiar with basic feeling words (e.g., happy, sad, mad, scared).” - PK4.I.B.2.b: “Child uses verbal and nonverbal communication to communicate basic emotions and feelings.” The original Child Behaviors include prompted own-feeling labels and story/movie character feelings. Familiar meaningful contexts, neutral prompts and conventional understood expressions are legitimate. Do not demand new contexts, self-initiation, personal disclosure, audible speech or paired channels in every event. Teach verbal/nonverbal breadth across opportunities; record a single channel/event as its actual limited contribution, not full mastery. Character feeling communication is not a child's own-state report; both can be educationally meaningful. Need requests deserve care but do not establish an inferred feeling.

Three focused methods

Lesson01: explore contextual meaning through gentle original stories and optional everyday communication. Guide01. Lesson02 (not yet in reviewed library): contrast reported meanings despite similar outward behavior, resisting facial stereotypes. Guide02 (not yet in reviewed library). Lesson03 (not yet in reviewed library): respond to an optional child-owned message and clarify only when genuinely needed. Guide03 (not yet in reviewed library). These mechanisms differ; their selected own-message opportunities do not narrow the published source for every learner. No one must complete all three or pass one before another.

Responsive sequence and evidence

ENTRY invites existing meaning with access available. MODEL explains a separate adult example and labels it as teaching. DURING offers manageable practice, child choice, contingent explanation and care. EXIT observes meaning without supplying it; familiar context is acceptable. Neutral prompting is different from an adult-dictated answer. REVISIT records an actual later opportunity; familiar reuse may inform retention, optional variation may inform transfer. Both remain NOT_OBSERVED until observed. Do not erase earlier or partial evidence because a later opportunity is declined.

Use DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md as flexible teaching options, not prerequisite tests. Begin where the learner can participate meaningfully. Model one word, accept synonyms and established accessible communication, and stop or simplify when attention/comfort calls for it. Unknown translation or intent is uncertainty, not inability. Do not remove communication aids when fading answer clues.

Resources and realistic human delivery

The first-party word mats contain optional stories and communication ideas, not a compulsory four-item quiz or diagnostic face test. Read aloud/sign/describe; no required reading, cutting or worksheets. The observation pages are private teacher notes, not child disclosures to display. One adult can narrate a model and ask/answer their own explanation. No second adult is required.

One-on-one: follow the complete Lesson01 and facilitator route with the child selecting participation, story or ordinary safe play. Classroom: one teacher keeps children within safe supervision, gives a brief optional story introduction and observes feasible focal children during ordinary meaningful activities. Other children continue supported play/book/drawing choices. Rotate attention over later opportunities and defer assessment rather than abandon care. A chorus or peer answer never stands for everyone.

Access, privacy and honest scope

No induced fear, shame, compulsory eye contact, voice, acting sadness or public mood circle. Honor home language, sign/AAC, neutral child-directed motor proxy, sensory space, processing time and relevant established individual plans. Keep minimal private instructional notes under setting rules, not intimate histories. Respond to actual harm or care needs regardless of target performance. U denotes child-owned meaning, H supported meaning, A child-directed access, X unclear; these are provenance descriptions, not ranked child traits.

R04 synthetic-helper evidence contract

This optional helper is not a teacher assessment or truth detector. All supplied flags must be literal True, False or unknown None; malformed strings/numbers are rejected before every inference. Grade/source and qualitative notes are exact strings. Omitted safety/refusal judgments remain None, not assumed clean negatives. Known unsafe conditions take priority even without a target observation; notes remain intact. Unknown safety cannot grant candidacy. An unobserved/refused turn has no justified channel contribution, while submitted channel/safety assertions are explicitly unverified. A known personal OR character communication route and a known language-bearing OR nonverbal route can support a bounded PK4 contribution while an unused route remains unknown. Neither mode is invented and a single-mode event is not the full outcome. PK3 semantic meaning does not require PK4 channels. Novelty, self-initiation and prior exposure flags remain descriptive, not gates, including when unknown. Preserve earlier supported meaning and current uncertainties in qualitative notes; do not force guessed flags or erase valid observations. These shared repairs do not qualify siblings.
