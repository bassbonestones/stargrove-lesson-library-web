Little leaf, different voices

Optional adult teaching card. No child worksheet or cutting needed.

Leaf up high, leaf down low.
Leaf rests here, soft and slow.

Sing or rhythmically voice gently in your comfortable range. Hands may open softly or stay still. Let the child watch, move, try a voice, choose a different image or stop.

Repeat a favorite line. Hearing the adult model is part of mimicry, not a reason to reject it. Notice approximate language intonation, not exact musical notes or perfect words.

Make room

Pause your voice when listening for the child. Co-singing is useful practice; only describe individual evidence when the child contribution is distinguishable. Movement alone is not vocal mimicry.

A choice of little lines

Use only as wanted, not as surprise tests.

Soft round stone, sitting still.
Small safe home, near the hill.

The stone is only in the words; no real stone is needed. Or shorten to “little leaf.” Repeat, slow down, let the child lead, or choose a familiar family-language song appropriately.

Keep it comfortable

Normal voice, no forced breath, loudness, pitch precision, public solo or touch. Keep established hearing and communication supports. Do not hide your face if it helps access.

Signing, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC, such as a communication board or device) and hand play are worthwhile communication. Record the actual channel; device or adult voice is not the child’s own vocal imitation. Stop for discomfort or refusal.
