1 · Original first-party BOOK: Garden Seed: the child asks AND answers

Real original complete first-party book content

One morning, Ari found a tiny seed beside a garden path. Ari asked Grandma where seeds grow. Together they made a small hole in soft soil and put the seed in. They gently covered it and watered it. Each day they checked the same spot. After many days, a small green shoot peeked through the soil. Ari smiled and said, ‘Our seed is growing!’

2 · Original first-party BOOK: Duck's Block Bridge: toy crossing PRETEND carpet river

Real original complete first-party book content

In a playroom, a toy duck sat on one side of a blue carpet river. Jo wanted the duck to visit the toy pond on the other side. Jo and Sam laid broad soft blocks across the carpet to make a pretend bridge. They checked that the blocks stayed still. Jo slowly moved the toy duck across the pretend bridge. Everyone cheered when the duck reached the toy pond.

3 · Original first-party BOOK: After the Rain: original complete factual mini-book

Real original complete first-party book content

Rain falls from clouds. Some rainwater gathers on a path and makes a small puddle. Other water sinks into the ground. After the rain, warm sunshine can help the puddle become smaller. Some water changes into water vapor and goes into the air. We call this evaporation. Later the ground may be dry. If rain falls again, new puddles may appear.
