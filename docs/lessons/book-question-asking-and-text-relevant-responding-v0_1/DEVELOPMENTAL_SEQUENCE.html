III.D.3 — child owns book QUESTION and ANSWER PK3, relevant read-aloud TEXT question + response PK4

Official PK4.III.D.3: “Child asks and responds to questions relevant to the text read aloud.” SHA-256 a9f421b97fef7cbc25c3186bed915ba77425d6fc08c34c983ee40a75e92143ca. Both verified against frozen original official source research cells; raw original two-column printed PDF text concordance remains pending.

Source distinction: PK3 official printed text says “Child asks and answers age- appropriate questions about a book.” The hyphen spacing in “age- appropriate” is preserved; no specific READ ALOUD condition is present in PK3 D3, although reading together is a valuable accessible method. PK4 says “Child asks and responds to questions relevant to the text read aloud.” This source REQUIRES genuine TEXT read aloud at PK4, and child's question and response both connect to text. A teacher asking all questions while child only answering does not demonstrate either child's ASK. Questions and answers may be through native/home-language voice, sign, AAC or genuine child-directed gesture; no demand for exact wording, spontaneous speech without support or forced trivia.

Three originally authored book question contexts with ground-truth source

Original book A — “The Garden Seed” (complete short first-party story, read aloud): “One morning, Ari found a tiny seed beside a garden path. Ari asked Grandma where seeds grow. Together they made a small hole in soft soil and put the seed in. They gently covered it and watered it. Each day they checked the same spot. After many days, a small green shoot peeked through the soil. Ari smiled and said, ‘Our seed is growing!’” PK3 genuine child question can be “Where does the seed go?” and genuine age-appropriate answer after reading “In soil”; PK4 genuine question “What did Ari and Grandma do after putting the seed into the hole?” and relevant real text response “They covered it with soil and watered it.” Adult's question/answer cannot substitute for child's two actions. Children need not read letters or narrate all beginning/middle/end (separate D1).

Original book B — “The Duck's Block Bridge” (complete first-party pretend playroom story): “In a playroom, a toy duck sat on one side of a blue carpet river. Jo wanted the duck to visit the toy pond on the other side. Jo and Sam laid broad soft blocks across the carpet to make a pretend bridge. They checked that the blocks stayed still. Jo slowly moved the toy duck across the pretend bridge. Everyone cheered when the duck reached the toy pond.” Genuine child questions might include “Where did the toy duck want to go?” or “How did the duck cross?” and responses “Toy pond” / “Across the broad soft block bridge.” This is a CARPET pretend river, not guidance to cross real streams or build dangerous elevated structures.

Original informational book C — “After the Rain” (read all three factual panels aloud): “Rain falls from clouds. Some rainwater gathers on a path and makes a small puddle. Other water sinks into the ground. After the rain, warm sunshine can help the puddle become smaller. Some water changes into water vapor and goes into the air. We call this evaporation. Later the ground may be dry. If rain falls again, new puddles may appear.” PK3 meaningful question “Where did puddle water come from?” and answer “Rain”; PK4 child asks “Why might the puddle get smaller after rain?” and responds to question “Where does some of that water go?” with age-appropriate accurate “It becomes water vapor and goes into the air.” Exact adult scientific term “evaporation” isn't required for recognizing meaning. No touching standing water.

All books/text first-party original, not copied commercial publications. A child may ask a genuine age-appropriate question and later independently answer that same earlier inquiry after exploration, or another relevant book question; no rote Q-and-A script, unrelated prior-knowledge trivia, adult-controlled pretend character dialogue or compelled verbal mouth-only response. Sign/AAC/gesture, home language, optional quiet/hearing/vision/motor and repetition allow authentic source.

Four PK3 stages — age-appropriate asking AND answering actual book questions

PK3 Q3-1 — Ask an own simple meaningful who/what question about a REAL book

ENTRY: Offer actual original Garden Seed book content through page viewing or accessible whole story reading, child first may truly ask about a specific shown/read book event. MODEL: Adult demonstrates QUESTION about DIFFERENT Duck playroom story on adult-only material, no answer for child. DURING: Child voluntarily asks a genuine age-appropriate question such as 'Where does Ari put seed?' about actual book, then may answer a separate neutral educator question with own 'in soil' response, using sign/AAC as needed. EXIT: Fresh different original Duck book context; child really asks AND answers a book question, not teacher speaking both. REVISIT: Later new genuinely encountered book with real own question and answer, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 Q3-2 — Answer an actual age-appropriate detail question from book

ENTRY: Open or read original Duck story before child response, neutral actual who/where question without full answer, first response recorded. MODEL: Adult on DIFFERENT Garden Seed story models its own age-appropriate question and answer without child scoring. DURING: Child responds to genuine detail 'Where does toy duck go?' with personally sourced 'toy pond' and ALSO asks relevant own 'How did it cross?' or other age-appropriate book question, not merely echo the adult's rehearsed words. EXIT: Fresh complete original Rain book with child-owned actual ask and answer in developmentally supported format. REVISIT: True later different book and child two actions, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 Q3-3 — Communicate book questions and own answer through accessible modes

ENTRY: Actual first-party After the Rain book accessible as page/picture and read aloud as supported, offer learner choice/quiet and observe own question. MODEL: Adult model on unrelated toy Duck story and other source, no teacher-supplied rain question. DURING: Child may point/sign/AAC choose to ASK a true question about book rain clouds/puddle and personally ANSWER another realistic book question, with adult holding AAC board but not selecting full message. EXIT: Fresh originally authored Garden Seed book, child genuine age-appropriate two-direction Q and A, source not teacher puppet dialogue. REVISIT: Genuine later calendar date new book, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 Q3-4 — Transfer asking AND answering book questions beyond rehearsed pair

ENTRY: Access a newly authored full real original first-party book, child has authentic opportunity to ask and respond before any model. MODEL: Adult model different new original text, not upcoming response. DURING: Child actually ASKS one meaningful age-appropriate question ABOUT a BOOK and ANSWERS a genuine question about same book, with home language/sign/AAC/gesture or supported access; no compulsory PK4 text-read-aloud detail fluency bar. EXIT: New real unlike book source and genuine learner-owned question and answer, rather than fixed question button pressed. REVISIT: True later date new book two-way response, NOT_OBSERVED.

Five PK4 stages — own text-RELEVANT asking AND responding after true read aloud

PK4 Q4-1 — Ask a question truly relevant to actual TEXT READ ALOUD

ENTRY: Read full original Garden Seed text aloud FIRST with access, before adult model learner first asks true relevant question about events not merely guesses from picture. MODEL: Adult on DIFFERENT whole Duck book text models one question, not learner Garden response. DURING: Child personally asks meaningful text-RELEVANT question e.g. 'Why do they check the same soil?' and responds accurately to a separate educator's text-relevant question, not off-topic question about unrelated cartoon. EXIT: Fresh full original Duck story read aloud and child-generated genuine relevant ask and responsive text-based answer. REVISIT: Genuinely later full new read text, both actions NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-2 — Respond to actual read-text detail without adult supplying answer

ENTRY: Read original Duck's Block Bridge full text aloud, ask neutral 'What did Jo and Sam put across carpet river?' and record child first response. MODEL: Adult models different Garden Seed text questions/response unrelated to learner current story. DURING: Child responds with actual read text-specific information 'broad soft blocks', AND asks own question relevant to Duck story, such as 'How did toy get to pond?' rather than adult own Q counted as child. EXIT: New Rain full original informational text read aloud then learner asks+responds relevant to actual content. REVISIT: Truly later different actual read-aloud text Q and response, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-3 — Ask and respond using meaningful causal links to text rather than a picture cue

ENTRY: Read complete original Rain text with cloud/puddle/vapor info, child first owns a meaningful rain question. MODEL: Adult models on DIFFERENT Duck complete text, not a rain script. DURING: Learner asks meaningful 'How does water get in puddle?' and genuinely responds to appropriate question 'What can happen in warm sun?' by source-specific truthful water smaller/vapor concept, not adult naming all picture labels or demanding full scientific vocabulary. EXIT: New Garden Seed full text read aloud child asks and responds to distinct true text details without adult pre-answer. REVISIT: Actually later different whole original text read aloud, true child own relevant two-way exchange, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-4 — Stay relevant in text-linked follow-up, not just yes/no prerecorded options

ENTRY: Read full original Duck Bridge or another first-party story aloud and offer open-ended neutral turn, learner first question observed. MODEL: Adult unrelated original Rain text model illustrates its own response, never moves child Q/A puppet. DURING: Learner asks a relevant new book-text-specific question and gives own actual text-relevant response even with an adult neutral clarifying follow-up; AAC, voice, sign or gesture convey meaning, not memorized two-button script. EXIT: Truly unlike newly read Garden Seed text learner own relevant ask + actual relevant response, no off-topic generic favorited color answer. REVISIT: Genuinely later new book actual both text-source actions, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-5 — Transfer real text-relevant ASK and RESPOND to new full read aloud

ENTRY: Read genuinely unfamiliar-to-lesson full new original age-safe text aloud, listen to learner's first spontaneous relevant question and response opportunity. MODEL: Adult MODELS on a DIFFERENT full original book, no correct target wording supplied. DURING: Learner personally ASKS one text-relevant meaningful question AND responds authentically to a text-relevant question after FULL reading, no compulsory 'wh-' structure count, extended timed quiz or unrelated prior topic source. EXIT: New unlike complete original text read aloud again and learner-source both acts, with new content and true relevant answer before adult delivers it. REVISIT: Later different date actual full book text and genuinely child authored question and responsive answer, NOT_OBSERVED.

Four complete serious WRITTEN modes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future independently child-operable digital book experience must offer full original accessible human-like narrated TEXT for PK4 before source check, child-controlled page/replay/pause and optional quiet, vision, home-language and sign/AAC/motor modes. PK3 can access a genuine book by first-party readable/picture/narrated means and individually ASK and ANSWER real age-appropriate questions with own safe voice/sign/AAC/motor choices. PK4 truly ASKS a new relevant question about actual text READ ALOUD and RESPONDS to a different relevant question grounded in that read text. Pressing a generic “?” icon while automated characters supply the question or answer is NOT learner-owned source; prerecorded menu options need meaningful child-directed selection with content, not decorative gestures. Nonreader control independent of adult operator intended, software NOT IMPLEMENTED and no real child/mode efficacy tested.

CO-PLAY: Caregiver offers the genuine original book and listens for child's own age-appropriate question or enables child-authored AAC questioning, and offers neutral age-appropriate question to which learner responds. PK3 source is 'about a book' and does NOT expressly mandate that text be read aloud every time, though caregiver can read accessible book; don't fabricate that additional criterion. PK4 must have complete actual text READ ALOUD before child ASKS and RESPONDS relevant to it, in home language with sign/AAC gesture access if needed. Adult may repeat, clarify/access, but not supply whole child messages or a guessed answer before child attempt. Fresh EXIT and actually later REVISIT only if truly observed.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Educator provides authentic original age-safe book; for PK4 actually reads relevant full original TEXT ALOUD FIRST, and for PK3 can read aloud to support literacy but doesn't claim an unwritten prerequisite. Observe child ENTRY question and answer before unrelated adult MODEL on DIFFERENT book, then teach distinct real who/what/why question stems and responsive listening without compelled exact wording, standard answer length, or adult-only puppet dialogue. PK3 ASKS and ANSWERS age-appropriate book questions; PK4 ASKS and RESPONDS to questions relevant to actual read-aloud text. Give vision, hearing/quiet, home language, sign/AAC/motor and privacy; no deficit label for child unfamiliar with books or speech. Fresh unlike EXIT from new book/text and true later date REVISIT only when real.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Teacher shares first-party original safe books and actual text read aloud for PK4, gives authentic unprompted questioning opportunities and neutral open-ended text questions, but records individual child-owned meaningful ASKS and ANSWERS/RESPONDS, not teacher-generated questions or chorus as each student's performance. PK3 book engagement may be multimodal and does not need adult imposition of exhaustive content memory; PK4 must actually relate both question and response to text previously READ ALOUD. Accessible native/home-language sign/AAC/quiet/hearing/vision/motor methods, refusal respected; private observations not collected in shared source. New genuine source EXIT and later revisit not manufactured.

Source integrity and validation boundaries

Neither grade objective requires child to use only spoken English, ask a fixed number of questions, answer all 5W prompts, or recall unrelated facts. Both verbs apply: PK3 ASKS AND ANSWERS actual age-appropriate questions about a BOOK; PK4 ASKS AND RESPONDS to actual text-relevant questions after real full TEXT READ ALOUD. Child should own the asking as well as answer; adult who asks everything then counts one child response fails source. An adult-origin question can be a stimulus for the child response but not the child's own ASK. No guessing from only animated picture unrelated to actual book, recycled rehearsed teacher exact answer, or absence of source text at PK4. Safe original books, no copied copyrighted works; Duck story only low pretend carpet bridge, Rain story no unsafe puddle handling, seed planting pretend no small choking pieces. No observed learner, independent qualified educator, actual later date retention, physical/tactile printed QA, functioning independently child-only SOLO or empirical four-mode effectiveness established.
