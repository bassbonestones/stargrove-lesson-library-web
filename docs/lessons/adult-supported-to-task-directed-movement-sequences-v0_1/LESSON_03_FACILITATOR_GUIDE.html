Lesson 03 — Original Pretend Message Delivery: Complete Individual Facilitator Guide

Paired source lesson: LESSON_03_ORIGINAL_PRETEND_MESSAGE_DELIVERY.md. Family teacher guide: TEACHER_GUIDE.md. This is written instructional design, not a fourth counted lesson, implemented app, physical classroom trial or child performance report. Original 2022 Texas Education Agency Prekindergarten Guidelines printed p.76 (PDF index 75): PK3.IX.A.2 “Child engages in movement sequences with adult support” (original research SHA f748de7e4b034e5263511d0773315285b4e3143b8eb855013ccfd54706452426); PK4.IX.A.2 “Child coordinates sequence of movements to perform tasks” (SHA fc198498ea4448cb9c6c34e9dea81b73b469dd42edea5780b9fc877f0ca73cb5). Published examples include nonlocomotor axial reaching, turning, bending and twisting: source does not require grip, walking, any exact move–turn–deposit choreography, actual written mail, a perfect placement or wholly novel task for every modest source-action candidate.

Distinct real-movement invitation

A harmless large blank pretend message has a broad friendly safe pretend box. The child can explore where their own body needs to go or face to make the delivery happen. Preferred actual paper-delivery route: child physically approaches or repositions in safe accessible space, reorients toward a broad stable open box and contributes real body motion to bring a large intact paper note toward/into it. Source-valid task-related alternative: if grasp/release/paper texture is inaccessible, invite the learner to move their own body to locate, reach/reorient and indicate the broad pretend message destination; a trusted adult can place the paper on their own behalf, but that is NOT child physical note delivery. Record the pupil's actual linked body actions and meaningful PK4 task orientation separately. A spoken instruction, video/avatar delivery or teacher entirely puppeteering limbs can never replace genuine child physical sequence.

For PK3, adult support may be age-appropriate responsive supervision, safe access provision, reassuring availability and step cue when helpful; no compulsory verbal instruction at each movement if the child initiates an authentic sequence naturally. For PK4, child coordinates actual movements toward a purposeful task, even if the paper slips or final box placement is incomplete due separate finger-grip limitation; adult should not dictate every limb transition. A real purposeful linked attempt is not identical to successfully moving paper, and neither is clinical evidence of motor mastery.

Preflight, consent, materials and child protection

Prepare one large broad intact lightweight paper note, blank or with purely fictional harmless symbols, no real names, home address, photos or sensitive messages. No sharp staples, paper clips, metal fasteners, small detachable beads or rigid folded sharp points. Prepare a wide open stable low reachable pretend box or equally broad resting/indicating surface, secured against tipping, with no narrow slot, pinch edge or small loose hardware. Use clear uncluttered flat floor and comfortable accessible seated/wheelchair turning space; check safe distances, stable height, visual/tactile target, hygiene and sufficient real adult supervision. No public stranger delivery, mailboxes outside, streets, running, climbing, unsafe reach, compelled paper touch, exact fine-grip performance, competition or public motor labels. Learner can pause or decline; if safe physical chance unavailable mark NOT_OBSERVED rather than inability. Keep actual student identities/mobility details only in appropriate private teacher records, never shared curriculum Git.

Five complete phase-specific tasks

ENTRY — child's original real physical decision. Present large harmless note and broad accessible box separately. Ask, “Can your body help this pretend message find its resting spot? Show me a comfortable way.” Give child a real physical action before an adult model: child may safely reach/reorient, position body near box then deliver, or physically reach/turn/indicate box while helper handles paper if grip inaccessible. Observe who initiated/controlled the child's bodily actions, adult support and who actually moved paper. Saying where box is or tapping a digital arrow while motionless is not source.

MODEL — different ADULT-only movement goal. A trusted adult at an ADULT-safe broad stable chair demonstrates real purposeful body transition toward becoming comfortably seated: approach if appropriate, gently lower into stable seat, adjust back/shoulders into comfortable position. It is not the learner's paper delivery, not a teacher performing the child's new story-spot EXIT, and is not mandatory pupil sitting. The adult bodily sequence is a worked movement concept, NEVER child-source evidence or permission to move learner limbs.

DURING — authentic child-directed goal and contingent support. Child chooses a safe route, orientation or comfortable seated movement to bring the fictional note toward the broad box if possible. PK3 adult stays safely responsive, may invite linked movement with warm word/gesture cues when needed and logs actual support; adult can stabilize/open the box while child does real bodily actions. PK4 child personally coordinates real movement toward delivery goal—such as move/shift toward the box, turn/reorient toward opening and reach or lower safely to deliver—without adult choosing each movement. If child cannot hold paper, child may bodily shift, turn and physically indicate broad target, while helper may deposit note with authorship adult and child-motor sequence child; never misstate as child carried paper. A paper dropping due incidental grip need not nullify otherwise meaningful PK4 task-directed bodily coordination. A single isolated movement or cartoon route is still not full physical sequence.

Feedback and repair. If learner only describes or points to a picture route, provide another safe real task opportunity. If only one isolated reach, gently ask if they want to turn/reorient toward box or another comfortable bodily action; adult models a separate stable chair task, not pupil's exact target solution. If adult performed all paper transport and pupil did no physical motion, mark no source, do not force praise; offer safe body-controlled alternative. If a pupil responds to paper touch with distress, use big no-touch marker destination and real body movement instead. If pupil genuinely coordinates body toward broad box but drops paper, record actual bodily actions, task goal and failed endpoint separately, not a global motor failure. Distinguish U original body action, H adult gives exact movement sequence, A neutral accessible setup/communication, X ambiguous provenance.

EXIT — genuinely changed non-delivery body goal. Remove paper carrying and original box entirely. In a new safe clear arrangement, put a large neutral story-spot marker within comfortable reach or wheelchair movement range. Ask, “Can your body find the new story spot and show you're ready?” Child physically adjusts position and orientation then reaches/gestures toward target or settles facing it, with no letter, paper or object to deposit. For PK3 adult remains responsively present and gives a cue if needed; PK4 child meaningfully coordinates body movement to reach/indicate/face story spot. This is a different purposeful physical task from the adult seated posture MODEL and original paper delivery; only an ACTUALLY observed attempt can support stronger novel lesson generalization. A familiar true body delivery attempt can independently be a cautious publisher source-action candidate without being labeled novel transfer.

REVISIT — only another actual calendar date. Invite safe different real story/marker task and observe actual first child body movements under PK3 adult support or PK4 personally coordinated goal; note support, conditions, source and date. No actual later child encounter has happened. REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

Age-specific staged extension and legitimate alternatives

PK3 Q3-1..Q3-4: Start with comfortable two linked body actions under responsive safe adult availability, progress through possible real delivery with big accessible note, and then another purpose when ready. A scripted verbal command after every move, flawless page deposited, walking or three exact moves are not source mandates; the child truly moves own body. PK4 Q4-1..Q4-5: Coordinate short child-selected task-directed body links, with optional real paper delivery, changed story-spot physical coordination and later actual transfer; full adult-body manipulation, button taps, fluent speech or requiring a neat final paper in the box are not equivalents. The source construct is actual bodily movement serving purpose, not mail handling or fine finger force.

Attention: Offer one safe obvious reachable box or story spot, pause between actions without stopwatch. Language: Home language, sign or meaningful child-selected AAC communicate route/choice, but real bodily action is separately observed. Sensory: Preview paper and box; no forced touch, sounds, bright flashes or public audience. Mobility/vision/hearing: Stable reachable broad low open box, clear access for mobility aids, tangible large target marker/neutral descriptions, safe seated/axial turns, neutral adult steadying, never claim adult-moved limbs as pupil. Cultural and social choice: Fictional harmless blank notes avoid assuming a particular home address, postal practice or stranger greetings; learner can choose neutral task meaning. Physical safety: No narrow slot/choking pieces, sharp fasteners, unsecured box, busy station routes, running or forced reaching.

Four complete prospective delivery routes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future narrated device may give a child choices about a REAL supervised physical note task but cannot verify actual motor performance from avatar, on-screen icons or Next taps, and cannot make a child's room safe without a responsible adult. CO-PLAY: A trusted caregiver checks actual space, offers authentic entry first, provides PK3 responsive support/PK4 neutral safety and observes first real child bodily sequence. ADULT-LED: Distinct adult safe stable-chair model, child physically practices the preferred paper task or source-faithful no-touch alternative, then a truly different new nontransport story-spot EXIT. CLASSROOM: One safely supervised moving learner at a time in a clear bay, all other children in genuinely supervised calm stationary activities under real existing staffing; if unavailable, defer and rotate across separately staffed sessions. Record only individually witnessed first bodily actions (perhaps 2–3 focus pupils per feasible observation period), never infer 24 from group demonstration or projected cartoon. No public scoring, peer video, timed race or fabricated staffing.

Teacher note: Grade and official source, task/context, child's actually observed physical movement sequence, child/adult paper handling, adult support/cues and U/H/A/X, actual task endpoint, familiar vs truly changed task, safe alternative used and honest later date. Document task action without diagnosising motor limitations.

Limitations: This full guide is written design only. No actual child delivery/alternate movements, field room/staff supervision, external teacher reliability, physical first-party print QA, later retention, functioning SOLO or measured four-mode effectiveness has occurred.
