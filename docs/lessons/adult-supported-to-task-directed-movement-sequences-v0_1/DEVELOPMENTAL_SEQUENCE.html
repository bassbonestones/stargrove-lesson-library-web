Target: PK3 physically ENGAGES in actual ordered movement sequence with real adult SUPPORT, no strict independent task completion quota. PK4 genuinely COORDINATES linked real physical MOVEMENTS to PERFORM TASKS, not merely correctly ordering picture cards; supportive communication allowed, adult manipulation isn't child coordination. Distinct from IX.A.1 single locomotor skill mastery/balance and IX.B.1/B.2 hand motor tasks.

Three substantively distinct original real safe movement SEQUENCE tasks

A — choose a friendly step–turn–wave sequence. On a clear flat indoor floor with space checked by trusted adult, child physically completes a gentle meaningful sequence: STEP or mobility-equivalent movement toward broad safe original marker, TURN toward an imaginary friend/person, then WAVE/gesture a greeting, with adult prompt/model support PK3. PK4 coordinates their actual sequence to complete purposeful greeting task, not simply repeat isolated motions out of context or have adult move their body. For learners with mobility differences, age-appropriate seated/upper-body equivalent ordered meaningful sequence maintains accessible coordination without forcing steps, turning speed or balance stunts.

B — movement sequence to place a large soft cloth in a safe resting spot. Original broad flat-floored 'resting spot' mat marked with simple first-party icon, adult-prepared large intact soft cloth, and child-chosen simple sequence: approach along short clear safe flat route, pick up/secure cloth with age-appropriate adaptive support, then turn toward and PLACE it on a low stable reachable surface. PK3 child ENGAGES in sequence WITH ADULT SUPPORT (neutral verbal/model gestures for ordering as needed), not all task executed by adult. PK4 genuinely COORDINATES ordered moves to achieve real cloth-delivery/placement purpose without requiring no communication or perfect motor smoothness. Distinct physical hand-held object relocation vs greeting gesture.

C — original safe pretend paper message delivery to indoor pretend box. Adult prepares one large harmless blank/fictional note without personal information, clips or sharp points, and a broad low stable tip-resistant open box in safe clear accessible space. Preferred: learner personally repositions, reorients and, where comfortable, moves large note toward broad box. If grip/touch is inaccessible, learner can actually physically shift, turn/reorient and reach/indicate the reachable safe box goal; adult paper transport must be credited to ADULT, not mistaken for pupil delivery. PK3 child engages in linked own movements with responsive adult support and cues as needed. PK4 child personally coordinates purposeful body movements in service of chosen real goal; final note deposit is not a universal test. No compulsory walking, forced grip, outdoor delivery, racing, fabricated child score or mandatory exact three teacher-chosen moves.

The printed outcomes are gross-motor/whole body MOVEMENT sequences, not mental picture ordering, arrow dragging, robotic narration or isolated skill mastery IX.A.1. Safe trusted adult sets clear flat space and remains appropriately nearby; no unsafe obstacle or speed demands. Adapt movement for disability, vision/hearing, sensory comfort, language and autonomy without implying a clinical diagnosis.

MODEL: Adult demonstrates a DIFFERENT adult-only purpose: while seated securely, gently turn/reach toward a large nearby teacher picture to inspect it, then settle back; unlike greeting or new paper-delivery EXIT, no adult action is credited as child's action. DURING: Child physically ENGAGES in linked STEP-and-WAVE or equivalent accessible movements WITH ADULT SUPPORT in sequencing, even with some prompts and incomplete smoothness, not just verbally names actions. EXIT: New original large safe paper message delivery movement with real child participating in adult-supported sequence. PK3 Q3-2 — Engage in actual step–turn–wave with supported ordering

ENTRY: Original first-party big greeting marker, safe flat space, child first tries own physical sequence. MODEL: Adult unrelated pretend paper delivery demonstration away from child body. DURING: Child physically performs ordered move-turn-greet or accessible seated equivalent with adult neutral support, without adult entirely executing it or requiring precise adult-free task completion. EXIT: Fresh actual soft cloth resting-place movement sequence supported and child-directed. REVISIT: True later real supported sequence observation, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 Q3-3 — Engage in adult-supported carrying/placing sequential movement

ENTRY: Real large intact safe soft cloth and safe nearby low resting place, first child actual movement then adult different task model. MODEL: Adult models DIFFERENT greeting movement without manipulating child's cloth/body. DURING: Child engages in moving along clear flat safe path, lifting/carrying appropriate large soft cloth with age-accessible support and placing on reachable resting spot, adult cues ORDER as needed, not adult completed delivery. EXIT: New first-party paper message to stable pretend box with actual child sequence after neutral prompt. REVISIT: Genuinely later other supported real movement chain, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 Q3-4 — Transfer adult-supported movement sequencing across meaningful tasks

ENTRY: Real safe original gesture marker, cloth or large pretend message, actual child first physical entry. MODEL: Adult different story/task example, support without performing child's target. DURING: PK3 ENGAGES in a VARIETY of actual linked physical movement SEQUENCES WITH ADULT SUPPORT, may need gesture/word cues; focus participation and order, not independence or perfect control. EXIT: New safe meaningful adult-cued task with child executes linked real movements, no screen-only sequence. REVISIT: Actually later fresh real physical sequence and supported participation, NOT_OBSERVED.

Five PK4 coordinated goal-directed real movement stages

PK4 Q4-1 — Coordinate simple linked moves to complete meaningful greeting

ENTRY: Child first tries actual flat-floor approach, turn and greeting toward imaginary friend, appropriate safe route. MODEL: Adult demonstrates a distinct adult-only seated reach/turn/bend sequence to look at a broad teacher picture, not the child's greeting or new paper-delivery source. Child can perform appropriate age-safe nonlocomotor sequence without compulsory gait. DURING: Learner COORDINATES actual sequence of self-directed body motions (step/turn/wave or accessible equivalent) to PERFORM task of greeting, not unrelated movements or adult moves learner body. EXIT: Fresh genuine original pretend-message delivery with meaningful coordinated child-action sequence. REVISIT: Real later new child-coordinated task, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-2 — Coordinate movement transitions toward real physical goal

ENTRY: Real original safe nearby flat-floor greeting marker, clear area, child first chooses own movement order. MODEL: Adult separate paper delivery task model, no exact target movements done for child. DURING: Child links sequential changes of body position/orientation and gesture in a functional greeting task with purposeful transitions; verbal encouragement allowed but not adult physically executing the sequence. EXIT: New real large intact cloth resting task, child coordinates approach/carry/placement. REVISIT: Actually later new goal-directed linked movement, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-3 — Coordinate actual transport and placement movement sequence

ENTRY: Safe large soft fabric, low stable reachable destination mat/spot, child first attempts unmodeled real task. MODEL: Adult MODEL on DIFFERENT greeting gesture activity, not child's cloth placement. DURING: PK4 child actually COORDINATES approach, age-appropriate reach/grip and TURN or reorient to PLACE cloth in intended spot to complete real task, rather than just holds cloth passively while adult carries. EXIT: Fresh original pretend paper message box nearby, child physically coordinates movement to deliver. REVISIT: Actual later distinct real movement task, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 Q4-4 — Plan through action and coordinate a safe message delivery sequence

ENTRY: Child first personally tries a meaningful real safe movement in reach of broad low stable pretend box, with intact harmless fictional paper note optional. Observe genuine bodily action and adult paper role before modeling. MODEL: Trusted ADULT safely demonstrates own separate stable adult-chair settling body sequence, never the child's paper-task route or story-spot EXIT and never moves pupil limbs as child source. DURING: PK4 pupil personally coordinates linked purposeful body shifts/reorientation/reaching toward broad pretend-note destination, preferably moving large note when accessible, otherwise a valid real no-grip bodily box-finding/indicating task; adult paper delivery is recorded separately and does not demonstrate child paper carry. Exact grip, fixed gait, flawless slot completion and spoken step obedience are not official source thresholds. EXIT: Remove note, box and transport; introduce genuinely new reachable broad STORY-SPOT marker. Child physically finds/reorients/reaches/indicates ready-for-story goal; actual newly observed child attempt offers stronger novel transfer only, and genuine familiar body movement remains a cautious source-action candidate. REVISIT: Actually later independent calendar day and genuine child's physical purposeful movement if observed; NOT_OBSERVED at written-design stage.

PK4 Q4-5 — Transfer coordinated body movement sequence to novel practical task

ENTRY: Completely new safe adult-prepared original flat space, accessible purpose and safe marker/object, child first attempts self-initiated sequence. MODEL: Adult demonstrates a DIFFERENT harmless movement task, not target full chain. DURING: PK4 genuinely COORDINATES ordered REAL MOVEMENT actions to achieve meaningful task, such as greeting, cloth placement or imaginary paper delivery, with child-owned transitions, not imposed exact speed, running/climbing mastery or adult puppeteering. EXIT: New original actually different practical goal with linked age-appropriate child movements and genuine successful/attempted purposeful task completion, no script replay. REVISIT: Actually later date new real task and coordinated movement source, NOT_OBSERVED.

Four complete substantive WRITTEN delivery modes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: A future child-operated narrated activity can present choice of physically SAFE original greeting, large-cloth resting or fictional paper delivery goal. Trusted adult prepares flat open low space and maintains age-appropriate proximity, then child directs genuine actual physical sequence; PK3 includes accessible prerecorded adult guidance/cues for ordering, which constitutes adult support ONLY if real appropriate adult support exists where needed, not a cartoon replacing required live safety supervision. PK4 child coordinates purposeful ordered movements to perform meaningful task independently within appropriate access support. An on-screen ordering of arrows or animated avatar walking does not demonstrate actual movement sequence. No operating solo system/actual learner performance measured.

CO-PLAY: Trusted caregiver checks clear flat floor and simple large safe props. For PK3 adult offers gentle support for ordering a real child-acted movement sequence (model, verbal/gesture cue), preserving child's physical participation. For PK4 child links actual movement transitions to achieve a real low-risk task, with safety/access support but not adult body-moving actions; caregiver may help set up reachable destination. Avoid speed races or obstacle hazards. Home-language, vision/hearing/quiet, sensory/mobility adaptations, child consent.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Adult observes first authentic learner movement ENTRY before adult MODEL on DIFFERENT safe sequence. PK3 learner truly ENGAGES in linked real movements with adult sequencing support. PK4 learner actually COORDINATES self-directed sequence to perform a task; educator distinguishes child ownership from adult carrying the object. New safe task EXIT and truly later calendar day REVISIT only if physically observed. No normative clinical judgment for mobility or development differences.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Arrange one clear accessible safe supervised movement bay with ONE actively moving learner at a time; others genuinely supervised using stationary age-safe tasks or rotate/defer when staffing cannot safely cover all. PK3 child genuinely engages in supported linked moves, PK4 coordinates meaningful real body sequence toward purposeful goal; witness each child individually. A teacher demonstration or class chorus describing order does not substitute individual real movement. Use native/home-language, vision/motor/hearing/quiet adaptations and inclusive seated movement variants, no real identifying notes, no competitive timing, no climbing/running thresholds.

Source-specific differentiation and evidence limitations

PK3 “engages in movement sequences WITH ADULT SUPPORT” means learner actively physically participates, and adults can cue/order sequence. A child who just watches adults move has not engaged. PK4 “coordinates sequence of movements TO PERFORM TASKS” means purposeful real movement order with child-owned transitions; no invented requirement of total absence of supportive words, elite speed or isolated running/climbing skills (separate IX.A.1). Safe adult physical preparation and age-appropriate oversight regardless of instructional mode.
