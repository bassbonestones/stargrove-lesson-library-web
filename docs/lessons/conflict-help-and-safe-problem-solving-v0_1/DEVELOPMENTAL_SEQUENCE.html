Developmental distinctions and firm safeguarding boundary

PK3 child SEEKS ADULT HELP while actually experiencing conflict WITH ANOTHER CHILD. Their agency may be genuine request in home language, sign/AAC or child-chosen gesture/approach to trusted teacher. No policy may require a three-year-old to resolve arguments alone, confront another child, wait for an unsafe problem to worsen or earn adult care through a particular phrase. A fictional puppet disagreement is a calm educational example only, not actual child source. A conflict between two adults isn't PK3's official child-to-child event.

PK4 child INITIATES PROBLEM‐SOLVING STRATEGIES when experiencing actual genuine naturally occurring conflicts WITH OTHERS and SEEKS ADULT SUPPORT WHEN NECESSARY. A safe learner-authored strategy may be proposing an available alternative, asking for space, clarifying preferences, requesting a fair adult-aided choice, or choosing a safe break; adults remain readily available and must intervene promptly when harm or bullying risk occurs. Do not impose unsupported zero adult assistance, fixed mediation scripts, compelled compromise, peer apology or requirement to achieve consensus. If conflict is genuinely unsafe, educator acts FIRST to restore safety, not tests child independence; such event is not a scored curriculum opportunity.

Never provoke conflict to meet observation source: no taking a preferred toy, artificially rationing materials, staging peer rejection/exclusion, forcing bodily contact or eye contact, creating frightening scenarios, making a peer threaten, exposing privacy, denying access/comfort/food/water/toilet/rest, rewarding child compliance with an adult-created problem, forced turns or class anger rankings. Teacher may teach a fictional first-party gentle story during a comfortable lesson, but child performance with an icon/puppet is teaching preparation, NOT real source. If no actual naturally arising safe disagreement occurs, mark NOT_OBSERVED, not 'student failed' or pretend source success. Actual peer safety and trauma-informed care supersede curriculum observation. Preserve culturally responsive home language, sign/AAC, safe quiet sensory access, adapted motor choice and individualized IFSP/IEP/504.

Teaching sequence: ENTRY checks a comfortable help route; real source is observed only if naturally available, never required before teaching. A single adult can narrate an explicitly fictional MODEL. DURING teach safe strategies calmly with optional true real support only if naturally needed. EXIT source action may arise in familiar or changed ACTUAL naturally occurring manageable peer/person disagreement and child-authored real source response; unavailable must remain NOT_OBSERVED. REVISIT at actually later different calendar date if natural safe event occurs; currently NOT_OBSERVED. No fabricated event, copied teacher script, diagnosis or automatic mastery.

PK3 four responsive stages — real other-child disagreement and adult help

PK3 H3-1 — Find a trusted adult during real naturally arising child disagreement

ENTRY: During normal safe chosen play, if an actual benign disagreement with ANOTHER CHILD naturally occurs, observe first learner's own help request; do not stage it. MODEL: On unrelated calm teacher-owned materials, one adult narrates where adult help is available if anyone feels uncomfortable; no pretending a real child is upset. DURING: Explicitly show learner a nearby trusted responsive adult, home language/sign/AAC/gesture option and unrestricted break/exit; adult will actually respond to a real request. EXIT: In a familiar or changed genuinely naturally occurring mild real other-child conflict, learner seeks actual trusted adult assistance, or source NOT_OBSERVED if nothing occurs. REVISIT: On an actually later date with familiar or changed naturally occurring safe other-child disagreement, document learner-origin help-seeking and actual adult response, currently NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 H3-2 — Ask adult for help with genuine boundary disagreement

ENTRY: At safe ordinary play where another child naturally expresses different location/preference, teacher keeps both children protected; child can seek adult. MODEL: Adult on different own scenario models accepting no and requesting trusted guidance, never demands peer apology. DURING: Teach how child may tell/show trusted adult 'I need space/help' when natural disagreement arises; calm role rehearsal is not source evidence. EXIT: A familiar or new safe child/peer situation if naturally arising, child actively communicates adult-help request and receives care, no independent self-resolution demanded. REVISIT: Genuine later date if real safe conflict occurs with another child, NOT_OBSERVED now.

PK3 H3-3 — Seek appropriate adult co-regulation in actual benign shared-resource issue

ENTRY: Teacher never removes a cherished toy; only if two children naturally want same optional safe shared large item, observe child agency. MODEL: On unrelated safe adult materials, educator demonstrates an adult request for help finding another equivalent option. DURING: Provide age-safe available alternatives, clear child-selected help access and adult-guided fair handling of actual shared supply; do not force a turn or shame sadness. EXIT: A familiar or new naturally arising real child-to-child dispute, learner chooses adult support with actual request and response; without event NOT_OBSERVED. REVISIT: On an actually later date with familiar or changed naturally occurring safe disagreement and responsive adult, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 H3-4 — Optional transfer real help seeking across changed safe peer contexts

ENTRY: Observe learner's real spontaneous help/space communication when minor real conflict emerges in normal voluntary peer activity, not manufactured. MODEL: Adult models on unrelated separate adult-owned event while child comfortable, preserving access to trusted teacher. DURING: Give learner chance to identify trusted adult and child-controlled communication method, practice with fictional story only as rehearsal not authentic evidence. OPTIONAL TRANSFER: Only if a genuinely changed safe real other-child conflict naturally occurs, child meaningfully seeks adult help with any appropriate access and actual adult responds; don't require confrontation. REVISIT: Actually later date and naturally occurring safe child-peer issue, including familiar recurrence; currently NOT_OBSERVED. A changed context supports a separate optional transfer claim.

PK4 five responsive stages — learner begins safe real solutions and seeks help when necessary

PK4 H4-1 — Recognize an authentic conflict and choose a safe opening strategy

ENTRY: During real ordinary safe child/peer interaction, if natural disagreement occurs, note initial child response without forcing conflict or leaving child unsafe. MODEL: Adult on DIFFERENT unrelated adult-owned normal choice models 'I can ask for space/help or propose another safe way', not exact child script. DURING: Teach child to offer an appropriate option or communicate boundary while trusted adult remains available; avoid forced apology or punitive negotiation. EXIT: Familiar or changed genuine naturally arising safe real conflict, child meaningfully initiates context-appropriate noncoercive strategy, not adult-authored line. REVISIT: Actually later date with familiar or changed safe natural disagreement, NOT_OBSERVED until observed.

PK4 H4-2 — Propose safe alternate resource or consenting turn without property pressure

ENTRY: At genuine classroom art center, if peer and child naturally prefer same available optional broad paper color, teacher protects both choices. MODEL: Educator on unrelated adult resources demonstrates how an adult offers another equally usable material with no forced relinquishment. DURING: Child can propose available alternative, different station, respectful optional turn, or choose break; adult helps if needed and ensures safety. EXIT: Familiar or changed actual naturally occurring manageable resource disagreement, learner initiates own safe plan and seeks adult if assistance actually needed, otherwise NOT_OBSERVED. REVISIT: Actually later date with a naturally occurring safe resource disagreement, including familiar materials and peers; NOT_OBSERVED until observed.

PK4 H4-3 — Respond to a genuine space boundary by self-advocacy

ENTRY: If actual safe willing peer play yields naturally different space preferences, learner may communicate own boundary or hear peer's, adult safety first. MODEL: On unrelated consenting ADULT demonstration, speaker says 'I want some room', other respects no without punitive apology. DURING: Invite learner to propose moving own materials, alternative activity, clarifying without coercion or ending interaction; adult available when necessary. EXIT: Familiar or changed real naturally emerging harmless boundary disagreement, child initiates an appropriate action and accesses adult help if needed, never forced eye contact. REVISIT: Actually later date with naturally occurring safe context, including familiar recurrence; NOT_OBSERVED until observed.

PK4 H4-4 — Recognize need for adult support while making own safe problem-solving contribution

ENTRY: In naturally arising real disagreement of manageable intensity, adult monitors child and other person's safety, not leaves them alone for a test. MODEL: Adult demonstrates on other adult-owned project how one can propose alternative and still ask trusted help, no unsupported independence rule. DURING: Child can request necessary adult guidance immediately or try a simple safe option if disagreement persists or feels unsafe; adult intervenes immediately for harm/threats. EXIT: In familiar or changed natural safe conflict, learner initiates a real strategy and if support is needed actually seeks it; neither zero-adult-help nor adult handling everything are sufficient. REVISIT: On truly later date natural safe disagreement, learner source strategy/support recorded, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 H4-5 — Optional transfer child-origin safe problem solving across naturally different contexts

ENTRY: Only if a real benign disagreement emerges during usual safe child class interests, observe learner's spontaneous option or adult support request. MODEL: Educator models unrelated adult-owned alternative strategy and explicitly says unsafe conflict is not an assessment event. DURING: Learner selects and owns context-suited reasonable action: boundary, safe different option, respectful request, break, trusted adult help as needed. OPTIONAL TRANSFER: Only if a changed real manageable disagreement with a real person naturally occurs, child actually initiates safe strategy and appropriately accesses trusted adult if necessary; no compelled reconciliation. REVISIT: Actually later date with familiar or changed real safe conflict if one naturally arises, NOT_OBSERVED until then. Changed context supports a separate optional transfer claim.

Three substantive original instructional mechanisms

01 Actual trusted adult help-seeking in real naturally arising mild other-child disagreement, without forced independence (LESSON_01_ACTUAL_TRUSTED_ADULT_CONFLICT_HELP.md). 02 Genuine optional resource-choice alternatives when harmless disagreement about truly available shared supplies arises spontaneously, without engineered scarcity or forced turn (LESSON_02_REAL_RESOURCE_DISAGREEMENT_SUPPORT.md). 03 Child-origin safe boundaries and alternative positions/plans with real accessible adult help in naturally occurring gentle space disagreement, no forced reconciliation (LESSON_03_BOUNDARY_AND_ALTERNATIVE_RESOLUTION.md). These are distinct safeguards and strategies, not three rehearsals of the same cartoon.

Four serious FULL WRITTEN learning modes — no fake real-conflict source

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future fully child-only independently navigable prereader tablet can tell original age-safe fictional preference differences via narrator, child-controlled nonreading AAC/sign/choice controls, and show safe strategies including reaching a trusted adult, moving to quiet or choosing an alternative. No real adult must be required to operate intended practice. But a virtual puppet disagreement does not mean a child actually experienced a naturally arising real interpersonal conflict or initiated genuine response; official source for I.C.5 remains NOT_OBSERVED absent real occurrence. Software must never engineer real distress, infer child's private emotions via camera, mandate simulated confrontation or reward violence. Functional implementation, privacy, child usability and empirically equal learning value across four modes unverified.

CO-PLAY: Trusted caregiver and child explore benign original first-party story while calm, practice how to find real responsive adult, sign/AAC safety message and choose consensual alternatives. If an actual safe child-to-child disagreement NATURALLY occurs during unrelated normal real family or peer play, learner can seek trusted adult help PK3 or try safe child-origin option with adult help when necessary PK4. Adult protects both child/peer, never stages conflict, withholds toys or forces apologies. In one adult-child session without a real other-child conflict, PK3 official source remains NOT_OBSERVED, yet teaching remains substantial and complete.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Educator begins with safety/assent/access and a simple unrelated original story, observes real ENTRY only if organically arising genuine conflict present, models on different adult-owned materials, teaches meaningful optional help/alternates DURING and uses new first-party story for learning transfer. Do not count adult-acted disagreements as authentic source. A familiar or changed naturally occurring safe real conflict EXIT can only be documented if it actually arises while adult keeps children safe; one-to-one lesson alone does not supply PK3 real conflict with another child. REVISIT genuinely later date only. No child is denied adult help to make a benchmark.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Teacher ensures supportive nonpunitive ordinary class play with genuine willing peers and plentiful accessible materials, never stages scarcity/rejection, prevents bullying, and keeps trusted assistance immediately available. Teacher-led whole-class fictional story and group discussion are legitimate instruction only; each child's source requires individual ACTUAL natural harmless disagreement and genuine child-owned PK3 request or PK4 initiated safe strategy and needed adult support. If none happens, log NOT_OBSERVED, not misconduct. A distressed child receives care immediately and assessment stops. Adults do not compel peer apology or play resumption. Authentic familiar or new EXIT and real later-date REVISIT only when safe actual events occur naturally.

November 13 applied clarification: source action, teaching and transfer

A genuine child-owned help request or safe strategy during a familiar, naturally occurring real conflict can demonstrate the relevant source action. A changed context is an optional transfer observation, not a prerequisite for source-action credit. "Unmodeled" means the adult did not supply the child's decision in that event; it does not prohibit a familiar learned phrase, a previously taught strategy or a stable communication aid. Record immediate answer prompts separately from access support. Later calendar-day evidence supports a separate revisit claim; it is never fabricated to complete this lesson.

The staged lists are flexible teaching examples, not mandatory developmental ranks or a diagnostic ladder. PK3 help-seeking does not require self-resolution. PK4 may initiate a request for necessary adult help as a meaningful problem-solving strategy; do not demand a failed solo attempt first. An unavailable or unresponsive adult is an adult/context failure, not proof the child failed to seek help. Remedy adult access immediately. If the adult must intervene before any child action, record support provided and source action not observed, without a negative child judgment.

One adult can teach this lesson: narrate the original fictional paper story aloud, identify it as pretend, and think aloud: "I could ask a trusted adult for help. The adult listens and helps keep everyone safe." There is no need for a second adult, conflict actor or real peer during calm preparation. Children may rehearse an actual help signal for an ordinary harmless need and receive a real response; this establishes a usable communication route, not real-conflict evidence. Stop rehearsal on refusal, fatigue or unease. A child may choose another trusted adult; never insist that trust is owed to the educator. Follow safeguarding procedures if a child reports feeling unsafe with an adult, and do not promise secrecy.
