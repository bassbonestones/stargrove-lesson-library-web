Family educator overview: composing and decomposing quantities

This overview is uncounted, not a fourth lesson. The current DOT10 repair concerns Lesson 01, two trays and its facilitator companion. Shared source boundaries below apply across the family. Lessons 02 and 03 retain historical plans and require independent reconciliation/review; this repair does not accept their older novelty and multiple-pair gates.

Source objective and evidence boundary

Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines 2022, printed/PDF page 51, PK4.V.A.7: “Child begins to understand that numbers 0-10 can be composed and decomposed in various ways to represent a quantity”. Printed punctuation adds a period. Research-cell SHA-256 f516c6263cd777db194ae66fe60757491e4e68630b8f02cff8125cec7f3d0876. Archived publisher page text compared; no new external certification claimed. PK3.V.A.7 is a NON-OUTCOME research note. Optional younger-child play is not official PK3 mastery.

A child-chosen valid partition with a meaningful whole check can be local evidence. Varied unordered sizes support a broader variety inference, not a mandatory cutoff. Reversed pairs remain mathematically valid; zero has only 0+0 and one only the unordered pair 0+1. Familiar quantities/materials can give genuine local evidence. Record prior teaching rather than calling all familiarity invalid. Transfer, later retention and full 0–10 mastery are separate, unearned claims unless specifically supported.

Three methods, distinct mechanisms

  1. Physical two-tray method: move the original bounded objects into child-chosen parts, rejoin and check. ENTRY starts with two or a comfortable chosen whole, MODEL shows three as 1+2 and 0+3, DURING gives responsive support, EXIT invites a fresh choice without answer hints, REVISIT is a later real date. Never secretly introduce a block; transparent separate contrast teaching is enough.
  2. Frame/slider method, historical plan LESSON_02_FRAME_AND_SLIDER.md: structured occupied cells and moving divider; distinguish capacity from contents. Sibling not accepted here.
  3. Story method, historical plan LESSON_03_NUMBER_STORY_REVERSAL.md: child-authored redistribution and meaning of moving versus arrival/removal. Sibling not accepted here. Read DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md for optional stages. Number order is not an age lock or forced one-day checklist.

Human teaching and observation

Teach 1-on-1 uses one child, two shallow trays, enclosing mat and two initial large safe blocks, with a separate three-block adult model. A class of twelve in pairs uses fourteen trays, seven mats and thirty-three blocks including the model; initially two per child mat, spare pieces controlled by adult. One educator maintains supervision and samples two or three focus children when safe, rotating later. A pair's product or chorus is not each child's evidence. Independent sets and no-story play are valid alternatives. No helper adult, printer, device or worksheet is required.

U: child-origin choice without conceptual help. A: access-only assistance following that choice. H: answer-bearing conceptual support. X: uncertain provenance, independence NOT_ASSESSABLE. No observed response is NOT_OBSERVED. Record actual part pairs, whole check, support and context, not a diagnostic percentage. A later-date REVISIT remains NOT_OBSERVED until it occurs.

Access and humane participation

Start small, repeat calmly and pause when needed. Accept home language, sign/AAC, tactile and high-contrast boundaries, description and precise motor proxy. No literacy, English, eye-contact or speed prerequisite. Use quiet blunt nontoxic objects, not choking pieces or food. Follow actual individualized support plans without diagnosing. Respect refusal and avoid public comparison, trick tests or forced family disclosures. Minimal local notes are enough.

CO-PLAY and ADULT-LED preserve child choices; CLASSROOM preserves safe supervision and individual evidence. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. These written routes are not field-tested equivalents.
