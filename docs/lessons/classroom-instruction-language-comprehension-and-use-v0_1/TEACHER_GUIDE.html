The outcomes describe classroom instructional language broadly, including content/descriptive terms; Method01 focuses on a few useful action words.

Current source rule

A genuine meaningful observation in a familiar real learning context can count. A changed room, new materials, an unheard word, a separate extra test and a later date are NOT prerequisites for current source evidence. Use contextual variation only to clarify a real ambiguity or explore optional transfer. Receptive understanding is not blind obedience: a child may demonstrate meaning while declining. An unexplained grab, fixed motor routine, adult gaze cue, supplied device answer or immediate answer-cued echo is not automatically evidence of meaning. Familiar learned phrases/signs/symbols are valid when meaningfully child-selected.

PK3 requires receptive understanding, not expressive speech. PK4 requires both receptive understanding and meaningful expressive use. Identify the two dimensions and help separately; both may occur in one authentic episode or separate opportunities. A child’s relevant description such as “I checked it” may be expressive use, not only a command to another person. Do not force an extra event after genuine evidence already exists.

Actual classroom language must be used in a real instructional activity: an individual classroom/tutoring opportunity or actual home-learning session can qualify in context. A household game is useful related practice but is not automatically a classroom-language observation. State the actual setting and purpose; do not invent school events or impose obedience.

Teaching and practical materials

For one adult/child, two safe familiar large objects and a stable accessible workspace suffice. Same materials can be shared in turns; separate model or optional contrast pairs and mats are conveniences. No printer, six-object requirement, second adult or purchased device. Check familiar referents, safe edges/no choking fragments, willingness, sensory access and usual communication supports before interpreting ability. Verify uncertain translations. Keep established AAC vocabulary available; do not remove access to manufacture independence.

ENTRY notices real meaning before supplying this answer; MODEL explains a useful contrast on the adult’s own demonstration turn; DURING gives contingent teaching and returns semantic choice; EXIT is a current observation or optional further natural check, not a mandatory new-material test. REVISIT occurs only on a genuinely later actual date, using the same or different routine as useful; until then retention is NOT_OBSERVED. Immediate retry is practice, not delayed evidence.

U = child-selected meaning without semantic answer help; A = access-only support preserving the child’s choice; H = supplied meaning; X = unclear origin. Record actual prompt/context, response and help rather than a label alone. The teacher saying the receptive stimulus word is not automatically answer help; pointing to the semantic answer is. A child-directed motor proxy can preserve meaning, while a helper guessing it cannot. Low-stakes descriptive evidence is not validated mastery.

Methods and scope of this revision

Method01 words and actions, with its facilitator, provides the complete current candidate route: choosing, showing, placing and checking in meaningful learning. Method02 routine detective (not yet in reviewed library) and Method03 child instructions (not yet in reviewed library) are unchanged historical unaccepted drafts. They retain restrictive novelty and, for Method03, two-adult model wording that needs their own repair/review before publication. They are not prerequisites and are NOT qualified by this shared clarification or helper update.

Human routes, access and limits

CO-PLAY follows the child’s useful purpose without inventing classroom evidence from any household request. ADULT-LED one child models a useful contrast and returns choice. CLASSROOM uses the usual safely supervised area, with two objects per participating child or plentiful shared materials, meaningful nonfocal projects and brief focal observations. No public test queue or simultaneous quota. Stop notes for supervision; defer unobserved children and rotate invitations fairly, including quiet/AAC learners. Peers may teach each other; seeing a peer does not automatically invalidate later genuine meaning, but copied answers must not be called independent.

No forced eye contact, unwanted touch, sharing personal objects, rankings, photos, diagnosis or family disclosure. Use ordinary private local notes and setting consent/storage rules. SOLO is NOT IMPLEMENTED; no actual child outcomes, physical/tactile field trial, educator validation, later retention or equal four-mode efficacy is claimed.
