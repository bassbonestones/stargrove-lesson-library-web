02 · Different classroom words do different work

Adult-only meaning comparison; do not present as answer strip at EXIT

CHOOSE

Select one available safe option; a person may choose not to participate.

COMPARE

Look at two safe things to notice an actual similarity or difference.

CHECK

Examine whether the work meets the intended task, not merely move an object.

SHOW

Make some part visible or meaningfully indicate it, with accessible sign/AAC.

PLACE, SHARE, WAIT, TAKE TURNS

These can guide safe optional cooperative classroom activity; consent and sensory/motor access matter. “Share” is not an order to surrender personal items. Physical movement alone isn't proof of word meaning.

These original English teacher examples must be adapted to child's real home language/dialect/sign/AAC. There is no state-mandated exact word list, timing cutoff, English-only grammar, full sentence, forced gaze or compliance requirement.
