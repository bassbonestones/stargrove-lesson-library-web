Original practice 3 versus 5 — the spaced line LOOKS longer

What matters: NUMBER of objects, not the distance between them. A has fewer than B. B has more than A. These are teacher-facing discussion notes; hide correct answer when independently probing.

Original equality trap: 4 versus 4

Spacing differs; counts remain equal. Try moving the same original pieces and predicting whether the relation changes.

PK4 comparison practice, deliberately misleading length

Seven far-apart items occupy a longer row than ten close-packed items. A has fewer than B; B has more than A. Guide learner to match/count rather than choose the longest row.

PK4 equality practice: 8 versus 8
