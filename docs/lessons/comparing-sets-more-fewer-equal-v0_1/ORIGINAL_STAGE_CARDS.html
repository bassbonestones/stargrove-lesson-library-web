01 · Two-set matching and comparison mat

Original first-party teaching resources · source PK3.V.A.8 / PK4.V.A.8 · no child data · practice sheets, not pre-exposed independent EXIT.

Start with only two or three oversized nontoxic pieces per set, never more than five in PK3 or ten in PK4. Learner pairs objects ONE to ONE and indicates the number relationship using words, AAC, sign or specific relation symbols.

Pairing is one valid comparison strategy, not mandatory. At EXIT supply previously unseen set sizes and different object identities; support A is access-only, H is conceptual help.

02 · Reusable source-grade stage ladder

Original first-party teaching resources · source PK3.V.A.8 / PK4.V.A.8 · no child data · practice sheets, not pre-exposed independent EXIT.

PK3 · 0–5 per group

C3-1: 1–3, matched versus extra. C3-2: unequal and inverse “more/less” to five. C3-3: equal quantities in different layouts (optional empty). C3-4: previously unseen novel unequal + equal comparisons.

PK4 · 0–10 per group

C4-1: revisit five. C4-2: 6–8. C4-3: 9–10 plus equals. C4-4: reversed directions/spatial tricks. C4-5: independent small + 6–10 transfer.

A longer line can have fewer objects. Printed illustration above is a *teacher/practice* sample, not new EXIT. Child may match or count, but must actually express relation of TWO bounded sets.
