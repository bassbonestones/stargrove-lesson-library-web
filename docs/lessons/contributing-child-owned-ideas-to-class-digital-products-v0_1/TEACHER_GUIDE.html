Family educator guide: child-owned ideas in digital class products

PK4.X.A.3: “Child uses digital learning applications to contribute to class-made digital products that express own ideas, as appropriate.” Texas Education Agency, 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines, Technology p80. No PK3 outcome for this domain. Official source: https://tea.texas.gov/educators/early-childhood-education/2022-texas-pkg-comprehensive-guide.pdf . The introduction says the Guidelines are not a curriculum, assessment tool or checklist; use descriptive observations for teaching, not child failure, readiness or diagnosis. Examples on p80 include drawing/writing, capture of original creations and co-creating books with adult support. This is a bounded source concordance, not a whole-publication audit.

Objective boundaries

Own ideas need not be novel, different from peers, freehand, verbally explained or unaided. Child-selected stock shapes and familiar images may express intention. Adult save/merge is permissible; do not confuse teacher technical work with teacher authorship of the child's idea. Meaningful child-directed app operation with access assistance is supported participation, not independent motor performance. Teacher-only upload without the child's app participation cannot establish child app use. A real class product may accumulate across days; preserve partial evidence and defer completed group-product confirmation until actual contributions are present. Repeated familiar apps and products remain valid; no new-app, new-theme or new-symbol gate. Paper-only practice is not digital source evidence. Static final files can preserve real contributions; a prefilled mock screen cannot establish them.

Respect assent and actual school permission, no compulsory screens or personal disclosure. This route chooses anonymous local files without child photos/voices and no publication; these are conservative lesson safeguards, not claims that TEA forbids every supervised recording. Use one approved device, adult-controlled saving and verified input access. Private notes belong with the educator, never in public artifacts or Git. An inaccessible tool or declined opportunity is not child failure. Record observed action, exact help, pending save/group status and uncertainty distinctly. No scores or mastery claims.

Select a mechanism

The mural in Lesson 01 adds a visual contribution to a real shared picture. It has an individually paired facilitator and two human website routes under DOT13 review. Lesson 02 (not yet in reviewed library) proposes a shared book page; Lesson 03 (not yet in reviewed library) proposes a shared sound scene. Those two historical plans have not earned DOT13 acceptance and may contain superseded novelty language; do not use their stricter statements to assess Lesson01. A separate full review must reconcile their teaching details before claiming them ready. This overview is not a fourth lesson.

Five phases without rigid hurdles

ENTRY invites a meaningful contribution and observes the actual starting point; help need not wait for a compulsory failed attempt. MODEL can show a tool in the same app on a spare file. DURING lets the child explore, select, direct and revise with access intact. EXIT is an honest description or optional less-cued decision in the same real project, not a compulsory transfer test. REVISIT on a real later date can use the same product; until then REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

Delivery and access

ADULT-LED uses one child with the teacher; CO-PLAY requires a genuine school project connection for full class-product inference; CLASSROOM uses teacher-adjacent focal turns over days while the wider group remains safely supervised. Optional paper work or established activities avoid compulsory queues. Do not promise all children today or infer individual action from class attendance. If supervision is compromised, stop. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: no independent narrated learning application is supplied.

Home language, gesture, sign/AAC, large controls, accessible input or directed adult motor help may preserve intention. Actually verify that the chosen app permits the child to control a meaningful contribution; don't claim paper art and digital input are interchangeable. Printed invitations and blank records are optional adult aids. No physical print, learner effect, independent educator evaluation, classroom trial or operational four-mode parity is established.

Synthetic input contract after independent SR01

The optional evidence.py helper organizes authored examples, not actual observations or a TEA checklist. Every Boolean judgment must be literal True or False; grade/source/support are explicit strings. Missing values, None, strings such as "false", numbers and containers are input errors before any outcome branch, never evidence of a child's inability, absence or consent. Supply support as INDEPENDENT, SUPPORTED, ADULT_ANSWERED or UNKNOWN based on the actual account, not a default. Supply real class context and decline explicitly; missing context is not silently a class project. If context is genuinely unknown, resolve/document it without inventing a score. An explicit home-only context remains related practice, while a genuine first contributor can have own idea/app action recorded with collective-product confirmation pending. Unknown support remains visible even when product confirmation is pending. The contextual facts in helper output are the adult's explicitly supplied record, not facts discovered by the software; child idea/action are None for an unavailable observation. Do not force a software record or assign arbitrary false values to teach this lesson. This shared contract applies to mural/book/audio example records without qualifying the unreviewed siblings; no novelty, unique idea, new app or unsupported motor independence is added as a source requirement.
