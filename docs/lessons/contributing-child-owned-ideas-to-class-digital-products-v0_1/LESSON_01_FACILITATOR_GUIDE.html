Facilitator: a child-owned contribution to our digital mural

Objective PK4.X.A.3: Child uses digital learning applications to contribute to class-made digital products that express own ideas, as appropriate. No PK3 outcome is printed. See the complete lesson for full source boundaries and the family overview for other mechanisms.

Before the child arrives

Use one approved stable device and familiar creation app, private class mural, backup, ordinary art alternative and private teacher note. Test create/undo/save/reopen in a spare file; verify the actual access route and sharing permission. Keep peer work protected. No account creation, public publishing, names, voices or child photos in this route. A class mural can grow across days; own app use is recorded now, group-product confirmation when genuinely present. A home-only product is practice unless part of a real school class project. Budget roughly 5–10 minutes flexibly, not a timed assessment.

ENTRY: offer an authentic invitation

“We are making a picture together. What would you like to add?” Let the child explore before defining an idea in words. Notice a meaningful choice through gesture, sign, AAC, home language or action. Decline, fatigue or inaccessible controls call for a break/alternative, not failure. A first attempt may be supported; record the help instead of pretending an unprompted baseline occurred.

MODEL: explain a control if needed

In the same app on a spare area, show your own brush mark or undo. Explain the cause: moving makes the mark; undo removes the last action. Return choice to the child. Different software and a novel theme are unnecessary. Do not change the child's idea to fit your demonstration.

DURING: notice, respond and return control

A familiar symbol, stock picture, repeat favorite or directed capture of the child's own artwork may express an idea. Let the child select/position/approve the actual app contribution. For motor assistance ask permission and follow the child's precise meaningful directions; do not invent meaning for random touch. Help find a tool without choosing the content. If control is difficult, try a large target, accessible input or shorter turn; if that still fails, offer art and defer digital evidence. Respond to accidental marks with keep/change/undo choices, not correction toward pretty art.

The adult may save and combine contributions. Show the real saved mural and accept the child's accessible confirmation or correction. Save failure does not erase observed app participation; record pending product confirmation. Teacher-only scanning/uploading without the child's app interaction is not the child's technology action. Same chosen image as a peer is not plagiarism or failure.

EXIT: record a small honest claim and stop

If useful, invite an unsupplied choice: “Anything to keep or change?” Same app and mural are fine. Separate supported teaching from an actually observed less-cued decision, not a demanded new product. Record idea/action, actual digital contribution and destination, support, assent/access, save status and remaining uncertainty. U = child-origin decision; A = access assistance under child control; H = adult supplied the specific content/next decision; X = support or ownership unclear. These are descriptions, not a score. X does not mean adult answered; H still describes meaningful teaching. No observation means NOT_OBSERVED. None of these establishes mastery.

REVISIT: a real later date

Return to the same mural or a different appropriate project on another day if worthwhile. Record date and actual action/help. A new app is optional transfer, not required retention. Until observed: REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

CLASSROOM organization

One educator keeps one teacher-adjacent device within normal sight/hearing of the group. Use a settled art/center time; other learners continue meaningful familiar activities or voluntarily plan on paper. Do not run this station if it compromises supervision. Invite one willing focal child; spread turns across days, keep private opportunity records, never promise everyone today. Pause for the wider group, close device safely, resume later. Peer suggestions and finished mural are not individual evidence. Changing peers' work needs permission or a separate area. No forced sharing, public comparison or child supervisor.

Four delivery routes and support

ADULT-LED is the flexible one-child sequence above. CO-PLAY at home requires an actual authorized class contribution to claim this source; otherwise it is related practice. CLASSROOM is the safe rolling-turn organization, not an extra adult or multiple-device requirement. SOLO is NOT IMPLEMENTED; no independently narrated app or observed efficacy is delivered.

Access: short turns/breaks for attention; gesture/sign/home language/AAC for communication; no reading, spelling, quantity or artistic-precision prerequisite; predictable preview and quiet for sensory comfort; one meaningful choice at a time for developmental support; verified actual large-control/switch/directed-motor/visual-description route for physical or sensory access. Preserve child control, not the exact adult interface. Do not infer absence of ideas from a failed tool.

Source checks and limits

TEA 2022 Technology p80 explicitly permits adult-supported co-creation and capture of creations. Novelty, unfamiliar software and independent artistic invention are not printed requirements. Own idea plus actual app contribution to the class product is the boundary. A child's first contribution can await peers without being called failed. Print pages are adult planning/blank recording aids; completed paper is not app evidence. No actual child, independent educator, field accessibility, classroom effect, physical print or retention was validated by authoring this guide.

Publisher use limit

PK4.X.A.3: “Child uses digital learning applications to contribute to class-made digital products that express own ideas, as appropriate.” Texas Education Agency, 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines, Technology p80. No PK3 outcome for this domain. Official source: https://tea.texas.gov/educators/early-childhood-education/2022-texas-pkg-comprehensive-guide.pdf . The introduction says the Guidelines are not a curriculum, assessment tool or checklist; use descriptive observations for teaching, not child failure, readiness or diagnosis. Examples on p80 include drawing/writing, capture of original creations and co-creating books with adult support. This is a bounded source concordance, not a whole-publication audit.
