Class Digital Mural · original teaching resource

Possible PK4 pathways, not a mastery ladder

PK4.X.A.3: “Child uses digital learning applications to contribute to class-made digital products that express own ideas, as appropriate.” Texas Education Agency, 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines, Technology p80. No PK3 outcome for this domain. Official source: https://tea.texas.gov/educators/early-childhood-education/2022-texas-pkg-comprehensive-guide.pdf . The introduction says the Guidelines are not a curriculum, assessment tool or checklist; use descriptive observations for teaching, not child failure, readiness or diagnosis. Examples on p80 include drawing/writing, capture of original creations and co-creating books with adult support. This is a bounded source concordance, not a whole-publication audit.

Own ideas need not be novel, different from peers, freehand, verbally explained or unaided. Child-selected stock shapes and familiar images may express intention. Adult save/merge is permissible; do not confuse teacher technical work with teacher authorship of the child's idea. Meaningful child-directed app operation with access assistance is supported participation, not independent motor performance. Teacher-only upload without the child's app participation cannot establish child app use. A real class product may accumulate across days; preserve partial evidence and defer completed group-product confirmation until actual contributions are present. Repeated familiar apps and products remain valid; no new-app, new-theme or new-symbol gate. Paper-only practice is not digital source evidence. Static final files can preserve real contributions; a prefilled mock screen cannot establish them.

Respect assent and actual school permission, no compulsory screens or personal disclosure. This route chooses anonymous local files without child photos/voices and no publication; these are conservative lesson safeguards, not claims that TEA forbids every supervised recording. Use one approved device, adult-controlled saving and verified input access. Private notes belong with the educator, never in public artifacts or Git. An inaccessible tool or declined opportunity is not child failure. Record observed action, exact help, pending save/group status and uncertainty distinctly. No scores or mastery claims.

PK4 P4-1 — Explore a meaningful app mark

Notice a child choosing or approving a mark in an accessible approved app. Teach controls when needed. This can be a useful precursor even before the class product exists; don't credit the missing class-product part.

PK4 P4-2 — Add to the real class mural

The child contributes through actual app action/direction and the adult confirms where it enters the shared class work. Adult may save and merge; anonymous private attribution distinguishes makers. First contributor's group-product status remains pending until others contribute.

PK4 P4-3 — Choose a book contribution

A picture/scene/caption chosen with adult-supported app input can join an actual class book. This is another mechanism, not an obligatory harder stage or proof of writing skill. Historical Lesson02 awaits its own review.

PK4 P4-4 — Choose a sound contribution

A safe permitted nonidentifying sound can express a child's idea in a class sound product. Volume, input access and actual participation matter. This is optional, not a requirement for a child who prefers visual work. Historical Lesson03 awaits its own review.

PK4 P4-5 — Extend or transfer when meaningful

Returning to a familiar project can build confidence; changing product type may offer transfer. Neither unfamiliar software nor new subject matter is compulsory for the source. Record the actual changed demand and help before claiming transfer.

Responsive phases and modes

ENTRY observes rather than diagnoses; MODEL shows a useful control in the familiar app if needed; DURING returns decisions to the child; EXIT records the real source action and support without a novelty hurdle; REVISIT is an actual later-day opportunity, currently NOT_OBSERVED. A child can enter through any appropriate mechanism rather than complete five quotas.

ADULT-LED is one-child teaching. CO-PLAY can prepare or join an approved real school product, otherwise home work is related practice. CLASSROOM spreads voluntary focal turns across days with one supervising teacher and meaningful familiar activities for others. SOLO is NOT IMPLEMENTED. No observed learning, physical print usability, field-tested accessibility or empirical mode parity is established. Do not use this author-devised sequence as the state's assessment checklist.
