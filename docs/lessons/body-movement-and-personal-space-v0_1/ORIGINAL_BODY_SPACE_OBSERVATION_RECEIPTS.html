01 · Distinguish body from proxy

Original observer reference for safe voluntary personally feasible action

Actual child action: Child personally starts a small hand open, head turn, seated shift or supported arm movement, and voluntarily stops. A support person may help hold a stable position while child meaningfully initiates part of movement; describe what the child initiated.

Not actual child body control: An adult moves child's hand entirely, a puppet walks, a screen avatar follows a tap or a motorized platform drives without observed child body contribution. These can teach planning/access, but must be labeled separately.

No exercise prescription, normative gait, exact “safe personal bubble” size, compulsory walking or unsafe physical challenge. If motor action isn't observed, report NOT_OBSERVED rather than diagnosing ability. Actual individual supports/IEP team must guide classroom use as needed.
