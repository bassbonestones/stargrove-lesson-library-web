Supported compound-word play: conditional choices, not a mastery ladder

The archived original 2022 TEA PDF printed page 33 was checked on 2026-11-13: examples include deleting either part, adding, substituting, and combining gestures. Do not falsely exclude all adding/combining from III.B.4. This family focuses on deletion and substitution; first-word removal is one slice, not mastery of the full outcome. Do not score PK3 children against an invented word-manipulation outcome.

PK4 M4-1 — Remove a first meaningful word

Lesson 01: optional ENTRY with RAINCOAT, adult MODEL BOOKSHELF without BOOK -> SHELF, child DURING RAINCOAT without RAIN -> COAT, optional EXIT on raincoat again or familiar SUNFLOWER without SUN -> FLOWER. Familiar practiced words can show a child-owned action; novelty expands transfer evidence but is not the official requirement. Record answer contamination, uncertainty, and neutral access separately. REVISIT on a genuinely later day remains NOT_OBSERVED until it occurs. Consult the full Lesson 01 and its own facilitator for usable materials, feedback, access and observation procedure.

PK4 M4-2 — Explore last-word removal if useful

When the child understands the first game and wants another, BOOKSHELF without SHELF leaves BOOK. This is a different operation, not a required next gate. Check word meanings, model separately, allow an independent supported response and record what happened. Lesson 02's historical detailed draft is not accepted by the DOT11 Lesson 01 review; its novelty and source-scope language needs separate reconciliation before acceptance.

PK4 M4-3 — Communicate the meaningful remainder

In any deletion turn, speech, sign, usual AAC or a meaningful selection can express the remaining word. This is not an additional demand for an oral explanation sentence. Card movement alone may show instruction-following rather than linguistic manipulation. If ambiguous, record uncertain rather than demand repeated tests. This choice can occur alongside M4-1 or M4-2; numbers are planning labels, not developmental diagnoses.

PK4 M4-4 — Try a meaningful whole-word substitution

A known BOOKSHELF can become BOOKCASE by changing SHELF to CASE, only if those meanings are genuinely familiar. English compounding cannot be assumed to survive literal translation. A knowledgeable speaker can choose a valid home-language example. Lesson 03 has a distinct historical teaching draft; it requires its own substantive examination, including picture meanings and source-scope language, before acceptance. No sibling acceptance is inferred here.

PK4 M4-5 — Invite transfer, not compulsory novelty

A different known word, support format, person or later setting can explore flexible use. Change one demand at a time; unknown vocabulary is not a fair transfer test. Same-word later performance may inform retention, different-word performance may inform transfer; neither means universal mastery. No required correct count or compulsory later-day test is source-supported.

Delivery and evidence

CO-PLAY and ADULT-LED: a caregiver/educator offers a meaningful game, may model first if requested, and preserves child linguistic agency. CLASSROOM: shared modeling is teaching, not individual evidence. One teacher can observe short willing turns within an already supervised routine, keeping all children visible; defer when supervision or access would be compromised. Classroom guides specify amounts and pacing, not imaginary extra staff. SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED: no static page proves a working child-alone tutor or equivalent learning.

Children may watch or stop, use gestures/cards/sign/AAC, familiar compounds, a neutral motor proxy, or additional processing time. Fade supplied answers, never needed access. Declined/deferred/inaccessible opportunity is NOT_OBSERVED, adult answer followed by echo is supported practice, and ambiguous picture choice remains uncertain. A clear supported child-owned response is a limited source-action candidate, not mastery. A private note captures words, prompt, response, supports/help, rehearsal and next choice. No recording or upload is required.

This narrow language activity does not replace broad program experiences, social play, arts, movement or relationships. Families may suggest familiar meaningful words without mandatory homework or purchases. No actual child data, physical print/tactile use, external educator field validation, working SOLO, measured mode parity or delayed retention is claimed.
