Objective-wide reference: supported compound-word play

This is an uncounted family reference. Use the complete Lesson 01 and its own facilitator for the first-word-removal game; the separate Lesson 02 and Lesson 03 historical drafts address last-word removal and substitution and require their own review. DOT11 does not accept those siblings.

PK4.III.B.4: “Child manipulates compound words with pictorial or gestural support.” The archived original TEA 2022 Guidelines printed page 33 was independently checked on 2026-11-13. Its examples include first/second-part deletion and saying the remainder, adding, substituting and combining gestures. Do not narrow the entire outcome to this family's deletion/substitution focus or categorically reject combining as only III.B.3. PK3.III.B.4 explicitly has no outcome; no invented PK3 test or mandatory mastery ladder is authorized.

Lesson 01 uses BOOKSHELF without BOOK -> SHELF as adult MODEL, RAINCOAT without RAIN -> COAT for willing child ENTRY/DURING, and optional EXIT on raincoat again or known SUNFLOWER without SUN -> FLOWER. Word meanings must be familiar, not just named by an adult seconds earlier. The child controls the linguistic change; movement can be by a neutral child-directed proxy. Picture/gesture supports are part of the source, not cheating. An independent response on a rehearsed word is possible, but record rehearsal and possible echo. Novelty is optional transfer evidence, not a blanket pass gate. REVISIT is NOT_OBSERVED until an actual later opportunity occurs.

Source-action candidates, supported practice, uncertain responses, not-observed opportunities and observed-not-yet-shown responses are different. Record exact prompt and child response with language, support and adult/peer help privately. Do not infer competence from chorus, card movement alone, printed answer reading, or a supplied remainder. Do not reject a clear response because it uses signs, AAC, accent, processing time or familiar words. Do not force a test, sentence explanation or unsupported unaided performance. Stop kindly when requested.

Prepare large safe meaning pictures or accessible gestures, keep adult answer equations away, check the child's usual response system, and choose culturally familiar genuine compounds with knowledgeable language support. Printed words are adult labels, not reading targets. CLASSROOM independent turns must fit safe supervision of the whole class; defer rather than invent extra staff or score a chorus. CO-PLAY/ADULT-LED are human teaching routes; SOLO is NOT IMPLEMENTED. See DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md for conditional next choices, not a compulsory five-stage sequence.

Synthetic helper input contract (DOT11 repair)

evidence.py is optional synthetic documentation, not a teaching prerequisite or an assessment system. Every Boolean field requires a literal Python True or False, including explicit decline/access and uncertainty-resolution judgments. Strings, numbers, containers, missing values and None are invalid inputs; evaluate raises TypeError before any inference. An input error means repair the adult record, never mark the child wrong or absent. Do not convert unknowns to False mechanically. If an accessible response was observed but alternative explanations remain, explicitly use alternative_explanations_resolved=False for UNCERTAIN. If no interpretable opportunity occurred, explicitly record that fact; no required software form should obstruct responsive teaching. genuine_new_unmodeled_compound=False preserves legitimate familiar-word source evidence, while distinguishing transfer metadata. Neutral AAC/motor access does not become adult-owned answering. This shared contract applies to the deletion/substitution examples of Lessons 01–03 but does not qualify siblings or the full official B4 outcome. All observations and type choices remain fallible adult judgments.
