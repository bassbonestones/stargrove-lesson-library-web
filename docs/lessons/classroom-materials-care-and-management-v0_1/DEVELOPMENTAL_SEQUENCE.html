Source boundary and use of this sequence

This is an author-designed menu of teaching starting points, not an official stage sequence, diagnostic scale or mandatory ladder. Choose the smallest useful step for the child. Familiar materials and previously taught routines are valid. Novelty, fixed speed, a fixed object count, pre-model success, verbal explanation and zero adult help are not requirements of either printed outcome. PK3 meaningfully participates in actual care AND management with adult assistance. PK4 meaningfully contributes to actual care AND management with appropriate support documented. Adult takeover is not child action; speech about a picture is not actual care. A child-confirmed, neutral access proxy can realize the child's management choice without pretending the child physically lifted the item. Home practice is useful but is not literal observation in classroom context.

Observation cycle shared by these starting points

ENTRY: invite a wanted activity with a familiar intact book or mat and reachable actual home. Child may watch, ask, decline or begin. A cold pretest is not required. MODEL: if useful, adult demonstrates on a separate item, explaining the real payoff: the material remains usable for next time. No adult colleague is required. DURING: give contingent guidance, preserve access supports, record what the adult and child each actually do. EXIT: at the natural ending, observe gentle handling and appropriate placement separately; the same item and location are valid. Transfer with different materials is optional separate evidence. REVISIT: a genuinely later occasion can use familiar materials; do not fabricate a date or infer retention without observation. Actual child and revisit data are NOT_OBSERVED. Support tags U (no prompt in recorded step), H (instructional assistance), A (child-directed access assistance), X (unclear provenance) describe the record, not automatic success or failure. H is compatible with meaningful PK3 action; X stays uncertain. Not offered, declined, inaccessible and unobserved opportunities cannot be converted to deficits. Care without management or management without care is partial evidence. The synthetic evidence.py helper only checks a hypothetical record; it does not certify a child.

PK3 starting points, adult assistance retained

PK3 T3-1 — Gentle use

Use the wanted intact broad book or mat with help. Adult may stabilize the item while the child turns or directs a page. Record the child contribution; watching adult use alone is not actual care. ENTRY offers choice; MODEL is available before success; DURING adjusts help; EXIT records actual contribution; REVISIT awaits a later observation.

PK3 T3-2 — Accessible return

Help identify the real reachable home and support the child placing or intentionally directing placement of the used item. Combine this with observed care before claiming both verbs. ENTRY offers choice; MODEL is available before success; DURING adjusts help; EXIT records actual contribution; REVISIT awaits a later observation.

PK3 T3-3 — Ask for safe help

If an ordinary benign care need naturally occurs, child can ask a trusted adult or select a safe protective location. Do not create damage or a hazard. Adults handle repair. ENTRY offers choice; MODEL is available before success; DURING adjusts help; EXIT records actual contribution; REVISIT awaits a later observation.

PK3 T3-4 — Broaden when useful

Optionally try another appropriate classroom material with continued adult assistance. Transfer is enrichment, not a prerequisite for familiar material care. ENTRY offers choice; MODEL is available before success; DURING adjusts help; EXIT records actual contribution; REVISIT awaits a later observation.

PK4 starting points, appropriate help retained

PK4 T4-1 — Gentle practical use

Child chooses or contributes to a safe handling action during a wanted familiar activity. Do not demand novelty or unsupported dexterity. ENTRY invites participation; MODEL can precede a successful response; DURING preserves needed supports; EXIT captures actual care and management; REVISIT is a later real occasion.

PK4 T4-2 — Useful organization

Child selects a functional place for accessible materials in actual use. No fixed inventory count. Record choices rather than rewarding a memorized slogan. ENTRY invites participation; MODEL can precede a successful response; DURING preserves needed supports; EXIT captures actual care and management; REVISIT is a later real occasion.

PK4 T4-3 — Care and return

At the natural end, observe careful use and return to the real home. Assistance is described, not automatically disqualifying. Adult deciding and doing everything is not the child managing. ENTRY invites participation; MODEL can precede a successful response; DURING preserves needed supports; EXIT captures actual care and management; REVISIT is a later real occasion.

PK4 T4-4 — Protect or ask

If a harmless need naturally appears, child may select safe protection or ask an adult. Never stage a fall, damage, chemical cleanup or unsafe repair to create evidence. ENTRY invites participation; MODEL can precede a successful response; DURING preserves needed supports; EXIT captures actual care and management; REVISIT is a later real occasion.

PK4 T4-5 — Sustain through an activity

Optionally observe selection, use and return across a larger authentic project. A larger workflow is an extension, not a new source minimum or required completion target for Lesson 01. ENTRY invites participation; MODEL can precede a successful response; DURING preserves needed supports; EXIT captures actual care and management; REVISIT is a later real occasion.

Modes and logistics

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: prepare one intact lightweight classroom item, reachable actual storage and optional separate model. Stay within safe reach and stop when tired, distressed or uninterested. CO-PLAY: take turns without turning the adult's work into the child's evidence; at home label the setting honestly as practice. CLASSROOM: embed care in existing supervised centers; one teacher rotates a small focal observation across days while maintaining sightlines and ordinary routines. No queue, mandatory stations, second adult or simultaneous record for every child is assumed. Record afterwards only what was actually seen; supervision takes priority. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: no child-operable autonomous digital route, reliable sensing or independent physical supervision is supplied. Printouts are optional adult references, not a self-running lesson.

Safety and access

Use intact lightweight large materials appropriate to the actual learner and setting, with owner consent. No sharp, hot, electrical, chemical, heavy, broken or choking-risk items; adult handles repairs, body fluids and disinfecting. Never make material care a punishment or reward requirement for food, belonging or play. Offer stable seated reach, tactile/high-contrast cues, home language, sign/AAC, quiet space and neutral proxy access. The child need not speak, explain why, lift without help or accept touch. Follow existing individual support plans, not an invented diagnosis.

Family mechanisms and scope

Lesson 01: direct gentle use and return of one real item. Lesson 02: notice an actual benign care need and select protection/adult help. Lesson 03: manage supplies through an actual shared project. DOT07 fully examines only Lesson 01; this shared sequence receives a source-fidelity correction, not acceptance of sibling lessons. Consult the specific plan and facilitator guide.
