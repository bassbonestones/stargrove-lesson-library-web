Exact published source actions and stage distinction

PK3.I.B.1.b — Child takes care of and manages classroom materials with adult assistance. (original research-cell SHA-256 091c0055a4809f93064ca8eda9c0b421fa6a203a2f490dc8c853b3c190d8e811). Preserve: PK3 child genuinely TAKES CARE OF AND MANAGES real CLASSROOM MATERIALS WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE, which the printed source explicitly permits and requires as context. Child must actually meaningfully contribute to careful use/protection AND material management in a real classroom, not puppet tidy icon, teacher doing every physical decision, or unsafe repair work.

PK4.I.B.1.b — Child takes care of and manages classroom materials. (original research-cell SHA-256 ae881a51b89fef58e7441a6ecec9c069ef3701acf2744e0c365811ba97e082a1). Preserve: PK4 child genuinely TAKES CARE OF AND MANAGES real CLASSROOM MATERIALS with meaningful own choices and appropriate safe help available. Neither complete unsupported independence nor fixed number of items is in printed outcome. Caring for materials (safe protection) AND real management (selection, location, storage) must be observed, not just labeling the supplies.

Three distinct complete lesson methods with unique real-world assessment

1. LESSON_01_REAL_CLASSROOM_MATERIAL_CARE_AND_RETURN.md and facilitator companion

Mechanism: Direct practical gentle use and return of a single real durable classroom item, with actual child contribution and age-appropriate assistance rather than a virtual storage-card activity. Source-faithful EXIT: At the natural ending of real use, observe care and return of the familiar classroom item to its actual reachable home. PK3 meaningful adult-assisted action remains eligible; PK4 contribution and support are described without a zero-help rule. Staff doing every meaningful action is not child evidence. Adversarial misconception: Teacher moves all books while learner presses decorative 'tidy' icon, or demands PK3 zero adult help; neither demonstrates child real caring and management. Use an actual safe resource with meaningful child contribution; familiarity is legitimate.

2. LESSON_02_NOTICE_AND_PROTECT.md (not yet in reviewed library) and facilitator companion (not yet in reviewed library)

Mechanism: Real benign care-risk identification and safe protection or appropriate adult-help request, unlike routine return or entire-project resource logistics, explicitly disallowing unsafe repairs. Source-faithful EXIT: A genuine harmless real classroom object with an ordinary naturally occurring non-dangerous care need, familiar or optionally changed: learner actually protects material and manages its placement or selects safe adult help; record all child decisions, not merely announces 'be careful'. Adversarial misconception: A child merely identifies a cracked cartoon while adult repairs a real broken item and assessor scores care-and-management; stop unsafe repair, use a harmless actual real resource and record genuine child safe actions.

3. LESSON_03_SHARED_PROJECT_SUPPLY_KEEPER.md (not yet in reviewed library) and facilitator companion (not yet in reviewed library)

Mechanism: Coordinating usable safe large materials throughout a real consenting shared art/book project, spanning selection, access, protection and storage, rather than one-item handling or a repair observation. Source-faithful EXIT: An authentic shared safe classroom project, familiar or optionally different: child meaningfully selects, cares for, organizes and returns distinct real supplies, PK3 with adult assistance; PK4 with meaningful own management and safe support, with separate care and management observed. Adversarial misconception: Adult sets out and packs away all supplies while child receives 'supply keeper' badge: honorary role without child action cannot prove printed care and management. Real meaningful child contribution is required; a familiar task is valid, with changed context optional.

Seven learning-difference access profiles

Attention/executive: Use one familiar actual durable item and one meaningful safe class cleanup action, with optional later extension; no timed cleanup score. Language/communication: Present accessible true care/management directions and learner-origin ask-for-help via sign/AAC/home language. Literacy/phonological: Storage pictures and large shapes aid access but icon matching cannot replace genuine physical classroom material care. Numeracy: Avoid item count quotas, speed and complicated inventory calculations not in source. Autism/sensory: Neutral dry safe classroom materials, no forced messy/dirty repairs or public shame. Intellectual/developmental: Teacher demonstrates on different real materials and allows adult assistance for PK3, fades answer prompts for PK4 without withholding safety/access. Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Accessible real broad items, low reachable storage, stable equipment and exact learner-directed neutral proxy, not educator doing entire care/management.
