Shared sky-object teaching boundaries — DOT34 causal repair

The exact 2022 Texas PK4.VI.C.2 outcome is “Child identifies, observes, describes, and discusses objects in the sky.” Original PDF page 63 visually checked for DOT34. No PK3 counterpart. Publisher examples include making cloud representations and sharing shape, size or color; questions/comments about Sun, stars, Moon; identifying/commenting on commonly seen sky objects.

A cloud-focused encounter is a bounded opportunity toward this broad outcome, not a simultaneous four-verb or two-object quota. Preserve actual component contributions, supported learning, familiar sources, child-led timing, home language/sign/AAC, and mediated observational access. No compulsory novelty, independence, next-calendar-day timing, Moon availability, comparison or art skill. An independent check, transfer or delayed retention must be separately and honestly described when claimed, not demanded for ordinary learning.

Safety is non-negotiable: no direct solar viewing or optical aids toward Sun, unsafe window/roof/night trip, crowding or unsupervised station. A secure shaded window is safe only if actual viewing direction excludes Sun/glare. Direct view, real photograph, illustration and accessible description are distinct provenance categories; drawings are useful representations but not evidence of direct sightings. Adult supplies source provenance, not a child quiz. Keep identities and observations out of this repository.

Lesson 01 daytime inquiry has the revised complete plan and facilitator guide. Lessons 02 (Moon shape/position) and 03 (stars/planets) remain historical unqualified sibling designs; the boundary notice on each overrides old blanket eligibility gates but does not qualify their full teaching design. No sibling publication or acceptance is implied. Any future sibling review must examine the full artifacts and shared resources afresh.

ENTRY invites participation and current ideas; MODEL can use the same source with help recorded; DURING follows a meaningful feature/question; EXIT collects attributable components rather than all-or-none mastery; REVISIT may be same-day or later, actual timing recorded, REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED in this authored design. CO-PLAY supports reciprocal inquiry; ADULT-LED responds to the individual; CLASSROOM uses one teacher with a single supervised source and deferred individual opportunities. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. No observed learning or digital parity.

Optional pathways, not a readiness ladder

PK4 P4-1 — Cloud noticing

A child may notice one genuine cloud, a clear sky, or an accessible description; acknowledge partial features without a naming test.

PK4 P4-2 — Cloud variation

Explore another cloud or a familiar photo when useful. Comparison and Sun discussed safely through representations are optional enrichments, never solar viewing.

PK4 P4-3 — Moon curiosity

Include a genuine visible daytime Moon or a truthfully identified photo if available. Missing Moon is not failure; no compulsory phase labels or late outing.

PK4 P4-4 — Stars and uncertain identities

Use a credible image or safe actual opportunity. A bright point is not automatically a planet; distinguish observed appearance from established identity without making advanced astronomy a gate.

PK4 P4-5 — Connections over opportunities

Revisit familiar or new sources, representations and questions; note growing breadth without a prescribed number or ordering. Transfer is optional evidence, not a prerequisite.
