02 · Different safe learning routines

Teacher-owned examples of optional kind classroom rules

Art beginning and ending

Choose a wide clean page; keep broad tools in safe shared art area; return them when finished if able/with help.

Quiet reading transition

A story can end kindly; learner can choose a safe place for broad book or ask for support.

Shared work boundary

Ask before moving another person's own project. A partner may decline or request space.

A changed accessible cue

Gentle transition reminder rather than startling alarm or punitive time limit.

These examples are not state-prescribed commands. Do not require speech, sitting still, normative gaze, peer participation, exact speed or ignoring access/support needs. Child may need sensory processing time, an AAC prompt, a break or help.
