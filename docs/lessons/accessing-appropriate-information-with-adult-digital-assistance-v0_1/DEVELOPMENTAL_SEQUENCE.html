PK4.X.A.4 — five developmentally different PK4 stage lesson continuum

Exact published source: “Child uses technology to access appropriate information, with adult assistance.” Full original source research-cell SHA-256 710f09cbf4de290007dbb2d794f8dcd916b5ff1963a48572a944c7d884afaec5. No published PK3 parallel. Source boundary: In a genuine offline curated or district-approved supervised digital reference, the child follows an actual learning question/topic, uses/directions access to appropriate content with a present helpful adult and personally locates or communicates a relevant fact/image/detail from trusted source, keeping separate adult-only safety/source selection. The official phrase WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE is essential. A responsible adult must select and supervise a genuinely appropriate accurate age-safe digital information source and meaningfully support the child's use; source doesn't require an unsupervised internet search or an independently typed query. Child must genuinely USE functioning technology to ACCESS relevant INFORMATION, not just receive an adult answer or view fictional illustrations passed off as sourced facts. This is not simply opening/navigating a game (X.A.1), naming camera/keyboard (X.A.2), producing class artifact (X.A.3) or solely exhibiting safe screen behavior (X.A.5); information must actually be accessed via technology with meaningful adult assistance. No age-unsafe open web, unsolicited disclosure or mandated screen time.

ENTRY: Adult offers a genuine new familiar safe question such as where a plant grows or what part of leaf is visible, learner first indicates what they'd like to inspect before teacher shows answer. MODEL: Teacher on DIFFERENT unrelated digital information page demonstrates safe source choice and reading one visible fact, not learner target item. DURING: Learner meaningfully selects/opens approved reference image or audio page while adult guides age-safe navigation, notices real information detail rather than a generic unrelated picture. EXIT: New teacher-approved factual question and actual vetted digital image, child with adult support accesses and indicates relevant information. EXIT: Fresh true sourced question with new correct information reference, learner accesses relevant detail with documented adult help. Three independent mechanisms, not duplicate activity wrappers

Use an actual adult-curated offline nature reference to find plant information: A real approved supervised child-friendly nature digital reference lets child purposefully access an authentic source-backed image/fact about a harmless plant, with adult verification/assistance and personal noticed detail, unlike sky records or cultural museum objects.

Adult-assisted access to a real child-safe astronomy digital reference: A genuinely reputable school-curated real astronomy image/fact reference provides actual source-backed Moon or star information with adult support, distinct from real plant catalog and protected museum object information; child extracts a real relevant detail rather than merely naming sky emoji.

Find information about a cultural or science object in an adult-managed digital collection: A genuine age-appropriate local/district-approved digital museum or science collection yields trustworthy description of a real shared object, letting child with adult assistance access a specific source fact while exploring culture/science respectfully, unlike plant or astronomy entries.

Four substantive written delivery routes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. Future prereader fully narrated adaptive interface could let learner choose to complete source actions with true supported child-origin digital interaction, but no functional independent application has been authored or tested in this curriculum batch, so no automatic evidence exists. Printed simulated screen is not real use; teacher safety permissions remain essential.

CO-PLAY. Caregiver prechecks real age-appropriate supervised device/app and child needs, captures FIRST unmodeled child attempt and desire, models process on unrelated example, then lets learner actively lead source-critical safe action. Give home language/sign/AAC and motor support if child directs. Clearly separate adult-only tasks from learner actions, keep all identities private, allow off-screen alternatives.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD. Educator opens approved secure controlled device context, offers learner source opportunity before adult target solution, models different sample, follows responsive learner guidance and evaluates a NEW unmodeled true digital source action under consent. Record exact what learner did, why appropriate, assistance and failed/unavailable source outcomes; do not mark child proficient from printed mock card.

CLASSROOM. Staff establish child-safe offline/curated teacher-managed digital stations with individual real turns where developmentally appropriate, no unmonitored network/ad purchases or child account creation, and strong accessibility/offline alternatives. Each learner's actual individually attributable source decision/action is documented privately, never inferred from class artifact, chorus or teacher's operation. NO real child participation has occurred.

Privacy, safety, quality and honest gaps

Adult controls whitelisted/offline vetted child-safe reference, accurate trustworthy educational content, device permission, search queries, account access, ads/links, popups and information privacy; no open web, unsupervised browsing or child's identity entered. Correct uncertainty, never rely on inaccurate copied captions or frightening medical/hazard details, no geolocation collection, no direct solar viewing for space reference. Hearing/vision/AAC, accessible pictures/audio and screen-free alternative if inappropriate.
