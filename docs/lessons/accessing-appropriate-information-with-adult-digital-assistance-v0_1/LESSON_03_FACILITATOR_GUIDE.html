ENTRY — actual first child evidence before adult supplies target

MODEL — different safe adult-owned sample, no child source credit

Attention/executive: Adult preloads one genuine safe approved functioning relevant resource at time, pauses allowed. Use one meaningful child-first opportunity and optional pause; no timed compliance gate.

Language/communication: Child-chosen actual information question and authentic sourced meaning via AAC, sign, home language. Use first-person home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) as appropriate; staff never authors child meaning.
