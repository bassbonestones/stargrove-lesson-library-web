Authentic method target and reasons it differs from the other pathways

Real adult-supervised approved offline/locked child-safe plant digital reference lets learner ask own true plant question, operate actual search/navigation and personally retrieve fact or authentic photograph.

Exact published source contracts and age/ability boundaries

PK4.X.A.4 — “Child uses technology to access appropriate information, with adult assistance.” Full research-cell SHA-256 `710f09cbf4de290007dbb2d794f8dcd916b5ff1963a48572a944c7d884afaec5`.

PK4 child USES TECHNOLOGY to ACCESS APPROPRIATE INFORMATION WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE. Actual approved secure functioning digital tool and genuine vetted source-backed factual content must be accessed by learner in purposeful manner, with real adult assistance permitted. Simulated web screenshot, teacher reading an imagined result, clicking decorative screen or unverified invented fact does not count; no PK3 outcome.

A learner must genuinely experience and use adult-supported technology to access appropriate relevant information, rather than be credited for a paper mock or adult-only unsourced explanation. The published PK4.X.A.4 does NOT universally require independent taps or navigation: authentic learner-experienced informational digital books/videos, virtual field trips and appropriate voice-assisted answers can be legitimate with adult assistance. This lesson chooses the narrower child-guided plant catalog inquiry, where the learner meaningfully selects/directs actual authorized entry and notices a true plant fact. Treat that route as a local learning intention, not a publisher-wide mandatory motor test. Preserve developmental access differences, and do not silently invent a PK3 parallel outcome. Original research-cell hashes are documented above. Publisher-original PDF two-column visual/text concordance is separately PENDING and not falsely certified.

Official standard versus chosen inquiry mechanism: The 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines include genuine adult-assisted use of informative digital books, videos, virtual field trips, and voice-activated information. These may be source-valid without requiring a tap, keyboard or autonomous navigation. This lesson deliberately teaches a narrower child-guided plant catalog question-and-discovery mechanism; assess that local process only where it truly occurs, and separately recognize other authentic standard-aligned experiences. A passive irrelevant screen or unsourced teacher memory is not valid technology-mediated information learning. Do not turn missing catalog navigation into a diagnosis of a child's technology learning ability.

Set up safe original teaching resources and adult safeguarding

Adult preapproves genuine functioning age-safe offline digital plant library or allowlisted trustworthy resource, tests accessibility and data accuracy, turns off unrelated network/navigation and child tracking; original safe real plant photo/reference and adult present.

Before real instruction, check that access needs, sensory comfort, safe materials, genuine human recipient when demanded, and child assent are respected. Record adapted sources accurately. If physical source or safe consenting participants are not available, keep relevant observation NOT_OBSERVED; never stage danger, scarcity, humiliation or compel participation.

ENTRY — actual first child evidence before adult supplies target

Before adult selects plant page, child notices real safe plant item and asks or chooses actual relevant question, then personally operates approved real digital catalog search/page to access source information with adult guidance.

Record independent first response verbatim/descriptively, the physical/person/source context, the learner's own control, genuine prior instruction or prompt that might contaminate the response, and whether any component is absent. If no real source and first child response, do not convert adult simulation into a learner score.

MODEL — different safe adult-owned sample, no child source credit

Adult demonstrates genuine safe Moon/sky information with the same approved functioning general catalog if that catalog provides a separately verified sky entry, or an independently approved alternate source if already available. For plant-only catalogs, an unrelated trusted plant record with a different question/property also works, provided the model never reveals the child's plant target or EXIT information. A second app/device and a particular topic are not prerequisites for this selected lesson.

The adult explains WHY that different example works, including an authentic mechanism rather than response imitation. Adult model never uses child planned new EXIT object/material or identifies answer before child independently responds.

DURING — differentiated practice and useful corrective feedback

Assist child to locate actual source metadata, open true relevant plant text/image and extract factual leaf/plant detail; child determines question and points/signs/words own observed fact, adult verifies authenticity without writing response.

Wait for a child-initiated real response, acknowledge partial correct meaning, correct the specific misconception below on unrelated adult-owned object/context, ask a new open question and fade answer-bearing scaffolds for a genuinely changed independent EXIT. Recording codes: U = child-origin meaningful choice/action, H = adult provided semantics/next step, A = neutral child-directed sensory, motor, or communication access, X = uncertain provenance. H is meaningful teaching but not an unprompted source-performance score.

EXIT — distinct uncoached transfer, with adversarial test

Different truly functioning vetted digital information entry about another safe actual plant: learner with adult support uses real technology to ACCESS source-backed appropriate answer to own/new relevant question and communicates sourced detail, not screenshot pretend search.

Do not reuse ENTRY item or adult MODEL. For every official source ID record actual grade-specific printed verb(s), original meaningful evidence, what it DOES and DOES NOT prove, setup, accommodation, real participant/provenance and any absent essential source. A score from this curriculum is not developmental mastery or a validated educational effect.

What goes wrong and source-preserving specific repair

Teacher holds a drawing of a search engine result and says 'tap the flower' then grades technology access; no real functioning resource was used. Only actual approved live/offline digital source and learner operation can count.

Re-teach on completely different harmless adult source. Do not announce child's target solution; retest only with new meaningful source. If valid source absent, result remains NOT_OBSERVED rather than fabricated mastery.

REVISIT — strictly genuinely different calendar day

Try on another real date with a materially new safe prompt and independently chosen child response; note original outcome ID, optional alternative access and prompt history. This authored material has no real field sessions: REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

Seven individualized learning-difference decisions for this exact method

Attention/executive: Adult preloads one genuine safe approved functioning relevant resource at time, pauses allowed. Use one meaningful child-first opportunity and optional pause; no timed compliance gate.

Language/communication: Child-chosen actual information question and authentic sourced meaning via AAC, sign, home language. Use first-person home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) as appropriate; staff never authors child meaning.

Literacy/phonological: Neutral reading of genuine verified digital source allowed, but learner must operate actual approved technology to access information. Allow neutral text support except where real original print or spoken sound is itself the construct; do not substitute image-only or adult-read content when source requires independently observed print meaning.

Numeracy: No search quantity, tap speed or page-count mastery invented. No invented quantity, score, time, precision or literacy prerequisite beyond what is actually printed.

Autism/sensory: No ads, surprise video/links or busy network search, local closed vetted environment. Respect autonomy and an accessible quiet route rather than compulsory gaze, touch, speed or emotional performance.

Intellectual/developmental: Adults may assist actual access explicitly by source but must not completely operate and fabricate child choice. Supported DURING can use short steps; new EXIT must still demonstrate actual child-origin meaning with no answer-bearing prompts.

Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Actually accessible working device, large controls/switch/voice option and child-directed assistance; if app unavailable mark source NOT_OBSERVED. Neutral proxy may move a safe object chosen/controlled by child, not choose target or comparative relation. Verify required original sensory/physical source truly remains accessible.

Four written delivery modes — no functional or field equivalence asserted

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: eventually narrated child-controlled digital opportunities might rehearse safe reasoning; real physical comparisons and social actions cannot be assumed from a simulated click. CO-PLAY: trusted caregiver sets up a genuinely age-safe source, does not supply child's assessed words, choices or physically performed action. ADULT-LED: educator owns different MODEL; child chooses DURING supports and performs fresh novel EXIT. CLASSROOM: With one authorized working device and one adult, use a short supervised reference-inquiry station for two or three focus children per center period, each with genuine child-selected/directed reference access and observed actual information meaning; other children have established safe independent non-screen opportunities. Arrange real later small-group turns for everyone else, and record unknown/not offered rather than full class achievement. Additional simultaneous device stations require genuinely available safe adults and access; no imposed universal amount of screen time or false adult-count assumption. A shared informative digital book/video could support the broader official standard when genuinely child-experienced with an adult, but is not automatic evidence of this lesson's richer selected catalog-choice method. Record privacy, adult help and source trace for those actually observed, not collective chant or teacher-only navigation.
