People and families: source and route guide

For the revised evidence portrait activity, start with Lesson 01 facilitator guide, then its technical plan. Both materials and complete example accounts are on those pages. No additional family document must be decoded to teach it.

The first objective, PK4.VII.A.1, concerns similarities and differences among self, classmates and other people through specific characteristics and cultural influences. PK4.VII.A.2 separately concerns family characteristics. Original 2022 Texas publisher pages 65–66 expressly show no PK3 outcome for this domain. Exact quotations and research-cell hashes appear in DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md; sources do not impose novelty, independent-only first attempts, one-turn completion or a calendar-day wait.

ENTRY invites an available child idea; MODEL gives useful instruction, not child credit; DURING preserves supported learning; EXIT records warranted thinking with actual help; REVISIT happens when meaningful and records actual timing. Familiar responses, access-only supports and partial evidence remain valuable. U child-origin, A access-only, H conceptual help and X unclear origin describe observations, not children. No field is a mastery threshold. A declined, absent or unsafe opportunity is NOT_OBSERVED, not failure.

A benign voluntary visible characteristic can be compared without implying culture or ability. Never force body inspection or household disclosure. A factual cultural practice must be known or narrated, not guessed from names/appearance. Fiction supports other-person and family learning but is never an actual classmate. Missing classmate information limits inference, not participation. A1 and A2 remain separate; families are not ranked or confined to a household arrangement.

For one adult/one child, narrate two accounts and offer a blank canvas or no-paper response. For one teacher/class, keep familiar drawing/story activity in view, collect focal observations only when safe and rotate opportunities across sessions. A class poster or chorus cannot stand for each child's comparison. Stop for distress, access or supervision; no second adult or staged new peer encounter is required. Home language, AAC, sign, descriptive narration and child-directed scribing preserve the comparison; note conceptual help honestly.

Original story sheets offer narrated fiction; blank canvas/record is optional. Neither is a covert identity survey. No device/print purchase is required. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED; ADULT-LED and CO-PLAY are human routes, CLASSROOM is one-teacher design. No actual effectiveness or observed retention claim.

Both person boxes on the optional canvas accept a known real person or a clearly labeled fictional character. Note whether each detail was freely shared, neutrally noticed without inspection, or stated in a story. Record self, genuine classmate and other-person coverage separately; using fiction never fills missing genuine classmate evidence.
