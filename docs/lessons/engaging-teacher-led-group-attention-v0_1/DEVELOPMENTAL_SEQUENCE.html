Developmental sequence: engaging teacher-led group attention

The original Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines 2022, PDF page 14, prints: - PK3.I.B.3.b: “Child remains focused on engaging, teacher-led group activities for up to 10–15 minutes at a time.” Research-cell SHA d631816fb3c5ef9a72b7f3dcaced28f1becec6e942cb92e301ee0fa1eba83595. - PK4.I.B.3.b: “Child remains focused on engaging, teacher-led group activities for up to 20 minutes.” Research-cell SHA 2aae05ae8f43d2cf942a4d74139075e589d88c70f239eef26d96f2f05ffb225b.

Original PDF outcome and adjacent illustrative child behaviors were read directly in layout extraction on November 13, 2026. The examples include growing stamina at story time and attentive circle/small-group participation. Eye contact and raised hands are examples, not universal requirements. Neither novel objects, an unrelated staff demonstration, a correct verbal answer nor compulsory elapsed-time thresholds appear in the outcome. Shared instructional rules

ENTRY means invite meaningful participation and notice access. MODEL is an optional brief teacher example, possible with one educator, using familiar or different content. DURING means responsive practice. EXIT means a bounded account of actual engagement, not a compulsory new scenario. REVISIT occurs later; familiar content can show repeated engagement, different content may explore transfer. REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED until real evidence exists. No answer, unmodeled content or separate adult actor is required by the attention outcome.

One educator keeps the group supervised. Quiet/movement/break options remain within supervision or the established supported transition plan. Pause teaching when a child's care prevents safe oversight. Actual group means multiple participating learners, not puppets or simulated classmates. Adult-child dyads teach related engagement but do not establish group evidence.

Responsive PK3 pathways

  • PK3 T3-1: join a brief accessible teacher-led group invitation.
  • PK3 T3-2: stay involved in a meaningful exchange with available support.
  • PK3 T3-3: use chosen communication, sensory or movement access to keep following content.
  • PK3 T3-4: grow involvement across genuine familiar or varied opportunities.

Responsive PK4 pathways

  • PK4 T4-1: join meaningful shared teacher-led content.
  • PK4 T4-2: follow an unfolding exchange or narrative.
  • PK4 T4-3: listen, contribute or explore through chosen access.
  • PK4 T4-4: stay involved through a naturally developing topic, with support.
  • PK4 T4-5: gradually extend engagement when interested; neither exact twenty minutes nor novelty is mandatory.

These authored pathways are not prerequisite gates or added state standards. PK3 and PK4 windows retain their exact meanings, but a short episode is partial evidence, not proof of mastery. Never equate the planned group duration with individual attention. Record actual bounded observations and interruptions; unknown duration preserves qualitative evidence without inventing minutes. Familiar materials, repeated words or adult support do not automatically erase genuine engagement. Quiet listening deserves uncertainty where evidence is unclear, not a mandatory visible response test.

Three mechanisms

Lesson 01 uses shared physical-object noticing. Lesson 02 uses a teacher-led narrative. Lesson 03 uses group movement/making. Their mechanisms differ, but attention is the common construct; correct vocabulary, synchronized motions, story recall or artistic product quality are not hidden required outcomes. Siblings remain unqualified by this targeted family correction.

Honest delivery modes

CLASSROOM gives real teacher-led group context. ADULT-LED one child and CO-PLAY are worthwhile responsive practice but official GROUP evidence stays NOT_OBSERVED. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED; no digital parity or software usability is claimed. No field outcomes or later learning are established.

Access and family context

Use home language, sign/AAC, tactile/hearing/vision supports, safe chosen movement and individualized plans. Fade unnecessary repeated redirection rather than communication access. Never withhold toilet, food, water, comfort or rest. Ask families about interests and supportive communication without requiring home work or disclosure. Protect identified records. Optional adult print aids support notes, not public ranking, diagnosis or scored learner worksheets.
