Objective-level educator guide: group attention

This uncounted family guide supports PK3.I.B.3.b and PK4.I.B.3.b. Read DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md for exact source text and responsive pathways. The 2022 TEA original PDF page 14 was directly compared; local hashes are provenance, not learning evidence.

Lesson 01 is physical-object noticing; Lesson 02 is interactive narrative; Lesson 03 is movement/making. Choose according to children's interests and available access, not a compulsory method ladder. One educator can teach; no staff-only demonstration is necessary. A model is optional and may use familiar content. Attention is not original answers, eye contact, correct naming, stillness or independent performance without access.

ENTRY invites and observes, optional MODEL demonstrates a teacher-owned action without crediting it to children, DURING follows and develops interests, EXIT records meaningful engagement without requiring a new object, REVISIT returns on a later genuine occasion. Familiar participation is valid; transfer claims require actual changed-context evidence. Keep supervision and care ahead of testing. Use manageable focal observations, rotate later, record only known intervals and interruptions, and preserve qualitative evidence when timing is unknown. Do not infer continuous attention from the class timetable or reject quiet listeners because they do not perform.

PK3 up to 10–15 minutes at a time and PK4 up to 20 are developmental context, not minimums, ceiling failures or diagnostic thresholds. A short span is partial evidence. CLASSROOM requires a real educator-led group for that source context; ADULT-LED one child and CO-PLAY are related practice, group evidence NOT_OBSERVED. SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED. Learning, field effectiveness and physical accessibility are unverified. Targeted family repairs do not qualify Lessons 02/03; their remaining authored detail needs independent examination before acceptance.

Explicit synthetic-helper record contract (DOT20 R06)

The optional evidence.py helper is not required to teach, observe or retain a qualitative note. All Boolean fields require literal Python True/False, including the assessed interval's opt-out/safety judgment. Strings, numbers, containers, None and omitted opt-out judgments raise a caller TypeError before any inference. Do not coerce unknowns to False: repair the adult record, never label the child. Grade and source must be exact strings; duration may be None (unknown) or a finite nonnegative int/float, never a Boolean. No minimum minutes, novel context, stillness or eye-contact gate is introduced. Meaningful qualitative evidence survives absent, untruthful or zero measured duration. Untruthful duration is never exposed as observed minutes. A NOT_OBSERVED/opted-out assessed interval reports no duration or changed-context observation; an earlier genuinely observed interval must be stored separately and is not erased by a later break. Different-context metadata requires an actual teacher-led real group, not a dyad or virtual group, and never implies transfer success. All inputs remain fallible adult judgments; correct types are not proof of actual attention or mastery. This shared contract applies to sibling helpers without qualifying their teaching designs.
