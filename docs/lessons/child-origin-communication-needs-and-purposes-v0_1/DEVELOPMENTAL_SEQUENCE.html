Language a child actually authors: needs/wants to multiple meaningful purposes

Original 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines Domain II.B.1 PDF zero-index 20, published printed cells: PK3.II.B.1 “Child uses language to communicate basic needs and wants.” SHA 0063578a583acfe8cb1a4f6454615316564a8f1646525607bfbd4c285428e3ca; PK4.II.B.1 “Child uses language for multiple purposes.” SHA 701a1b3eee0e95ffa56e4e4a0fcd4558b56a564de7cd1275d6dbd31f8e69c274. Registry PRINTED_GUIDELINE_OUTCOME, original research PRINTED_PK_GUIDELINE_OUTCOME, original-cell SHA matches; alternate-PDF-text concordance still pending. Not a published requirement for three requests or any standard fixed communicative-purpose count. This family is not II.B.2 nonverbal conversational conventions or II.B.4 context-specific style.

Define actual source language use. A child chooses an actual message meaning and expresses it through spoken home language, child-origin sign, meaningful expressive AAC (not merely adult prefilled sentence accepted by a passive tap) or validated accessible language. PK3 purpose is a real child's own safe basic need or want (help, accessibility support, preferred harmless activity/material, permission to stop, comfort) communicated to a responsive real listener in a natural opportunity; puppet saying it for child or tapping a staged answer about fictional thirst is teaching practice only. Do not manufacture distress or withhold basic care, food, water, toileting, safety, appropriate attention or medical aid. An adult may proactively meet a need even without verbalization; do not withhold it pending student speech. Optional communication opportunities can arise during a safe shared project where child makes free choices and may ask for materials, help or to finish. Respect refusal and right not to engage; absence of genuine opportunity results NOT_OBSERVED rather than "failed to communicate."

PK4 multiple purposes: Child chooses different reasons to communicate, not simply more words. Some genuine functions are request, report a discovered fact, ask an information-seeking question, explain a preference/reason, suggest an idea or initiate/repair an exchange. A sample across at least two visibly distinct purposes can support a source-action candidate, with broader samples across contexts for credible transfer; the author records a varied sample, not a state-mandated numerical minimum. All-puppet scripted talk, three repetitive requests, copying adult statements, a click that auto-sends an adult-origin message or reading scripted display alone is insufficient. An authentic responsive human listener helps ground intended purpose; the SOLO specification may support interactive practice with learner-authored expressive choices in a future app, but no working source evidence is currently observed. A child may use language in safe play through imaginative content; do not equate fictional action with verified real-world transfer.

Source evidence cadence: ENTRY before adult MODEL with no supplied phrase, genuine open choice or unfamiliar shared observation; record child first freely authored expression, purpose, referent, real listener or transparent simulation, listener response, true origin. MODEL educator produces an unrelated communicative act on DIFFERENT objects/person/idea and names what it accomplished. DURING coached turns and semantic repair (H conceptual answer prompting vs A child-directed device/motor operation). EXIT a genuinely NEW independent communicative opportunity with no adult-supplied content; PK3 a genuine own need/want to a real willing listener, PK4 child meaningfully uses different independent communicative functions across new contexts with responsive acknowledgment. REVISIT actual later calendar date different opportunity, NOT_OBSERVED until performed. Evidence first-origin U independent, H adult conceptual supplied, A access-only under child's exact intended meaning, X unclear. If listener cannot interpret, give honest clarification chance without inserting adult content; independent child self-repair can support use, but a teacher-provided full sentence is H. No synthetic result means actual learning or mastery.

Access and safety: Strong home-language and multilingual honor, translation for listener but no invented child meaning, signed/expressive AAC child-chosen message, visual/tactile object choice without preselecting child's preference, quiet sensory supports, safe large nonchoking materials and true child motor proxy A. If adult knows child needs a break, provide the break whether or not asked. No publicly exposing private needs, health, families or personal information, no forced conversation, gaze, pleasantries, food deprivation, unnatural coercion, repeated requests for help already freely provided, or diagnoses from speech delay. Collaborate with real IEP/IFSP/504 team for actual child. Source does not require English grammar or “please.”

PK3 stage progression — E3-1 through E3-4

PK3 E3-1 — notice communicative choice and self-origin signal

ENTRY: present two harmless available large drawing materials and allow child to freely select, but do not ask them to recite adult wording. Say only “You can choose what you want to use, or choose not to.” Observe whether child independently communicates their want by own meaningful home-language words/sign/AAC, not whether adult infers choice from reaching. MODEL: with DIFFERENT teacher-owned books say “I want the large book,” then teacher models listening/responding without withholding it from child. DURING: invite child to try own meaning-bearing message in another comfortable choice context and provide AAC vocabulary if needed without choosing content. Misconception: adult says “Say red” and child echoes; label H, model other case and offer new free child-origin choice. Fresh EXIT: changed safe real opportunity with willing listener, child independently makes an understandable own request/want, or mark NOT_OBSERVED if no desire expressed; don't force. REVISIT: actual later date new opportunity NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 E3-2 — self-advocacy for assistance, comfort and stopping

ENTRY: during safe natural optional art setup learner might voluntarily need a large washable pencil or positional help; adult does not manufacture a deprivation or prevent participation. Observe if learner independently indicates “help,” “can you move this?”, “break please,” or corresponding own AAC/sign/home-language meaning. MODEL: teacher uses other adult-only self-advocacy “I would like the chair moved” and models immediate respectful listener response. DURING: adult practices interpreting different real needs while continuing to meet genuine needs proactively and documenting any adult phrase supplied H. False-positive: child requests water only after adult withheld it and prompted exact words; not acceptable; remove coercion, meet needs and later observe a truly spontaneous safe request. Fresh EXIT: new natural safe communication of genuine help/comfort/stop/want to a responsive listener, child origin and listener effect documented. REVISIT: different date, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 E3-3 — communicate across changed harmless choices and listeners

ENTRY: offer a new inclusive safe shared building or reading activity with real choice of material or desired change; child may communicate meaning to familiar willing listener voluntarily. MODEL: adult on different props asks for different art tool to change activity, identifies what listener understood. DURING: child learns to select clear meaningful words/sign/AAC and optionally clarify ambiguous expression to meet their own intention; don't overrequire a complete grammatical sentence. Misconception: adult interprets a copied decorative sticker as child's actual need, but child never chose meaning; ask child to independently direct actual message after model on other item. Fresh EXIT: changed real non-private activity/need/want with actual child-origin expressive meaning and responsive listener. REVISIT: later date.

PK3 E3-4 — full meaningful needs/wants source transfer

ENTRY: unfamiliar harmless free-choice activity and a receptive adult listener, no adult-supplied request phrase, observe first genuine self-origin expression if child has authentic want/need. MODEL: separate teacher-owned alternative request after ENTRY; DURING encourage communication choices and responsive listener with access-only A; full new unmodeled EXIT: an actually separate real safe child-owned need/want arising naturally and expressed in accessible language to real willing listener, child selects meaning and listener responds, no adult script, no withheld basic care or coercion. Consent not expressed or no suitable opportunity remains NOT_OBSERVED, not failure. REVISIT: later calendar date/new genuine need or want, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 stage progression — E4-1 through E4-5

PK4 E4-1 — retrieve independent authentic request/need

ENTRY: same source-free safe choice as PK3 foundation with a different natural real willing listener, child independently asks for requested object/help/stop in chosen language. MODEL: teacher requests a different noncritical material and receives listener response; DURING clarify words but no adult fill-in, fresh EXIT new own request used effectively; only prerequisite for PK4 “multiple purposes,” not full source. REVISIT another date.

PK4 E4-2 — independently SHARE information, not only request

ENTRY: child encounters a new real observable safe change, e.g. a large circle moved under the box, and a genuinely uninformed willing listener asks openly “What did you notice?” Child independently shares original true observation, not adult-scripted label. MODEL: adult on DIFFERENT tabletop change says “I noticed the square moved,” contrasting sharing information from asking for material. DURING: practice meaningful observation report and a child-chosen request, coach access if needed. Misconception: three different color requests are marked three purposes; repair by contrasting truthful report vs request on adult model, fresh child-owned unseen report/need. Fresh EXIT: new genuine safe observation reported in own language to listener, plus separate authentic own request when naturally available; record two distinct purposes but no state numeric cutoff. REVISIT later actual date.

PK4 E4-3 — ASK a genuine information-seeking question

ENTRY: with new covered-but-safe large object/material behind an opaque box whose contents child does not yet know, a willing listener can help; “What would you like to know?” is open invitation rather than supplied wording; child may originate an actual question, not a prompted “what's in it?” echo. MODEL: teacher on DIFFERENT mystery safe cloth bag asks own sincere question and waits for informative answer. DURING: learner explores how asking differs from requesting an object or telling a fact; support may be H, no dictated response. Misconception: teacher preselects a yes/no question button and child taps without wanting answer; repair child chooses genuinely uncertain topic and new question in own words. Fresh EXIT: new genuine safe information gap and child-origin meaningful question with real listener answer, plus a separate self-owned different communication purpose over new situation. REVISIT: later date.

PK4 E4-4 — EXPLAIN an idea or preference in meaningful context

ENTRY: child privately chooses among safe materials for an optional group project, and a willing listener genuinely asks “Tell me about your idea” without requiring adult's target design; child expresses self-chosen idea/reason or chooses to decline. MODEL: teacher describes DIFFERENT adult craft decision and its purpose (“I suggested a wide base so my own tower stays stable”), separates suggesting/explaining from asking. DURING: support child expression, AAC/sign/home language meaningful, listener may ask for clarifying detail but must not write child meaning. False-positive: child repeats adult's explanation word for word and it is counted as independent innovative purpose; teacher models separate, then a NEW real choice and own idea. Fresh EXIT: new project child originates explanation/suggestion plus a distinct purpose such as information question or request (record actual purposes, no fixed official count). REVISIT later calendar date.

PK4 E4-5 — full multiple-purpose source transfer

ENTRY: new genuine safe co-play or classroom project offering several authentic, different communicative opportunities (child can express a wanted change, report one newly observed detail, ask a real question or explain plan), without adult dictating content or coercing participation. MODEL: teacher on a DIFFERENT project demonstrates some distinct functions after first observed responses; DURING child practices and adult logs concepts H vs access A. Full unmodeled EXIT: in new low-risk opportunities child independently uses language for multiple meaningfully distinct purposes, e.g. a genuinely own request and an independently informative report/question/suggestion, with real listener response and actual child-origin semantic content. A broader three-function portfolio strengthens evidence but is author evidence sampling, not official fixed number. Same scripted request three times does not establish purpose variety. REVISIT: genuinely new later date new functions/context, NOT_OBSERVED.

November 1 access audit — authored child intent, safety and true multiple purposes

PK3 source is the child's own meaningful REAL basic need/want communicated in accessible language to a responsive real listener; no food, water, toileting, medical, safety or comfort need may be withheld to elicit a phrase. PK4 is genuine distinct communicative purposes, not three differently colored requests. Child-intentionally chosen PREPROGRAMMED AAC can be expressive; adult-clicked or adult-authored exact answer is HELPED. A report from an information gap, puppet's imaginary thirst or an adult requesting a material does not automatically satisfy PK3.

Modes and provenance: Solo app is unbuilt and its simulated puppet not a child-owned need; CO-PLAY, ADULT-LED and CLASSROOM require voluntary real listener acknowledgment, accessible child-directed semantic choice, distinction of content H from device/motor A and no mandatory speech/politeness. Absent a natural request, valid information gap, sincere curiosity or safe communication opportunity, record NOT_OBSERVED; never manufacture hunger/distress as assessment.

Three distinct pedagogical mechanisms

  1. Child-safe self-advocacy and free-choice message: a child chooses a real harmless want/need and initiates own language without deprivation; adult responds to actual meaning.
  2. Information-gap listener exchange (not yet in reviewed library): learner discovers information only they or another willing listener possesses, reports a genuine fact or asks a genuine question and sees language move information across people.
  3. Message-intent/repair lab (not yet in reviewed library): a listener initially misunderstands an ambiguous teaching-only message, child learns differing purposes and independently edits/clarifies their original meaning in a new communication opportunity; not an adult-provided politeness script.

Four full WRITTEN modes — intended conceptual ambition, not demonstrated equal effectiveness

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future child-only prereader tablet offers original narrated accessible safe choice spaces, real child-owned message generation via speech/sign/expressive AAC selection without adult-filled target phrases; agent listener may respond to original practice messages for request/report/question/explain, including meaningful mistaken interpretation for supervised teaching. PK3 source actual own real need/want is not established merely because child clicks a fictional character's thirsty request. Design could connect to genuine child's independently chosen harmless wanted activity or break (app respects stop) and verify child-origin message; no coercive need creation. PK4 could independently use multiple meaningful messages for distinct real questions/preferences/observations in actual interactive tasks, but virtual simulated feedback is not validated live learning. A future actual child data layer must log first semantic origin U/H/A/X, distinct function and real/fictional listener, unmodeled EXIT and truly different-date REVISIT. Home languages, AAC, switch/gaze, narration and safe offline privacy need actual implementation/expert testing. There is currently NO WORKING SOLO. CO-PLAY: Caregiver offers real safe choice and responds promptly to actual child-authored need/want without withholding basic care; PK3 listener listens to own request, PK4 adult also engages on genuine observation/meaningful question/child suggestion as distinct purposes. Before MODEL child first expresses freely, MODEL on different caregiver-owned context, DURING practice, EXIT fresh natural opportunity with response. Caregiver holds AAC board at child's exact direction A, cannot choose child's content H. Genuine later-date REVISIT and actual autonomy respected. ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Tutor plans nonintrusive voluntary art/project encounters with child choice and a real responsive listener; observes PK3 actual need/want vs proxy invention; teaches via different safe example and access alternatives, then checks independent new-context child language. PK4 provides distinct real purposes beyond repeated requesting, with opportunity to report observed fact, ask real question and explain child-selected design. Confirm sign/AAC/home-language meaning, actual IEP/IFSP/504 accommodations with appropriate team, private need data. Different-day revisit not yet observed. TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Educator prepares safe optional learning centers, always meets genuine physical/emotional needs with no language gate. Individual learners may independently request materials or stop and are listened to; PK4 additionally can report actual class observation, pose authentic inquiry or share own idea with a willing responsive adult/consenting peer; no forced peer communication, scripted group chorus or public disclosure. Student-level source evidence separates who authored meaning and purpose from teacher response. Model/DURING distinct, independent new source EXIT and actual later new-day REVISIT. No mandatory “please,” eye contact or grammar norm.

Differentiation/ethics: child-origin meaning and safety are primary; all needs proactively met, home-language or expressive AAC sign accepted, no adult-created answer button. Visual choice board can enable communication but adult determining the selection H not U/A. Include low-pressure listener consent, no assumptions about language preference, actual disability access-team IEP/IFSP/504 collaboration, privacy and non-mandatory participation. Only internally authored documents, digital print, synthetic evidence checks; no actual educator/child/physical/solo/mode effectiveness validation.
