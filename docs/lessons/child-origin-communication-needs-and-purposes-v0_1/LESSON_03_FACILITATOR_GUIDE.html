Companion facilitator 03 — Personally authored meaning repair

Paired complete lesson: Lesson 03; separately uncounted objective educator guide.

Specific instructional mechanism

The caregiver can hypothetically demonstrate an actually ambiguous adult-owned expression using two different safe adult objects, even if they are the only adult. A second genuinely willing adult can optionally demonstrate a real unrelated example. A child may already communicate own basic need or another meaningful function clearly; misunderstanding and repair are not prerequisites for official source language. If real harmless confusion arises naturally, a child may freely choose clarification. Never deliberately misunderstand a clear real care/stop message, demand extra adjectives or force English/politeness scripts.

Adult preflight, real materials, genuine child agency

Prepare safe broad accessible already available objects or familiar book, a different adult-owned potential ambiguity, quiet no-task exit and existing home language/sign/AAC. The caregiver is an actual willing listener; no extra human actor required. Adult demonstration with one person is hypothetical, no true listener misunderstanding claimed. Do not block water, safety, food, bathroom, comfort or communication access. Novel safe context can offer stronger optional transfer, not eligibility. Record actual child-authored message, whether adult initially understood, genuine optional child-chosen clarification and access-only versus meaning-bearing help.

ENTRY, adult-owned MODEL, child-led DURING, source-valid action and optional transfer, actual later REVISIT

ENTRY: observe child actual own meaning to one present real caregiver before adult content model; if child already clear, no repair needed, if listener genuinely misunderstands preserve original expressive act and comprehension separately. MODEL: caregiver with two different adult-owned truly confusable objects can explain a hypothetical ambiguity “that one,” with any fictional reply labelled imaginary. Extra real willing adult is optional. DURING: offer authentic sign/home-language/child-selected stored AAC expression and optional neutral clarification only when actually uncertain; avoid adult-chosen exact words and coerced repeats. EXIT: PK3 actual child-owned basic need/want may occur on familiar material, independent of repair; PK4 genuinely different original purposes (including unforced greeting/refusal/comfort/narrative/inquiry) over actual safe occasions, not merely two rephrased requests. New context optional stronger transfer, not source. REVISIT only later date actually witnessed, NOT_OBSERVED beforehand.

False-credit and misconception-specific correction

A caregiver deliberately pretending not to understand a clear child care/stop message is unsafe and invalid; meet essentials promptly. A short “that one” with meaningful child-selected sign/AAC and shared context may already be clear. If genuinely unclear, ask neutrally and permit child to stop, while retaining the original expressive act even if listener has not yet understood. Child echoing teacher’s corrected sentence is guided practice, not independent meaning. A more detailed request is still request; PK4 distinct purposes may also include genuine social, creative or narrative language, never forced apologies or greetings.

Grade-specific source boundaries and honest original-language evidence

PK3.II.B.1: child genuinely independently uses expressive LANGUAGE to communicate a real own BASIC NEED or WANT, including chosen stored AAC, meaningful sign or home language to responsive real listener. Silent grasp, puppet answer, fabricated deprivation and adult-selected phrase cannot independently prove this source. Never withhold actual essential care or communication access. PK4.II.B.1: actual original language for MULTIPLE DISTINCT PURPOSES, including genuine request, report, question, suggestion, explanation, greeting, refusal, invitation, comforting, narration and other contextual functions; not merely three identical requests, copied frames or speaker-selected icons. No fixed word count, sentence length or English-only voice criterion; source functions can be evidenced across appropriate genuine opportunities. Track child-original U, adult-written H, access-only A, uncertain origin X, true recipient, actual purpose and whether listener really responded. If no genuine need or relevant purpose arises, mark NOT_OBSERVED, no child deficit. Genuinely later calendar REVISIT only, no fabricated sessions.

Seven differentiated needs and written delivery modes

Attention/executive: authentic small optional choices, fade neutral picture reminders. Language/communication: learner-created purposeful home-language, sign, AAC including personally selected meaningful stored vocabulary; an adult cannot select semantic content. Literacy/phonology: no written words prerequisite. Numeracy: number of utterances never proves different purposes. Autism/sensory: voluntary low-stimulation context, no forced eye contact or communication performance. Developmental/intellectual: adult demonstration on different situation and increasingly original child requests/reports across grade. Motor/hearing/vision: stable accessible safe items and genuine neutral learner-directed proxy/qualified individualized access rather than preselected answer. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: virtual message button alone, especially preselected by an app, cannot prove real original communicative intent; no operational learner system. CO-PLAY: caregiver listens and responds to genuine child chosen meaning, essential needs served regardless of phrasing. ADULT-LED: adult-only model uses different props and words before unbiased new learner purpose. CLASSROOM: respect children's privacy, own individual messages and responsive willing listeners rather than choral recitation. Real child learning, independent educator and access expert approval, physical print validation, actual later REVISIT and four-mode educational parity NOT VERIFIED.

COMM55 source/reader validity note: PK3 needs genuine own basic needs/wants in child-authored meaningful language; PK4 needs different original pragmatic purposes, including child-led social/storytelling functions and contextually judged emerging alternatives, not a closed enum. An already-clear message, a familiar prop, an adult who understands immediately, or child refusal of repair is not an educational deficit. Additional listener, staged ambiguity directed at a child, novel context, forced longer phrase or compelled apology are not source requirements. Future child-only digital mode remains unimplemented, and actual classroom/child effectiveness isn't observed.
