01 · You choose what matters to YOU

Teacher setup for true available safe material choices · Original II.B.1 first-party teacher materials · NOT observed child outcomes

Both options are always freely available—never withhold a basic need

Teacher: “These are both available. You may use either, ask for help, or choose to stop.” Child's message is NOT required to receive essential care or access. Listen if they independently express a want with voice, home language, child-directed sign/AAC; do not make them say a prewritten exact sentence.

PK3 source: child's own **real basic need or want** expressed through meaningful language to responsive willing listener. Adult-scripted puppet request is practice, not the child's own need. PK4: authentic language for *different purposes*, not merely three different requests.

02 · Different ways to communicate

Adult-only MODEL of functions, no scored child answer choices · Original II.B.1 first-party teacher materials · NOT observed child outcomes

REQUEST · child owns the want

Adult teacher model with different items: “I would like a large sheet.”

REPORT · share actual information

Teacher: “I noticed MY sample circle moved.”

QUESTION · seek missing information

Teacher asks what they truly do not know about a different safe tool.

SUGGEST · explain a new idea

Teacher suggests a different design and describes why they chose it.

These examples are adult MODELS, NOT a menu of correct child messages. For PK4 different purposes require different intentions, not several versions of “give me that.” No fixed official purpose count or sentence length. A child may choose stored AAC vocabulary with genuine independently selected semantic intent.
