Seven individualized learning-difference decisions for this exact method

Attention/executive: Give one vessel and one large fitting object per first exploration, clear quiet work area and voluntary pauses. Use one meaningful child-first opportunity and optional pause; no timed compliance gate.

Language/communication: Accept home language/sign/AAC to describe hollow versus solid and inner room; partner may translate only child's idea. Use first-person home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) as appropriate; staff never authors child meaning.

Literacy/phonological: No requirement to read capacity labels, diagrams or numeric scales; usable tactile boundary is source. Allow neutral text support except where real original print or spoken sound is itself the construct; do not substitute image-only or adult-read content when source requires independently observed print meaning.

Numeracy: Counting same pieces can help fair comparison PK4 but cardinal number, milliliters or printed measurement not part of PK3 source. No invented quantity, score, time, precision or literacy prerequisite beyond what is actually printed.

Autism/sensory: Use dry non-smelly nonspill materials, avoid startling drops and respect touch refusal; offer child-directed proxy. Respect autonomy and an accessible quiet route rather than compulsory gaze, touch, speed or emotional performance.

Intellectual/developmental: Adult models a different hollow/solid example, scaffold real interior inspection and fade prompts before new actual vessels. Supported DURING can use short steps; new EXIT must still demonstrate actual child-origin meaning with no answer-bearing prompts.

Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Stable waist-level wide blunt physical vessels, tactile exploration and child-directed placement for vision/motor needs; staff filling then choosing result is not child recognition. Neutral proxy may move a safe object chosen/controlled by child, not choose target or comparative relation. Verify required original sensory/physical source truly remains accessible.
