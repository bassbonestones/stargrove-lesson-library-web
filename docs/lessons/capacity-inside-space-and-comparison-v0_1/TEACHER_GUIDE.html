Actual distinct methods and their own companion guides

Lesson 01: LESSON_01_HOLLOW_INTERIOR_CAPACITY_DISCOVERY.md (resource not in reviewed collection)

Individual facilitator guide. Discover actual container interiors by feeling and filling a clearly hollow vessel with oversized safe items, contrasting with safe solid shapes whose outside size falsely implies inside capacity. Novel independent EXIT: Offer unfamiliar pair of safe blunt vessels with deceptive outer design and unlike actual usable interiors. PK3 independently shows awareness of capacity of actual inside of each; PK4 genuinely recognizes and compares which internal space fits more, without adult arranging answer. Misconception guard: A child picks a brightly colored tall solid model and staff credits high capacity, although it has no accessible cavity. Return to unrelated adult-owned hollow versus solid example; new actual internal-space probe.

Lesson 02: LESSON_02_COMMON_FILL_TRANSFER.md (resource not in reviewed collection)

Individual facilitator guide. Fair constant-piece transfer asks the learner to pack identical physically fitting oversized pieces into two different genuine cavities, with the same fill level and arrangement checked before comparing capacity. Novel independent EXIT: New pair of actual differently shaped wide safe containers with same-size physical pieces and comparable fill-to-rim conditions; PK3 attends to actual space inside as pieces fit; PK4 independently decides which usable capacity greater/equal by fair common-unit interior occupancy. Misconception guard: Teacher allows one container overfilled above rim then calls it bigger; overflow measures pile, not cavity. Demonstrate equal fill constraint on other objects and let child retest a novel honest setup.

Lesson 03: LESSON_03_PREDICTION_AND_COUNTEREXAMPLES.md (resource not in reviewed collection)

Individual facilitator guide. Prediction, disconfirmation and corrective evidence challenge misleading external shapes or apparent fullness using real unseen vessels and a child-chosen fair empirical inner-space test. Novel independent EXIT: Completely different pair of real original vessels with counterintuitive exterior but preverified different inside space: child predicts, executes fair actual inner-fit test, updates PK3 genuine recognition or PK4 capacity comparison without adult telling what to conclude. Misconception guard: A learner repeats 'tall means more' after an adult points to height without ever inspecting inside, even if guess happens correct. Change vessel shape and collect child-owned valid physical capacity evidence.

Seven authentic accessibility profiles and four written routes

Attention/executive: Give one vessel and one large fitting object per first exploration, clear quiet work area and voluntary pauses. Language/communication: Accept home language/sign/AAC to describe hollow versus solid and inner room; partner may translate only child's idea. Literacy/phonological: No requirement to read capacity labels, diagrams or numeric scales; usable tactile boundary is source. Numeracy: Counting same pieces can help fair comparison PK4 but cardinal number, milliliters or printed measurement not part of PK3 source. Autism/sensory: Use dry non-smelly nonspill materials, avoid startling drops and respect touch refusal; offer child-directed proxy. Intellectual/developmental: Adult models a different hollow/solid example, scaffold real interior inspection and fade prompts before new actual vessels. Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Stable waist-level wide blunt physical vessels, tactile exploration and child-directed placement for vision/motor needs; staff filling then choosing result is not child recognition.
