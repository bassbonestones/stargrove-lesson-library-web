Method 02 — Constant-Unit Transfer Laboratory (controlled physical evidence)

Original E2.2 written method; read DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md. Official PK3.V.D.2 begins to recognize capacity as room inside (SHA af075d156cb7f89fb2adf8dc3868c173f7db3375755781c76423b1bb16ff9f56); PK4.V.D.2 additionally recognizes and compares capacity (SHA 3b79f798a1971f8adbe5c11113650b2f73531846df9d7662735a9dfafd0b4717). Unique mechanism: learners produce and test controlled physical evidence by filling to a consistent interior limit with identical oversized dry units, reusing the same units and procedure. This differs from method 01's exploratory cavity recognition and method 03's counterexample prediction/argument. Unit counts illustrate evidence; no official standard-unit, exact numeral requirement or volume-conservation theorem is imposed.

Material and fair-comparison contract

Use clean stable large-mouth blunt-edged premeasured regular rectilinear interior boxes (no false bottoms) with dimensions intentionally fitting whole identical oversized blocks in a reproducible grid: for example, teacher may locally build two broad stable cardboard-lined bins whose interior floors hold one row of two vs two rows of two giant blocks at equal layer height. These are illustrative designs, not official quantity targets; actual pieces/containers must be dry-fit-tested in advance and never force-packed, obstructed or confused by thickness. Use a fixed orientation, one consistent inside-fill ceiling, identical block footprint/height and repeatable no-extra-gap layout; if boxes have different shaped voids, counting loosely packed items is not a reliable universal capacity measurement. All pieces must be larger than choking threshold, safe for youngest class child and never heavy enough to hurt. No loose small grains, food, hazardous liquids, high stacked columns, unstable towers or mouth-sized pieces.

Put different sizes/colors of teacher-only and child-use pairs on different mats; keep ENTRY, MODEL, DURING and EXIT disjoint. A printed count-box is an illustration; ORIGINAL_CAPACITY_CASES.html is not real evidence until actual capacity of local boxes is verified. For a non-rectangular vessel, compare by transferring measured equal-volume large safe containers of material in adult-controlled demonstration or use a verified volumetric equal-volume unit; note gaps and overflow instead of assuming item counts perfectly quantify arbitrary volumes. Do not overstate exact numerical capacity.

ENTRY before MODEL (grade-specific): For PK3, show a genuinely new accessible safe hollow vessel beside a similarly striking solid prop; ask “Where is there room inside for this big safe piece? Show me.” Record the child-origin real hollow/empty-space response before any model; no larger/smaller capacity ranking, formal equal-unit count or spoken numerical explanation required. For PK4, instead present a genuinely new two-hollow, adult source-verified regularly packable capacity pair and the same safe large uniform fillers; ask “Which could hold more if the inside were full? What fair way could you check?” Record the child-origin comparison plan, not the adult prevalidated answer, before MODEL. MODEL: entirely different verified rectangular pair; “I use the same kind and size of block in both. I start at the inside bottom. I fill each the same way until its inside is full. One could hold more of these identical blocks. That tells me about its room inside.” Narrate why incomplete fills and changing block size are unfair. DURING: learner controls equal safe pieces, chooses order and fills/child-directs adult to place blocks; ask “Are we comparing to the same inside top? Do the pieces match?” If adult chooses winner, label H. New EXIT (grade-specific): PK3 receives new actual safe hollow versus solid materials and independently demonstrates inside holding room without a relative size test. PK4 receives another real source-verified two-hollow pair, no teacher count/winner hint; child may select identical fully fitting units and complete the controlled fill or demonstrate another genuinely fair independently reasoned and actual-source-supported interior comparison. A guess about exterior height, teacher-supplied key or merely saying a word is not equivalent evidence.

PK3 progressive route — interior recognition before ranking

PK3 C3-1: ENTRY safe open box vs solid model, ask child where giant block could go. MODEL on unrelated hollow/solid props that a big piece fits only where room is inside. DURING child places/removes one oversized piece, then shows the empty cavity; EXIT new object child purposefully places or indicates inside. A learner able to put one piece in because teacher pointed gets H, not independent source evidence.

PK3 C3-2: ENTRY one empty broad box and same-size partly occupied box; ask whether each still has room inside. MODEL other identical broad bins removing blocks while walls remain. DURING child places and removes oversized pieces and senses space available; EXIT novel empty bin child still recognizes as having room. Counting how many blocks are visible now is not evidence of total capacity.

PK3 C3-3: ENTRY new low broad and tall narrow empty boxes, ask to show where pieces would go inside, not “which wins?” MODEL unrelated pair where neither outer height nor the printed color tells you what space is inside. DURING child tries one or more safe identical large blocks; no pressure to count or always rank capacity. EXIT new hollow versus similar outside-size solid object shows the inside criterion, and teacher optionally records comparison interest as extension only.

PK3 C3-4: ENTRY new play supply bin with no contents and a solid display prop, child directs where blocks can fit. MODEL separate different teacher-only example of holding space. DURING repeated self-directed safe loading/unloading; unmodeled full PK3 EXIT two unfamiliar open/solid and previously-filled/empty situations demonstrate initial capacity-as-inside-room meaning. REVISIT later actual date with new items; NOT_OBSERVED until performed. Numerical precision is PK4 enrichment, not invented PK3 source threshold.

PK4 progressive route — evidence with a constant unit

PK4 C4-1: ENTRY two equal verified boxes, one empty and one partly occupied; ask “Can both hold the same total if emptied?” MODEL distinct identical pair, fully empty and then equal-fill. DURING child verifies; EXIT new same-capacity pair unlike fill levels and child distinguishes total capacity from current amount.

PK4 C4-2: ENTRY unfamiliar safe large boxes with different tested true capacity and identical safe units, ask for child-origin comparison plan. MODEL on another regular verified pair; learner hears why exact same unit, packing pattern, interior top and full completion must be held consistent. DURING learner fits equal units in each under supervision; if pieces do not fit neatly, teacher says “This pair does not give a fair result; we won't guess,” swaps in verified regular pair, and tests anew. Fresh EXIT: unmodeled pair and uncued full check, learner interprets which holds more/less/same, not merely reports raw numbers from mixed units.

PK4 C4-3: ENTRY new preverified tall slim / squat wide pair, ask whether a tall rim proves more internal room. MODEL separate intentionally counterintuitive boxes filling identical pieces to interior ceiling; “Height is only one part of the room.” DURING learner performs repeatable child-directed fill; EXIT different counterbalanced pair and independently states total capacity relation with actual evidence. Rote “shorter wins” is equally wrong and cannot be taught as a rule.

PK4 C4-4: ENTRY partly full large box and full smaller box, record whether child distinguishes amount-present and total possible. MODEL another pair: first name what is here now, then empty both and fully compare with same pieces. DURING learner directs removal and complete refill; EXIT new full-small vs half-big with opposite apparent fullness, independently gives two correct non-equivalent judgments and verification. Don't demand numerical capacity for every irregular vessel.

PK4 C4-5: ENTRY unseen, safe, verified pair with colors and sides switched; learner chooses a fair comparison procedure. MODEL separate pair if instruction is necessary after ENTRY. DURING respond to any mismatch of filler size or half-fill, then return a new pair. Source EXIT: genuinely unseen real source-verified two-hollow boxes, independently conclude which can contain more at a shared actual full boundary (or equal where empirically equal) using the taught same-unit complete-grid fill or another valid child-origin fair comparison of true usable inside space; no required numeric unit count and no adult selected answer. The actual number of units is optional. REVISIT on later calendar date with different pair, NOT_OBSERVED now.

Four practical written routes (no claims of actual parity)

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future prereader tablet simulates exact identical large virtual 3D blocks inside transparent rectilinear cavities sized to integral fits, blocks cannot overlap walls or each other; every placement verifies a child gesture, switch, dwell, AAC or gaze choice rather than automatic autofill of comparison. PK3 starts one big piece in/out to learn capacity is inside, including an EMPTY virtual vessel with room. PK4 places into two fresh containers to complete a common-unit fill, with narration “Same size pieces, fill both to the INSIDE line”; child records comparison after fill. The app must detect and reject comparing unfinished fills or unlike units without announcing right answer before EXIT. Separate teacher modeled pair, independent new EXIT pair and actual later-date revisit must be implemented; nothing exists yet. Screen simulation cannot prove physical manipulation competence.

CO-PLAY: Caregiver independently precalibrates safe dry large-mouth boxes and shared oversized identical blocks. Child says/shows where they fit (PK3) and chooses/executes a same-unit fair test (PK4), adult gently steadies boxes at child's direction and records when teacher has helped conceptually. MODEL is caregiver's different pair, never the exit pair. After supported DURING, child gets new comparison materials with no hand-over-hand direction. REVISIT on a future day with fresh family containers if they can be calibrated safely.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Tutor separates four sets of pretested materials. For PK3 use gentle occupancy exploration, no new state requirement to count. PK4 elicits independent method before MODEL, teaches same-unit interior fill with different teacher objects, lets child repair an intentionally invalid comparison with teacher guidance then poses a new untouched pair. Source EXIT counts only when the learner initiates or child-directs complete comparison. Home-language or AAC responses count on actual concept, not English words; motor access assistance marked A, adult chosen winner H.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Teacher demonstrates with oversized pieces into teacher-only regular bins under supervised hands-on station; use limited supplies so no unsafe small items or blocked exits. PK3 child station: open/solid and empty/partly full; PK4 station: two calibrated bins + same giant blocks. Each learner gets an individual untouched scored pair and teacher records its actual check; group shouting a number before the child responds is not valid child-origin evidence. Children who cannot safely move a large piece direct an adult or use tactile/high-contrast and language supports. Plan separate sessions for REVISIT, not forced same-day “retention.”

Misconceptions and adversarial review

False-positive: child puts four small blocks in one vessel and two huge blocks in another, announces “four is more,” and adult scores accurate capacity. Or adult finishes one box, leaves second half empty and learner repeats “more.” These are not controlled comparisons and may reverse true interior volumes. Correction: teacher uses unrelated clean regular boxes and identical oversized items, shows both fully fitted to same inside boundary without gaps, asks child to explain why mixed units/half-fill are unfair; next independent pair requires learner-origin equal-unit complete verification. Another false-positive: PK3 child counts visible pieces and calls full box “more space,” never recognizes empty interior. Model a different emptied box, then test a new empty one. Remaining limitation: exact full-volume comparison with rigid blocks is valid only for controlled shapes and packing; irregular vessels demand more suitable uniform measurement/transfer. A single correct count is neither generalized capacity reasoning nor evidence of actual long-term mastery. No child observation, educator field validation, actual print or functioning independent SOLO or equal four-mode learning validation.
