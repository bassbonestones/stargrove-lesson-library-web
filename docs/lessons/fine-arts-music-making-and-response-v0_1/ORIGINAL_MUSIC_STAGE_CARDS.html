Choose a gentle musical role

Optional adult reminders · PK4 musical participation · PK3 enrichment

Use with a trusted adult. No reading, printing, counting, perfect timing or three-role performance is required.

Voice

“Would you like to hum, join our song, or be quiet?” Familiar singing and copied fingerplay are legitimate participation. A spoken response is not automatically singing.

Percussion

Offer one stable padded surface or inspected large sealed soft shaker. Child plays; adult-only demonstration is not child execution. No loose seeds, small pieces or loud surprises.

Move or rest

“A little seated move, your idea, or rest?” Keep safe personal space. A child-directed adult proxy preserves direction; record adult physical action honestly.

Model a soft hum and gentle taps, then show a pause. Repeat or change only as welcome. The child can join with support; no reserved new music test. Stop at fatigue or withdrawal.

One teacher: use one visible supervised space, not simultaneous stations. Children without the pad can hum, move, listen or rest. Notice individuals only when safe.

Optional: different musical styles

PK4.VIII.B.2 is separate from the participation roles on page 1.

Provide two genuinely different accessible musical styles using material you may lawfully use. A familiar lullaby and a distinct gentle dance song can work. Simple original long/short sound exploration is worthwhile, but does not automatically establish different musical styles.

Invite, do not prescribe

Preview softly. “Would you like to move or play with this music?” Small seated movement, safe toy play, a welcomed copied motion or a repeated meaningful movement are possible. No correct emotion, different dance or verbal explanation is required.

Keep the inference narrow

Record what music actually occurred, the child's movement/play and support. Printed genre labels, unchanged music renamed twice or adult-only response do not establish the child's response to different styles.

Access and choice

Use agreed signed/AAC/gaze choices and individualized visual/tactile access without inventing unaided hearing. Keep sound gentle and stoppable; do not apply touch or vibration without consent. Defer the extension if suitable styles/access are unavailable.

Familiar permitted family music is welcome without obligation to contribute. This packet contains no recordings. Revisit later only when welcome, and record actual later observations rather than planned results.
