Compound Words: Parts & Formation · original teaching resource

Compound-word objective educator reference (uncounted)

Familiarity boundary: For formation, the two base meanings must be familiar; the resulting meaningful conventional compound may be new to the child. Do not reject a first-time SUNFLOWER construction by a child who knows SUN and FLOWER. Recognition begins with an interpretable genuine whole-word prompt; a child hearing STARFISH for the first time may recognize familiar STAR and FISH. Prior familiarity with the completed compound is not a requirement for either target. “Familiar word” checking below is an available comfortable option, never a PK4 prerequisite that the completed compound was previously known.

Lesson 01 and its facilitator companion provide the current candidate sunflower word-garden path. Developmental choices explain the two source targets and optional local examples. This reference is not a fourth lesson.

PK3.III.B.3: “Child recognizes the individual words in a compound word.” PK4.III.B.3: “Child uses two familiar base words to form a compound word with pictorial or gestural supports.” TEA 2022 comprehensive PDF p.32 directly text-checked November 13, 2026. Child adding BOW/DROP after teacher RAIN is an official illustrative formation route. The publisher does not require both words be adult-supplied, an unheard compound, English-only speech or independent unaided responding.

Teach familiar meanings, invite the child, model as needed, practice with pictures/gestures, then note actual age-specific action and help. An immediate echo is supported practice; a later purposeful response on the same taught word can show source action. Optional transfer and later retention are separate. Never count a chorus, adult-selected card or an unobserved turn as an individual's response. Record uncertainty explicitly, without U/H/A/X codes. No opportunity is NOT_OBSERVED, not child failure. Keep access supports and child agency; do not require a fresh symbol layout or remove familiar AAC vocabulary.

Lesson 02 (not yet in reviewed library) focuses on object/place meanings; Lesson 03 (not yet in reviewed library) focuses on optional generalization. Both are historical unaccepted drafts pending separate substantive review. Shared corrections do not qualify these lessons or their companions. Existing novelty-focused probes are only transfer tasks, never grounds for denying genuine familiar-word source actions.

Use one adult, broad safe pictures/gestures and brief comfortable play. Classroom teachers retain sight of everyone during focal observation and defer evidence when supervision intervenes. Genuine compounds in the child's language, sign/AAC/gaze and child-directed placement are valid where meaningful. Teach inaccessible word meanings before drawing inference. Ask available family language knowledge without making family homework or specialist availability an entry condition. No forced speech, diagnosis, public ranking, recording or child data in shared files.

ADULT-LED and CO-PLAY are written human designs; CLASSROOM has supervision and individual-observation limits. SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED. No observed child learning, educator trial, physical/tactile print validation, completed later REVISIT or empirical mode equivalence is claimed.
