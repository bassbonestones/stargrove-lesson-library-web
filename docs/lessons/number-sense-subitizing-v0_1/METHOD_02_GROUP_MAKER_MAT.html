Make a Group, Notice a Group

MATH-PK-SUB-02 • Original blank maker/viewer mats, printable A4 • Child gets no printed answer or numeral pattern • Adult prepares safe large objects.

Maker workspace

Child chooses how to position large safe objects. Maker may count to build; this does not automatically prove no-count recognition.

My own pattern

Keep the object's quantity/number hidden from the later viewer.

Viewer workspace — a NEW group, made unseen

Adult makes a distinct unfamiliar set when child's attention is elsewhere (no blindfolding). Child now becomes the viewer. Show this new group without a count cue, and record the first quantity response.

What number do you notice here?

Use one original group at a time; PK3 quantities through 3, PK4 through 6. Do not flash, pressure, or score response speed. Real alternative/tactile safe group support may be needed.

Blank observation — Maker vs Viewer

Keep adult-only. Never treat a correct Maker construction as source-level Viewer recognition, and never claim real child data were supplied with this blank original.
