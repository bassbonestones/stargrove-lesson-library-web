Lesson 01 companion facilitator guide — Actual voluntary peer play

Pair: Lesson 01; sources PK3.I.C.7 and PK4.I.C.7. This guide is specific operational support, not a counted fourth plan. See objective guide.

Mechanism and preparation

Ordinary genuine choice with real willing peers creates chances to practice and observe authentic social agency without compulsory spoken greetings or friendship declarations. Two different accessible normal activity stations, duplicate large safe books/art materials, voluntary quiet area and trusted adult supervision are prerequisites. Real peers must be free to opt in/decline. Do not set up a planned rejection test, take photos, create a public preference roster or use tiny choking materials. If a child uses AAC/sign, arrange neutral support that transmits their own peer-directed meaning, not an adult-completed sentence.

Facilitator's script and feedback

ENTRY: Present real ordinary options; wait for learner’s first approach/response. “Would you rather work here or there?” can open access. “Go meet your best friend” supplies outcome; never infer one. MODEL: Two consenting adults demonstrate voluntary shared-material turn and respectful “not now” on DIFFERENT materials, then withdraw. DURING: child and willing peer may share a book, art or quiet co-play and personally respond; if peer declines, calmly protect both children, offer other activity and avoid blaming. If child watches only, model another adult-only gentle real choice and let learner decide naturally; observation is not evidence of failure. FADING: Stop suggesting a particular person, assigning greeting words, interpreting proximity as preference; retain only neutral mobility and language access. EXIT: New naturally occurring safe willing-peer interaction, never a scripted re-enactment. PK3.I.C.7 requires genuine child-authored peer interaction, preferred friend optional. PK4.I.C.7 additionally considers child-owned genuine peer preference; an expression of interest is meaningful evidence but does not independently prove stable reciprocal friendship. Record actual real interaction, child's preference, peer's consent to this encounter and any stronger natural repeated relationship pattern separately. Do not require two calendar dates, public label or mirrored feelings. Missing history means stability unverified, not that real observed preference vanished. REVISIT: Different actual date, different natural context; not observed by the author.

Misunderstanding and remedy

“Must announce best friend”: explicitly remove as source requirement; choice is private and no peer forced to accept. “Silent child doesn't interact”: check meaningful peer-directed gestures, sign/AAC and shared voluntary contribution before judgment. “Child always beside same peer”: check if adults fixed seating; do not infer preferred relationship. “Peer refused”: protect peer choice and note whether a real contingent exchange (child invitation, peer 'not now', child acknowledgment) still occurred. No shared play followed, but one real exchange may be source relevant. If there was no exchange, mark NOT_OBSERVED, not child inability. “Same room means peer play”: independent parallel presence without directed interaction is not source evidence. “Only avatar play”: record social practice but real human source NOT_OBSERVED.

Seven access pathways and four mode responsibilities

Attention/executive — familiar low-pressure option routine; language/communication — child-origin sign/AAC/home language; literacy — no friend labels to read; numeracy — no rankings or friendship quotas; autism/sensory — quiet/parallel/solo options, no compulsory gaze; developmental — consent model then fade social scripts; physical/motor/hearing/vision — reachable joint materials, individualized family/professional access, neutral proxy without supplying relationship choice. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: simulation can only teach, never credit real peer contact. CO-PLAY: real child peer must actually participate before direct source evidence is considered. ADULT-LED: teacher alone cannot supply a real peer. CLASSROOM: one real other child is enough for human peer interaction. Observe a feasible number of actual focal child–peer exchanges individually with genuinely available safe adult supervision of everyone else; rotate/defer and mark unwitnessed source NOT_OBSERVED, never infer 24 real exchanges from station attendance; no popularity displays or forced pairings. Child data, expert review, tested print, real REVISIT and validated parity remain NOT VERIFIED.

2026-11-03 focused method-diversity calibration addendum

CALIBRATION: NATURAL_VOLUNTARY_PEER_ENTRY_AND_RECIPROCITY (Method 1)

Actual learning action: In a naturally available ordinary shared-activity setting, the child voluntarily participates in an authentic moment with another REAL freely willing peer using their own chosen form of approach, response, parallel/common-interest contribution, gesture, AAC or speech. PK3 does not need a preferred friend; a single occasion can reveal real PK4 child-selected peer interest but should not be labeled stable longitudinal friendship.

Child-facing invitation and preparation: Make two genuinely attractive accessible activity stations available, but do NOT assign children to pairs or ask the child to name a best friend. Offer a quiet area and optional participation. Adult notices unforced child interest and mutually willing human response; an adult conversation or scripted greeting is not a peer.

Mechanism-specific contingent teaching: If the adult has supplied all the words and negotiated the exchange, model a gentle optional greeting with another consenting ADULT elsewhere; on a genuinely ordinary new occasion fade the social words and observe the child's own bid or response. If a peer declines further play, respect and support both children; if there was a real child-owned invitation, the peer's authentic 'not now' and child's response can be a real brief peer interaction, though no shared play took place. Offer an alternative without forced second attempt.

Source-valid exit/evidence interpretation: Record a specific real willing peer encounter and child-origin contribution privately, separating child agency, genuine reciprocal opportunity and adult access support. Source-valid PK3 interaction may be naturally brief; do not tally greetings or eye contact. Recurring real choices across natural opportunities can increase confidence about stable friendship, but official I.C.7 does NOT mandate two dates before a child-expressed peer preference is source-relevant; this method's single real exchange cannot automatically prove an enduring friendship. If the interaction has not genuinely happened, record NOT_OBSERVED rather than child failure. No fabricated child observations; SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Adversarial positive/negative contrast: Child comfortably shares attention over a real book without forced speech: potentially a valid actual interaction. Child taps friendly-avatar dialogue: valuable fictional play but NOT valid real-peer source action.

Cross-method non-equivalence and safety: Mechanism is initiating or joining ONE authentic willing human peer interaction. It must remain distinct from studying naturally recurring preference histories (Method 02) and practicing boundaries/inclusion (Method 03).

Honesty: This is a proposed pedagogical design clarification; not a child observation, qualified external educator assessment, validated implementation, or awarded score. The source-specific original developmental sequence remains required.

Source-versus-stronger-evidence operational check (current I.C.7 reconcordance)

PK3 real-source candidate: Actual peer-directed child response, a voluntary shared-attention moment that visibly affects the other child, or a respectful AAC invitation–peer free refusal–child acknowledgment. No favored peer, successful shared play, forced speaking or greeting quota required. Silent side-by-side work without any social contingency is not source. PK4 two distinct aspects: (1) actual real peer interaction; (2) genuine child-authored expression or observed preference for a real person, including interest in playing outside school. Another child's current agreement to share a specific activity is separate from whether the learner has a preference; no classmate owes reciprocal feelings. Repeated actual choices can substantiate a stable relationship but the source does not mandate two real dates or a named 'best friend'. For both: a new unmodeled peer setting supports stronger transfer if truly observed; familiar genuine response still can be source. If there is no actual peer exchange, mark it NOT_OBSERVED; if no evidence of preference PK4, mark preference aspect NOT_OBSERVED, without treating that as social deficit. Only private institutional records may contain identifying facts. No authoring-stage child observation.
