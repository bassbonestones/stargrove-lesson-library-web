Objective-level educator guide — Social-emotional I.C.7 Authentic Peers

PK3.I.C.7 — Child interacts with peers and may have preferred friends

Official cell SHA 3ab1bbc943cb94fa1ae84d86771c96f4e2598aa9160c42d5d837733f0ff7f6a5. A learner meaningfully engages with REAL WILLING PEERS, including authentic jointly chosen safe play, gesture, sign, home language or personally composed AAC. The phrase MAY have preferred friends makes preference optional. Never score lack of a favorite, eye contact, loud speech or preassigned buddy as lack of the published action. PK3 F3-1 through F3-4 in DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md move from supported real opportunity to private child-origin real peer interaction across ordinary contexts. A child may decline and choose quiet, and unavailable safe willing peers require NOT_OBSERVED, not a deficit judgment. Independent EXIT: Observe true learner-owned real peer interaction without forced scripts, with every peer freely consenting to any continued shared play, on familiar or newly changed safe activity; a brief genuine invitation–refusal–acknowledgment exchange may be observed without requiring the peer to agree to continued play. New-context observation strengthens transfer if genuinely observed; PK3 preferred-person expression optional, no friendship ranking.

PK4.I.C.7 — Child interacts with peers and has preferred friends

Official cell SHA ec007141b8792c5cd02efdb56fad4f8d45f20fd223e6cf1b54e263fb4cc5fd99. Preserve two source-relevant aspects: (1) authentic child-owned real peer interaction, including safe peer's freely offered refusal and child response, and (2) authentic child-expressed preference for a real person. Established recurring friend context strengthens confidence, but separate dated encounters and another child's matching preference are NOT additional printed source conditions. Mutual willingness is mandatory for each actual shared activity, not for the child to have a preference. The plural official wording is not an invented required friend count or permanent exclusive best friend, and teacher cannot compel a peer to reciprocate. Child’s relationship choice can vary. A single hello, repeated assigned seating, chatbot avatar, adult claim or public vote does not establish authentic preference. PK4 F4-1–F4-5 support familiarity, real self-directed social approach and ethical dignity without fabricated observational dates or public friend labels. Independent EXIT: A different naturally occurring actual peer opportunity can probe new-context transfer when witnessed. Record genuine real child/peer interaction and separately genuine child-owned preferred-person expression; actual recurring voluntary patterns strengthen stable relationship inference only. No observed interaction → that aspect NOT_OBSERVED; missing longitudinal records ≠ invalid authentic preference. No child may be forced to declare friends, and a peer may decline any play encounter.

Three pedagogically distinct approaches

  1. Lesson 01: actual child-selected voluntary real-peer play, with companion 01, centers real peer social agency and immediate respectful exchanges.
  2. Lesson 02: recurring shared-interest continuity, with companion 02, distinguishes genuinely returning to a willing peer on real days from school-assigned proximity.
  3. Lesson 03: friendship consent, autonomy and inclusion, with companion 03, explicitly teaches social boundaries and equal access. A fictional choice can prepare but not prove real friendships.

Education, assessment and misconception decisions

ENTRY: child first has real ordinary voluntary interaction choice (not teacher-directed favorite); MODEL: two consenting adults demonstrate a noncoercive invitation and kind refusal on unrelated materials; DURING: give accessible peer participation without adult-written words or pressured reciprocity, gradually fade social hints and distinguish nearby independent play from true contingent peer exchange; EXIT: a changed real willing peer opportunity for stronger lesson transfer when observed, without imposing novelty as publisher source gate; REVISIT: new real calendar date, NOT_OBSERVED at authoring. If a child prefers solo play, respect choice; if a peer says no, protect both parties and offer another activity. Repeated seating near someone is not relationship evidence. Written moral response is not actual peer interaction. An adult may not stage rejection as a diagnostic test. Keep any genuine child identity or friendship history out of the source repository.

Inclusion and four written modes

All seven needs addressed: attention/executive — predictable optional stations; language/communication — own sign/AAC/home language and meaningful gestures; literacy/phonology — no reading required; numeracy — no friendship counting; autism/sensory — quiet or parallel participation, no compelled gaze/touch; developmental — separate adult model, fade prompting; physical/motor/hearing/vision — accessible safe shared materials and neutral child-directed access, specialist review when needed. Do not infer lack of understanding from communication barriers. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: fictional peer scenes are useful practice but cannot prove actual human interaction/preference. CO-PLAY: adult/child rehearsal requires real freely willing child peers for source evidence. ADULT-LED: a one-child teacher interaction is not peer source evidence. CLASSROOM: one other actual real peer can support genuine interaction; with actual staffing observe individual focal child–peer exchanges, keep other pupils safely supervised, rotate/defer and mark unwitnessed interaction NOT_OBSERVED. No buddy labels or group chorus. Qualified professional approval, real child performance, genuinely later REVISIT, physical print and four-mode educational equivalence remain NOT VERIFIED.

2026-11-03 focused method-diversity calibration addendum

Three causally distinct instructional mechanisms (one source-objective family)

Method Actual child/teacher learning mechanism What cannot earn source credit
1 — Natural Voluntary Peer Entry And Reciprocity In a naturally available ordinary shared-activity setting, the child voluntarily participates in an authentic moment with another REAL freely willing peer using their own chosen form of approach, response, parallel/common-interest contribution, gesture, AAC or speech. PK3 does not need a preferred friend; a single occasion never establishes PK4 longitudinal preference. Child comfortably shares attention over a real book without forced speech: potentially a valid actual interaction. Child taps friendly-avatar dialogue: valuable fictional play but NOT valid real-peer source action.
2 — Natural Longitudinal Reciprocal Preference Observe whether the learner repeatedly CHOOSES to seek the company of one or more freely willing real peers across genuinely separate ordinary occasions, possibly through recurring common interests. PK3 may have no preferred friend; PK4 genuine child-selected preference and real child-peer interaction are two separate source-relevant evidence aspects; time-separated authentic returns make the distinct stronger claim of stable established friendship more credible, but are not mandatory for recognizing a genuine expressed preference or source-action candidate. Repeated time-separated child-origin willingness toward the same peer can justify cautious inference if alternatives and assent are real; a teacher seating two children together five days has NO such warrant. Do not require exclusive friendship or a specific count.
3 — Consent Boundary And Noncoercive Inclusion Practice Learners explore how to express preferred companionship, respect another child's refusal, keep shared materials accessible and respond to naturally arising inclusive choices; REAL willing peer interaction remains the direct source requirement. A purely fictional correct-answer conversation is supplementary social/consent teaching and cannot be credited as real-human peer interaction or an existing PK4 preferred relationship. A learner safely says no to an invitation; an actual peer-directed invitation–'not now'–respectful acknowledgment can be a genuine human peer exchange without a shared activity, while silently adjacent peers with no exchange do not establish I.C.7. Adult safeguards boundaries without calling either child deficient. A fictional avatar apology does not prove a real mutual relationship.

F1 method diversity is a conditional internal design hypothesis, not a 3/3 automatic pass. The teacher must compare what the child actually does, which misconception is addressed, and what truly independent evidence would follow. A repeated source objective or reuse of safe teacher scaffolds is expected; a prop/theme swap alone is not causal method diversity. F3 is progression, not a demand to rehearse the same activity. Distinctly teachable methods can share curriculum foundations without requiring children to complete all three. Use developmental readiness, child-selected interests and accessible alternatives. Consent and learning evidence: Child-origin action is never inferred from teacher-only model, sample script, present tense future plan or fictional proxy; no real learning/delayed outcomes observed. Do not infer actual X1–X5 game implementation from prose.

Real social outcomes are naturalistic: A child's chosen peer interaction can occur naturally within a safe voluntary classroom setting. An 'EXIT' assessment is a prospective educator observation protocol, NOT a staged friendship performance or a demand for social contact. PK3 'may' does not require friend preference, while PK4 child-origin preference evidence must be distinguished from genuine repeated association and from peer's independently exercised consent; an adult/virtual friend never replaces a peer. Method 03's optional consent rehearsal may be useful despite not independently satisfying social source credit.
