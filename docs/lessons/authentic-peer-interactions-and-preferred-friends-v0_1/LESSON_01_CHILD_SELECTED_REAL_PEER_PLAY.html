Lesson 01 — Child-selected real willing-peer play

Distinct actual peer-source instructional method: Offer authentic ordinary age-safe real willing-peer play with choice of separate or shared activities, and record actual child-owned socially contingent exchange with a real child peer, including quiet shared attention or a respectful invite–refusal–acknowledgment. Source PK3 cares about REAL PEERS but says 'may' have preferred friends; no special friend selection is required. PK4 can show genuine personal preference in child-owned expressed interest in a particular peer; repeated authentic choices may strengthen evidence for an established friendship but are NOT a universal two-date source gate. Another peer must choose whether to participate, never mirror the child's feelings on demand. This approach differs from repeated shared-interest patterns (Method 02) and classroom inclusion/friendship ethics (Method 03).

Authentic safe materials, consent and developmentally appropriate prerequisites

Actual normal safe accessible art/story with at least ONE real child peer freely participating in actual communication, including a freely communicated refusal to more play; no invented third-pupil prerequisite, broad original large-paper pictures/books, separate choice/quiet stations, trusted adult and child-selected home language, sign/AAC/gesture/adapted motor access. No mandatory friends chart, paired assigned best friends, exposure of who likes whom, private friendship ranking or peer rejection script. Provide independent peer choice with safe large first-party visuals, adult safeguarding, home-language/sign/AAC, child-directed motor support and optional sensory quiet. Actual child and peer consent is essential, and people can decline a friendship request or choose any available activity. In DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md the PK3 and PK4 progressions remain separate to avoid false friendship labels and forced peer relationships. Protect private student records and do not put actual child preferences or names in the public source repo.

Five complete source-sensitive instructional phases

ENTRY — real child-owned peer choice before unrelated adult MODEL: During real accessible child-selected peer play, observe child's actual choice to interact with freely willing real peer: following a shared interest, choosing a related big story picture, offering respectful information, or using sign/AAC. Never create exclusive buddy roster or require each child name favorite peer. PK3 showing authentic peer interaction is meaningful even without stable preference. PK4 may choose a particular familiar willing peer naturally, but one greeting alone doesn't demonstrate that the child actually has preferred friends.

MODEL — different consenting adult-owned example, NOT child source: Two consenting adults using unrelated adult-owned books demonstrate a mutual optional interaction, both can choose separate work and still respect one another. Adult can illustrate preferences without telling a child who their friend is. The teacher's partner choice is not child-owned peer preference and must not become EXIT script.

DURING — active supported real safe peer learning: Provide actual inviting noncompetitive broad materials at neighboring safe stations and offer child control over what to make/read and with whom to participate. Peers can accept/decline, and teacher never manipulates someone into saying no. Support real child-selected home language/sign/AAC/motor contribution, quiet and movement access. PK3 source interaction can be an authentic child-owned response/invitation, shared socially contingent joint interest, sign/AAC or gentle gesture with real peer. Side-by-side independent work without peer-directed awareness, response or shared interaction is NOT source credit. A peer's genuinely communicated 'not now' with a child's respectful acknowledgment can itself be a brief real exchange, though no shared play occurs; neither child must continue. No compelled favorite. PK4 might freely choose to spend additional genuine time with an actual consenting peer across ordinary varied opportunities, establishing authentic private preference without public ranking, required exclusive friendship or mandated class 'best friend'. Encourage open access to activities without excluding classmates and protect social boundaries.

EXIT — new unmodeled real freely willing peer opportunity: At a truly different naturally existing safe activity station, see genuine learner-origin peer interaction with willing real child. PK3 can interact with any peer; 'no declared friend' is not failure. For PK4 record separately (a) real peer interaction, (b) real child-owned indicated preferred-peer interest (including expression that a child hopes to play with a peer outside school), (c) any independently grounded recurrent relationship pattern and (d) whether each peer independently chose the particular interaction. One assigned pairing, an adult's label or fictional avatar is insufficient; repeated dates can strengthen confidence but are NOT an extra official source eligibility gate. Actual new-context EXIT supports lesson transfer only if observed. Absent genuine opportunities is NOT_OBSERVED, not social deficit.

REVISIT — genuinely later calendar day, not yet observed: Genuinely later calendar date and different safe child-owned real peer context, document authentic interaction/reciprocal choice and if genuinely expressed preference, without forcing it; REVISIT remains NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 four graduated responsive stages — peers real, preferred friend optional

  • PK3 F3-1: Interact safely with an actual willing peer in ordinary play. Child genuinely responds, offers, shares attention or communicates meaningfully with real peer via self-selected gesture/sign/AAC/words or voluntary mutually contingent joint play. Pure adjacent/parallel presence without interaction is not enough. Adult provides comfortable access.
  • PK3 F3-2: Participate with different peers without having to choose favorites. Offer distinct voluntary activity options; children can work with any willing peer or separately; no social ranking or compulsion.
  • PK3 F3-3: Show optional authentic emerging peer preference without pressure. Teacher supports child's natural interest in one or more peers, honors peers saying no and does not insist every child select special friend.
  • PK3 F3-4: Transfer respectful real peer interactions across normal contexts. Child interacts through meaningful age-appropriate own choice, home language/sign/AAC/gesture, optional quiet and appropriate adult support.

PK3 printed outcome is “Child interacts with peers and may have preferred friends.” A child may interact with peers in many appropriate ways including brief reciprocal safe exchange, responding to shared interest, simple social language, sign/AAC or meaningful gestural agency. The word MAY means a preferred friend is optional, not compulsory. No mandate for exclusive relationship, named special friend, perfect initiations or count of reciprocal partners. A child can choose quiet, regulate sensory needs, decline or change familiar peers. Real willing children—not teacher-only or a story avatar—are the target. A repeated adult-assigned buddy cannot substitute for genuine child-directed free social preference.

PK4 five graduated responsive stages — existing actual preferred peers, no public ranking

  • PK4 F4-1: Interact meaningfully with familiar real willing peers. Child freely contributes to an authentic peer interaction with reciprocal choice, no forced greeting, exclusion or permanent friend labels.
  • PK4 F4-2: Notice self-selected shared interest with certain peers. Teacher observes real common interests and accessible communication over actual occasions without requiring public 'best friend' declaration.
  • PK4 F4-3: Show genuine emerging reciprocal preference over actual distinct occasions. Real peer preferences are child-owned and other child free to reciprocate or decline; teacher privately records actual repeated choices/comfort contexts.
  • PK4 F4-4: Respect friendships while welcoming other peers' dignity. Teach boundaries, nonexclusivity, respect for classmates who choose other activities, and no forced buddy/secret social hierarchy.
  • PK4 F4-5: Transfer real peer interaction with authentic preferred friend choice. Offer meaningful child-owned accessible real opportunities to revisit preferred willing peer or choose new peers, with respectful consent and no public ranking.

PK4 source “Child interacts with peers and has preferred friends” calls for genuine actual peer interaction AND child-authored preference for real peer(s), without fabricated teacher assignment. An observed child-owned expression of preference is relevant even if the peer is unavailable or declines; actual shared interaction requires the peer to choose that encounter. Repeated natural choices across occasions strengthen the distinct claim of a stable preferred relationship, not a required minimum calendar-date count. This does not create fixed number of friends, all-peers friendship quota, permanent exclusive best-friend identity or forced reciprocal acceptance. No educator may compel another child to be preferred, require a learner's social private disclosure or publicly rank peers. Child preference and peer independent choice both matter; one hello alone, assigned seating, cartoon selection or group chorus does not establish an enduring friendship; do not erase a genuine child-origin preference expression merely because only one occasion is documented. Child's preference can change while other children's access and dignity remain protected.

Four fully substantive WRITTEN learning modes — honest direct source limits

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: A future independently usable child-only prereader tablet could provide original narrated fictional peer-play choices, voluntary virtual friendship interactions, self-directed nonreading AAC/sign/gesture choices, user-controlled return to favorite fictional characters and inclusive safe responses with no adult operating routine experience. This offers meaningful social understanding but no ACTUAL willing human PEER, so PK3/PK4 source remains NOT_OBSERVED until genuine real human peer interactions are observed. Fictional favorite avatar cannot be counted as real preferred friend. Implementation, child usability/privacy and equal effectiveness across teaching modes not verified.

CO-PLAY: Trusted caregiver and child explore complete original accessible first-party stories, safe age-appropriate friend preference concepts, consent and classroom inclusion, with adult modeling on own example. If an actually freely willing peer participates naturally, observe genuine child-selected interaction and if child really freely indicates peer preference, record what occurred without manufacturing reciprocal feelings or recurring history; stronger stable-friendship inference needs better evidence. Without genuine peer, grade direct source NOT_OBSERVED regardless of excellent adult-child conversational learning. Preserve family/cultural variation and children's option to play independently.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Educator delivers full understandable real social choice instruction with high-contrast broad visuals, adult-owned MODEL, relevant practice and new unlike scenario; a one-child adult conversation cannot by itself establish direct actual peer interaction or genuine preferred friend. An authentic child-owned real willing peer encounter later may support real EXIT, and PK4 child-expressed preference must be recorded separately from a stronger established repeated-relationship inference. Teacher doesn't falsely count puppet or required buddy as social evidence, and later-date REVISIT is not automatically done.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: A safe inviting child-selected real peer art/story environment permits actual opportunities with truly willing classmates, large duplicated materials, AAC/sign/home-language and sensory/motor supports. Teacher observes actual contingent child–peer actions and each peer's independent choice without assigned best friends, coerced greetings or public votes. Observe only a feasible number of focal children individually while all other pupils remain genuinely supervised and free to choose quiet activities; defer/rotate under actual staffing and mark unobserved children NOT_OBSERVED, not failed. PK3 actual peer interaction is source candidate even without any named preference. PK4 genuine preference expressions may be noted privately as child-owned evidence; repeated voluntary contexts strengthen, but do not become mandatory dates or demanded friend labels. Educators ensure access and address bullying, without coercing friendships. Fresh real unlike EXIT and genuine later REVISIT only when really observed.

False credit and concrete educational correction

False credit: Educator gives every preschool child a compulsory 'best friend' badge paired by seat, then records it as their own peer preference, shaming a PK3 child without a favorite. Correct: genuine free real peer interaction, PK3 may have preferred friends but need not; PK4 preference must be child-authored, not assigned; real peer interaction must respect each participant's current choice, but the preferred peer need not reciprocate preference.

Individually record actual source U child-authored choice, H adult-supported child agency, A accessibility-only motor/language support, X adult/puppet/script; note real willing peer and reciprocal context. A successful solitary virtual friendship game, mandatory seat partner, one 'hi' or social photo badge is not proof a child has preferred friends. Child peer autonomy and genuine optional preference are more important than a teacher-dictated social skill performance. If no real peer exchange occurs, mark interaction NOT_OBSERVED without deficit judgment. If an actual directed invitation receives a peer's freely chosen refusal and child responds respectfully, record that brief authentic human interaction accurately while noting shared play did not occur. A child's preference may be expressed even without peer reciprocating it; never pressure anyone to accept.

Accommodations, ethical boundaries and validation honestly outstanding

Home language, sign/AAC, safe tactile/large picture materials, quiet parallel/independent play alternative, sensory/mobility support, child-selected adapted motor proxy and individualized IFSP/IEP/504 as appropriate. Never publish real child friendship data or compel disclosure, touch, spoken voice, eye contact or peer contact. A preferred peer may freely say no and all children retain safe classroom access. 2026-11-03 focused method-diversity calibration addendum

CALIBRATION: NATURAL_VOLUNTARY_PEER_ENTRY_AND_RECIPROCITY (Method 1)

Actual learning action: In a naturally available ordinary shared-activity setting, the child voluntarily participates in an authentic moment with another REAL freely willing peer using their own chosen form of approach, response, parallel/common-interest contribution, gesture, AAC or speech. PK3 does not need a preferred friend; a single occasion can reveal real PK4 child-selected peer interest but should not be labeled stable longitudinal friendship.

Child-facing invitation and preparation: Make two genuinely attractive accessible activity stations available, but do NOT assign children to pairs or ask the child to name a best friend. Offer a quiet area and optional participation. Adult notices unforced child interest and mutually willing human response; an adult conversation or scripted greeting is not a peer.

Mechanism-specific contingent teaching: If the adult has supplied all the words and negotiated the exchange, model a gentle optional greeting with another consenting ADULT elsewhere; on a genuinely ordinary new occasion fade the social words and observe the child's own bid or response. If a peer declines further play, respect and support both children; if there was a real child-owned invitation, the peer's authentic 'not now' and child's response can be a real brief peer interaction, though no shared play took place. Offer an alternative without forced second attempt.

Source-valid exit/evidence interpretation: Record a specific real willing peer encounter and child-origin contribution privately, separating child agency, genuine reciprocal opportunity and adult access support. Source-valid PK3 interaction may be naturally brief; do not tally greetings or eye contact. Recurring real choices across natural opportunities can increase confidence about stable friendship, but official I.C.7 does NOT mandate two dates before a child-expressed peer preference is source-relevant; this method's single real exchange cannot automatically prove an enduring friendship. If the interaction has not genuinely happened, record NOT_OBSERVED rather than child failure. No fabricated child observations; SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Adversarial positive/negative contrast: Child comfortably shares attention over a real book without forced speech: potentially a valid actual interaction. Child taps friendly-avatar dialogue: valuable fictional play but NOT valid real-peer source action.

Cross-method non-equivalence and safety: Mechanism is initiating or joining ONE authentic willing human peer interaction. It must remain distinct from studying naturally recurring preference histories (Method 02) and practicing boundaries/inclusion (Method 03).

Honesty: This is a proposed pedagogical design clarification; not a child observation, qualified external educator assessment, validated implementation, or awarded score. The source-specific original developmental sequence remains required.

Lesson 01 companion facilitator guide — Actual voluntary peer play

Pair: Lesson 01; sources PK3.I.C.7 and PK4.I.C.7. This guide is specific operational support, not a counted fourth plan. See objective guide.

Mechanism and preparation

Ordinary genuine choice with real willing peers creates chances to practice and observe authentic social agency without compulsory spoken greetings or friendship declarations. Two different accessible normal activity stations, duplicate large safe books/art materials, voluntary quiet area and trusted adult supervision are prerequisites. Real peers must be free to opt in/decline. Do not set up a planned rejection test, take photos, create a public preference roster or use tiny choking materials. If a child uses AAC/sign, arrange neutral support that transmits their own peer-directed meaning, not an adult-completed sentence.

Facilitator's script and feedback

ENTRY: Present real ordinary options; wait for learner’s first approach/response. “Would you rather work here or there?” can open access. “Go meet your best friend” supplies outcome; never infer one. MODEL: Two consenting adults demonstrate voluntary shared-material turn and respectful “not now” on DIFFERENT materials, then withdraw. DURING: child and willing peer may share a book, art or quiet co-play and personally respond; if peer declines, calmly protect both children, offer other activity and avoid blaming. If child watches only, model another adult-only gentle real choice and let learner decide naturally; observation is not evidence of failure. FADING: Stop suggesting a particular person, assigning greeting words, interpreting proximity as preference; retain only neutral mobility and language access. EXIT: New naturally occurring safe willing-peer interaction, never a scripted re-enactment. PK3.I.C.7 requires genuine child-authored peer interaction, preferred friend optional. PK4.I.C.7 additionally considers child-owned genuine peer preference; an expression of interest is meaningful evidence but does not independently prove stable reciprocal friendship. Record actual real interaction, child's preference, peer's consent to this encounter and any stronger natural repeated relationship pattern separately. Do not require two calendar dates, public label or mirrored feelings. Missing history means stability unverified, not that real observed preference vanished. REVISIT: Different actual date, different natural context; not observed by the author.

Misunderstanding and remedy

“Must announce best friend”: explicitly remove as source requirement; choice is private and no peer forced to accept. “Silent child doesn't interact”: check meaningful peer-directed gestures, sign/AAC and shared voluntary contribution before judgment. “Child always beside same peer”: check if adults fixed seating; do not infer preferred relationship. “Peer refused”: protect peer choice and note whether a real contingent exchange (child invitation, peer 'not now', child acknowledgment) still occurred. No shared play followed, but one real exchange may be source relevant. If there was no exchange, mark NOT_OBSERVED, not child inability. “Same room means peer play”: independent parallel presence without directed interaction is not source evidence. “Only avatar play”: record social practice but real human source NOT_OBSERVED.

Seven access pathways and four mode responsibilities

Attention/executive — familiar low-pressure option routine; language/communication — child-origin sign/AAC/home language; literacy — no friend labels to read; numeracy — no rankings or friendship quotas; autism/sensory — quiet/parallel/solo options, no compulsory gaze; developmental — consent model then fade social scripts; physical/motor/hearing/vision — reachable joint materials, individualized family/professional access, neutral proxy without supplying relationship choice. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: simulation can only teach, never credit real peer contact. CO-PLAY: real child peer must actually participate before direct source evidence is considered. ADULT-LED: teacher alone cannot supply a real peer. CLASSROOM: one real other child is enough for human peer interaction. Observe a feasible number of actual focal child–peer exchanges individually with genuinely available safe adult supervision of everyone else; rotate/defer and mark unwitnessed source NOT_OBSERVED, never infer 24 real exchanges from station attendance; no popularity displays or forced pairings. Child data, expert review, tested print, real REVISIT and validated parity remain NOT VERIFIED.

Source-versus-stronger-evidence operational check (current I.C.7 reconcordance)

PK3 real-source candidate: Actual peer-directed child response, a voluntary shared-attention moment that visibly affects the other child, or a respectful AAC invitation–peer free refusal–child acknowledgment. No favored peer, successful shared play, forced speaking or greeting quota required. Silent side-by-side work without any social contingency is not source. PK4 two distinct aspects: (1) actual real peer interaction; (2) genuine child-authored expression or observed preference for a real person, including interest in playing outside school. Another child's current agreement to share a specific activity is separate from whether the learner has a preference; no classmate owes reciprocal feelings. Repeated actual choices can substantiate a stable relationship but the source does not mandate two real dates or a named 'best friend'. For both: a new unmodeled peer setting supports stronger transfer if truly observed; familiar genuine response still can be source. If there is no actual peer exchange, mark it NOT_OBSERVED; if no evidence of preference PK4, mark preference aspect NOT_OBSERVED, without treating that as social deficit. Only private institutional records may contain identifying facts. No authoring-stage child observation.
