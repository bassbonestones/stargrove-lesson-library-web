Distinct source meanings and safeguarding principles

PK3: “Child interacts with peers and may have preferred friends.” May is explicitly permissive. A three-year-old interacting meaningfully with willing REAL child peers is source-aligned even without a stable favorite, exclusive play partner, named best friend, peer invitation success or willingness to publicly label another child. Age-safe parallel play can be a legitimate developmental pathway, but I.C.7 interaction evidence needs an observed peer-contingent child invitation/response, shared attention, meaningful joint activity, gesture, sign, home language or AAC; mere side-by-side presence without social response is not interaction. Do not substitute an adult, avatar, forced group chorus or arranged play for actual freely willing peer context. PK3 does NOT need to demonstrate established best-friend preference.

PK4: “Child interacts with peers and has preferred friends.” The preference must be genuinely child-expressed/observed, not merely assigned seating, a greeting or teacher claim. A real child can genuinely prefer a peer without that peer sharing the feeling; both must freely consent to each actual social activity. Authentic repeated natural interactions increase confidence in a stable preferred friendship, but official I.C.7 does not mandate two distinct dates to recognize child-origin preference evidence. 'Has preferred friends' does not mean exclusive clique, mandatory best friend count, public list, compulsory emotional disclosure, permanent fixed friendship, peer's obligation to reciprocate, rejection of others or a social status competition. Teacher recognizes authentic real preferred peer and meaningful interactions privately and accepts that child preferences may shift. Without natural accessible real opportunities, flag NOT_OBSERVED rather than labeling a child as socially impaired.

Safeguarding: No publicly ranked friendship charts, votes for favorite classmate, forced buddy assignments, engineered exclusion/rejection, bullying or coercion of a child into unwanted social contact. Distinguish child preference from another's independent consent. Protect access to learning activities and respect children who play independently, need breaks, are neurodiverse, communicate by sign/AAC or make different choices. Adults address bullying and exclusion; do not force the child to name a special friend or reveal private peer feelings. Cultural, family, language, sensory, mobility and individualized IFSP/IEP/504 supports are integral. This outcome differs from I.C.1 general positive relationships, I.C.3 ability to initiate social contacts and I.C.4 parallel/associative/cooperative play structures: I.C.7 emphasizes actual peer interactions and emerging authentic preference for particular real peers, not general task or play completion.

Teaching and source: ENTRY real first child choice before unrelated consenting adult-owned MODEL; DURING real uncoerced peer learning with protected partners, EXIT different unmodeled safe actual peer activity and real child choice, REVISIT genuinely later different calendar day (NOT_OBSERVED until performed). For PK4 a true child-owned expressed preferred-peer interest is source-relevant alongside actual real peer interaction; natural separate real occasions can strengthen a distinct stable-relationship claim but are not an official number-of-dates requirement. Never fabricate or impose a quota. Any synthetic source candidate is not actual observed mastery or diagnosis.

PK3 four responsive stages — actual peers, preferred friends optional

PK3 F3-1 — Interact safely with an actual willing peer in ordinary play

ENTRY: At a real accessible peer activity, observe a child-owned peer-directed gesture, invitation or meaningful response and the peer’s freely chosen genuine reply, including “not now”; mutual assent is required only to continue actual shared play, not to recognize the brief child/peer exchange. Do not stage rejection tests. MODEL: Two unrelated consenting adults on teacher-owned material model mutually respectful optional conversation without forcing a friend label. DURING: Child contributes to a genuinely contingent real peer exchange or shared attention/activity through personally chosen gesture/sign/AAC/words or response; quiet independent parallel proximity without any social interaction is not enough. Peer may decline extended play; real respectful invitation–decline–acknowledgment remains a brief human interaction. EXIT: New different real safe ordinary peer play context; actual reciprocal interaction noted individually. No preferred friend required. REVISIT: Actually later distinct date real safe peer interest and interaction, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 F3-2 — Participate with different peers without having to choose favorites

ENTRY: Observe child freely responding to or inviting safe play with another willing real peer without mandatory name/choice declaration. MODEL: Adult separately demonstrates respecting two different colleagues' choices, not naming a 'best friend'. DURING: Offer distinct voluntary activity options; children can work with any willing peer or separately; no social ranking or compulsion. EXIT: Unmodeled different real willing peer encounter with real child-owned responsive interaction, regardless of preferred relationship. REVISIT: Truly later date different natural peer interaction, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 F3-3 — Show optional authentic emerging peer preference without pressure

ENTRY: Observe only naturally volunteered child choice to revisit a familiar willing peer, if it occurs, and ordinary interaction with real peers. MODEL: Adult models personal comfort in different consenting adult setting and says preferring a collaborator doesn't exclude others. DURING: Teacher supports child's natural interest in one or more peers, honors peers saying no and does not insist every child select special friend. EXIT: Different genuine free peer interaction, learner choice documented privately; absence of preferred friend is normal PK3 source-consistent. REVISIT: Actual later date with real consenting peer and chosen activity, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 F3-4 — Transfer respectful real peer interactions across normal contexts

ENTRY: Observe child safe natural peer interactions with accessible options and independently consenting peers; may or may not show a preference. MODEL: On unrelated adult-only art demonstration, teachers model having different willing partners without compulsion. DURING: Child interacts through meaningful age-appropriate own choice, home language/sign/AAC/gesture, optional quiet and appropriate adult support. EXIT: Fresh unlike real voluntary peer interaction, not an assigned buddy, adult-only roleplay or forced special-friend label. REVISIT: Genuinely later date natural new real peer interaction, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 five responsive stages — actual freely preferred reciprocal friends

PK4 F4-1 — Interact meaningfully with familiar real willing peers

ENTRY: Normal real classroom peer-interest area, child and peer choose whether to play/share activity; observe without adult-selected friend assignment. MODEL: Adult demonstrates on entirely different own materials how two adults may prefer working together without making anyone else inferior. DURING: Child freely contributes to an authentic peer interaction with reciprocal choice, no forced greeting, exclusion or permanent friend labels. EXIT: New real safe familiar peer context, child's own meaningful interaction, peer actual choice and no compulsory group preference. REVISIT: True later date real peer encounter, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 F4-2 — Notice self-selected shared interest with certain peers

ENTRY: Across ordinary real activities, learner may naturally select an actual willing peer with familiar shared interest, unlike teacher-assigned seating. MODEL: Adults on separate benign own topic show freely repeated collaborator choice and both can choose different task. DURING: Teacher observes real common interests and accessible communication over actual occasions without requiring public 'best friend' declaration. EXIT: New naturally occurring child/peer choice and interaction with actual reciprocal partner, document preference as tentative actual pattern, not one label. REVISIT: Actually later different calendar-day peer choice and reciprocal response, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 F4-3 — Recognize genuine child-selected preferred peers without requiring an exact date count

ENTRY: Notice actual spontaneously expressed preference for real peer, even when stable-relationship history is not yet available; no other peer owes a reciprocal preference. Record any actually observed real peer interaction separately. MODEL: Teacher models with consenting adults how preferences can change and neither colleague owes anyone companionship. DURING: Real peer preferences are child-owned and other child free to reciprocate or decline; teacher privately records actual repeated choices/comfort contexts. EXIT: A changed real safe encounter may probe transfer only if actually observed; document genuine child-selected peer-interest expression and real mutually contingent interaction separately. Prior recurring voluntary relationships can strengthen evidence of enduring friendship but their absence is not an automatic new-date source failure. REVISIT: Truly later natural date real preferred peer free choice, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 F4-4 — Respect friendships while welcoming other peers' dignity

ENTRY: Actual learner and preferred peer may choose shared project; observe how children each maintain choice, privacy and welcoming environment. MODEL: Unrelated adults model being glad to work with someone while not excluding or ranking others. DURING: Teach boundaries, nonexclusivity, respect for classmates who choose other activities, and no forced buddy/secret social hierarchy. EXIT: Fresh actual peer-selected real activity with freely mutual reciprocal interaction and honest preferred-person pattern over real contexts. REVISIT: Later actual date natural different interaction, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 F4-5 — Transfer real peer interaction with authentic preferred friend choice

ENTRY: Observe genuinely naturally occurring peer interactions and actual freely chosen preferred peers across normal distinct class situations. MODEL: Adults model nonexclusive consented repeated working relationship on other activity without naming child friends. DURING: Offer meaningful child-owned accessible real opportunities to revisit preferred willing peer or choose new peers, with respectful consent and no public ranking. EXIT: Different real unmodeled peer encounter and authentic preference supported by prior real actual varied occasions; receiver may decline safely. REVISIT: Genuinely later date with actual peer choices, consent and meaningful interaction, NOT_OBSERVED.

Three substantively different instructional methods

Method 01: Real safe freely chosen peer interaction without compelled partner labeling (TEACHER_GUIDE.md). Method 02: Genuine voluntary repeated shared interests with actual independently chosen willing peers over naturally different real occasions (LESSON_02_SHARED_INTEREST_AND_REVISITING_PEERS.md). Method 03: Authentic preferred friend choice coupled with reciprocal boundaries, privacy and classroom inclusion without public ranking or forced friendship (LESSON_03_FRIENDSHIP_CHOICE_AND_INCLUSION.md). No method treats named classmates as collectible rewards.

Four FULL WRITTEN serious learning modes — authentic source not faked

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future independent child-only application can offer meaningful original accessible narrated virtual interactions, choices about fictional peer interests and boundaries, child-controlled sign/AAC/gesture, ability to return to virtual characters with evolving safe reciprocal responses and free choice to skip. These can teach friendship concepts without an adult operating the tablet but fictional avatar is NOT a willing REAL classmate, no actual PK3/PK4 peer relationship or genuine preferred friend established by repeated icon selection. Official I.C.7 source remains NOT_OBSERVED until genuine human peer interaction; working app, child usability/privacy and equivalent effectiveness unverified.

CO-PLAY: Caregiver and child use full original first-party choice story and accessible normal shared art/book for meaningful peer-relationship understanding. Where a genuinely willing REAL child peer naturally participates, learner may interact voluntarily, adult respects both peers' right to decline and observes any genuine recurring choice without labeling. If only parent-child session, PK3 and PK4 real PEERS source is NOT_OBSERVED, even though teaching is substantial. No parent scripts a child to pick a best friend or demands public preferred-peer declaration.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Educator can fully teach a child about voluntary peer interaction, partner preferences, consent and inclusion with original safe story and an unrelated adult-owned role model, and build real accessible willingness to respond. One-to-one adult-only session cannot prove real PEER interaction or actual preferred friend. If freely willing actual classmates meet later, teacher privately documents real child/peer interaction; PK4 genuine child preference can be indicated in one actual unforced expression; stronger established friendship needs credible natural context, not invented dates, forced peer agreement or a teacher badge. Unavailable source is NOT_OBSERVED; later-date REVISIT cannot be prefilled.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Teacher provides actual inclusive voluntary real child-peer learning stations with duplicate large safe materials and quiet access, genuine learner choices and independent peer consent. Observe individual child interactions and reciprocal responses; PK3 no special friend necessary. PK4 may genuinely self-select peers over actual varied occasions, documented in private protected observations without friend charts, votes, exclusions or compulsory selection. A peer who declines is not a test-failure, and teachers prevent bullying while preserving children's freedom to choose play partners. New truly unmodeled EXIT and later-date REVISIT documented only if they actually occur.
