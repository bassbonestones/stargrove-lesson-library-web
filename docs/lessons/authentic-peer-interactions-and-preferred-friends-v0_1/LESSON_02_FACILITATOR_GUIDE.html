Lesson 02 companion facilitator guide — Shared interest over real occasions

Pair: Lesson 02; sources PK3.I.C.7 and PK4.I.C.7. Consult objective guide; this guide does not count as a plan.

Pedagogical distinction and preflight

Naturally recurring shared interest is a window into relationship choice, not a scripted survey. Prepare accessible optional story/art interests with duplicates across ordinary separate days, quiet space, trusted safeguards and independent willing peers. Do not set a friend-frequency quota, arrange the same seat every day, require names, record an actual child's friendship in this repository or manufacture repeated dates. PK3 can legitimately have meaningful peer interaction without stable preferences; PK4 actual child-owned peer preference is distinct from a STRONGER naturally recurring relationship history; no requirement that the other peer mirror personal preference, only freely agree to any specific continued activity. Do not conflate enjoying an activity with preference for whoever staff placed nearby.

Adult sequence

ENTRY: Observe authentic willing-peer interaction or free independent choice during a normal activity; record the real date only in approved private school documentation, never invent observations in curriculum. MODEL: Two consenting adults on unrelated art demonstrate NOW that each may collaborate or work separately without shame, then explain a HYPOTHETICAL later different choice. This is not an actual dated child-peer interaction or observed relationship history. DURING: Offer safe activity choices at real natural opportunities as they arise. Allow child to return to an interest/peer or change; support original AAC/sign/home-language requests and another peer's freedom to accept/decline. For practice, use fictional examples but label them teaching, not real preference evidence. FADING: Remove adult's preferred buddy suggestions and scheduled adjacency. Keep necessary unbiased access assistance. EXIT: On a naturally occurring familiar or changed real peer occasion, PK3.I.C.7 may show genuine child-owned respectful peer interaction even with no identifiable preferred friend; a sincere child invitation–peer freely declined further play–child acknowledgment is still a brief real exchange, although not continuing shared play. PK4.I.C.7 needs genuine real peer interaction AND child-origin authentic preferred-peer expression/choice; a privately expressed preference from one real occasion may be source-relevant, while repeated freely chosen real occasions support this METHOD'S STRONGER stable-relationship inference but are not a publisher-imposed two-date gate. Another peer must freely consent to continuing play but need not feel the same preference. Do not demand a named favorite or score no opportunity as failure. A familiar self-chosen human exchange may be source; changed natural EXIT supports stronger transfer only when actually observed. REVISIT: New actual calendar date and new willing peer choice, NOT_OBSERVED until it happens.

Diagnostic response and learner differences

A learner returns only because teacher always assigns them: no evidence of relationship agency; offer true choice later. Peer declines: protect both parties and offer alternative without rejecting child. One child signals preference via AAC but adult preloaded peer name: validate the actual learner-selected meaning, never attribute adult-authored content. Child selects quiet activities: honor it and offer natural future opportunities. Attention/executive: short clear choices, fade station cards; communication: personally composed accessible meaning; literacy: no name-reading; numeracy: no popularity counts; autism/sensory: quiet familiar spaces and opt-out; developmental: adult-only mutually willing choices modeled before new contexts; physical/motor/hearing/vision: safe accessible co-activity and neutral child-directed proxy with individualized specialist assessment.

Four truthful written routes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: repeating a virtual avatar visit is not authentic human preferred friendship. CO-PLAY: caregiver can discuss previous choices, but only actual willing peers establish real source. ADULT-LED: adult can support safe opportunities, not stage a preferred friend. CLASSROOM: supervise all children with staffing that actually exists, while privately documenting only a feasible subset of individually witnessed child-peer exchanges, actual child-owned preference and peer's consent. Other children remain in genuinely supervised quiet/parallel choices; defer/rotate as needed and mark unwitnessed source NOT_OBSERVED. Never fabricate 24 individual observations from group attendance, assigned seating or a public ranking. Genuine later REVISIT, child observations, educator approval, functional SOLO and four-mode parity are all NOT VERIFIED in this corpus.

2026-11-03 focused method-diversity calibration addendum

CALIBRATION: NATURAL_LONGITUDINAL_RECIPROCAL_PREFERENCE (Method 2)

Actual learning action: Observe whether the learner repeatedly CHOOSES to seek the company of one or more freely willing real peers across genuinely separate ordinary occasions, possibly through recurring common interests. PK3 may have no preferred friend; For a STRONGER repeated voluntary preferred-association inference, observe actual dated naturally distinct child-owned choices in this method; authentic real peer interaction and privately child-expressed preference may be source-relevant without an official time-separated visit quota or partner's mirrored emotion.

Child-facing invitation and preparation: Maintain multiple freely open activity choices across days with equitable accessible materials and quiet/solo alternatives; do not place the same two children together to manufacture a pattern. Invite general material exploration, not 'go back to your friend'. Child may change interests and relationships. Any continuing shared activity must be freely chosen by BOTH children, but the learner's personal preference does not require the peer to feel the same; friendship may change.

Mechanism-specific contingent teaching: If recorded repeated co-location came from seat assignments, treat it as biased observation, remove imposed pair design, and create ordinary unconstrained choice opportunities. If a peer declines or the learner picks someone else, respect both; never coax the child to sustain one pair for proof. Teacher helps learners notice available shared interests, not choose a friend for them.

Source-valid exit/evidence interpretation: For PK4, distinguish actual observation timestamps, accessible choices available, child-initiated approach or response, freely willing peer participation, and cross-occasion regularity. A scripted greeting, three adult prompts, teacher-appointed buddy and intended future visit do not prove established preferred friendship; a genuine single child-origin statement/gesture about a preferred real peer is preference evidence, not longitudinal relationship proof. Do not store identifiable friendship ranks in repository. Unobserved genuine later dates remain NOT_OBSERVED; SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Adversarial positive/negative contrast: Repeated time-separated child-origin willingness toward the same peer can justify cautious inference if alternatives and assent are real; a teacher seating two children together five days has NO such warrant. Do not require exclusive friendship or a specific count.

Cross-method non-equivalence and safety: Mechanism is recognizing and responding to emergent LONGITUDINAL child-owned peer connection, not repeating an identical activity as a 'second method' or staging a school-day recheck. In contrast to Method 03, it does not create conflict to test boundaries.

Honesty: This is a proposed pedagogical design clarification; not a child observation, qualified external educator assessment, validated implementation, or awarded score. The source-specific original developmental sequence remains required.
