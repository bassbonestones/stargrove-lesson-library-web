Comparing Lengths & Heights · original teaching resource

Length and height: objective-level educator reference

This uncounted family guide is not another accepted lesson. Start with Lesson 01 direct comparison and its facilitator, or consult developmental choices. Later methods explore indirect comparison (not yet in reviewed library) and misleading layouts (not yet in reviewed library); their historical full plans are not newly qualified by the direct-comparison revision.

Source and choice

PK3.V.D.1: “Child understands that lengths of objects can vary and be compared” (539de8787e9668b6a8c5c85df2bb2217e38f5cc1ddc36fd472f8216fe0849b07). PK4.V.D.1: “Child recognizes and compares heights or lengths of people or objects” (d4016632bef8e4101f95defad4c5f53a1bf6d111afb729b958ba21275572461e). The original 2022 TEA PDF page 55 has these separate columns. The text introduces comparison before units. Height OR length is genuine: no body ranking, ruler reading, mandatory novelty, first-response success or stage quota. Direct comparison is an accessible starting route; indirect transfer and illusions are optional challenges selected by readiness and interest.

What makes a comparison fair

For endpoint comparison of straight strips, match starting points and direction. For box height, use a common base and name whether a support is part of the target. Equal extents can be equal despite width/color differences. A valid alternative comparison method is welcome. Teacher arrangement is distinct from teacher-supplied relational answer. Record the actual child's recognition/comparison and support, including self-correction or partial evidence, instead of equating independence with unaided motor execution.

Teach and observe proportionately

Invite an actual question; model reasoning when useful; let children choose, explore, rest or decline. Respond to offset starts by aligning, width confusion by contrasting dimensions, and inaccessible stimuli by repairing access. Use familiar or different safe objects; another pair may help distinguish comparison from echo but is not compulsory. A later revisit is recorded only when it happens. No source-mastery cutoff is defined here.

One adult with one child can follow a chosen path/bridge question. One teacher with a group keeps the comparison area visible, observes one child's contribution while others have familiar safe activities, and defers observations when supervision requires it. No class-wide inference from chorus or partner product. Broad blunt stable materials, no cords/climbing, consent-based touch, familiar communication and no public body comparisons protect safety and dignity. Private notes need only the actual arrangement, action, support, inference limit and next invitation.

Historical sibling instructions still need dedicated applied review; this shared guide does not waive their defects or qualify them. SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED. No observed child outcomes, actual later retention, tested classroom effectiveness or physical-print field validity are claimed.

For direct length comparison without sight or touch, see the current neutral endpoint-sound option. Confirm perceivable locations before asking a relation; do not supply it or score an inaccessible or declined opportunity.

For direct PK3 evidence, an attributable same-length comparison can show comparability, not variation by itself. Keep the relation actually shown and any separately witnessed independent unequal comparison distinct, and do not use listening practice, adult modeling or unknown data as an unequal-length observation. No extra trial required.
