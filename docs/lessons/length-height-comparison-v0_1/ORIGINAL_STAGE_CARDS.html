01 · Physical comparison: align starts

Original age-safe measuring-concept teaching examples · PK3.V.D.1 lengths vary / PK4.V.D.1 object height OR length comparison · NOT student outcomes

TEACHER EXAMPLE — direct fair compare

Both large safe strip models begin at the same left baseline; the upper extends farther. Meaning: actual lengths differ and CAN be compared, not that a color is “long.”

CHILD-OWNED BLANK COMPARISON SURFACE
Use safe broad blunt strips. Child may move pieces, direct a helper, or compare adult-aligned pieces. Note who supplied the comparison, not just who arranged them.

PK3 recognizes lengths may differ or be equal and compares. PK4 recognizes/compares actual height OR length; no rulers/metric-unit mastery mandatory. Adult examples teach; familiar materials may also support a child-owned comparison.

02 · Blank height and bridge comparison

Original age-safe measuring-concept teaching examples · PK3.V.D.1 lengths vary / PK4.V.D.1 object height OR length comparison · NOT student outcomes

MODEL TOWERS — common base

Only compare actual height from same floor/tabletop baseline. Stable safe model objects, never child bodies.

CHILD SOURCE HEIGHT CHALLENGE
Place low stable boxes on the same base. Child may indicate taller/shorter/equal using familiar communication; record actual support.
NEW REFERENCE STRIP (indirect comparison)
For physically separated objects, use ONE broad non-stretching strip. Align same BEGINNING at both objects; transfer unchanged far endpoint. Children choose positioning.

Safe low model towers only, no sharp ruler, recoiling tape, climbing or forced comparisons of children's bodies. A transferred reference is a teaching route, not official compulsory tool.
