Comparing Lengths & Heights · original teaching resource

Facilitate Start Together

Read the full lesson for the exact PK3.V.D.1 and PK4.V.D.1 source boundaries and optional developmental choices. The mechanism is fair direct comparison of actual safe object lengths, with optional PK4 heights. It is not a test of ruler reading, first responses or novel objects.

Before children arrive

Prepare four wide, rigid, blunt cardboard strips, one clearly unequal pair and one equal pair, on a reachable flat surface. Cut only during adult preparation, check edges and avoid small loose pieces, cords, elastic tape or unstable towers. Safe flat books can substitute. Set up high contrast or a tactile common edge as appropriate; ask about touch preferences and familiar communication signals. No printer is required. Optional print materials demonstrate relationships and offer a blank record, not a standardized score sheet.

ADULT-LED / CO-PLAY: one adult and one child

Invite “Which will reach across your pretend path? How can we find out?” Let the child choose, change the purpose, watch or stop. Observe before deciding whether to teach. To model, match the starting ends and trace to the far ends: “From the same start, this goes farther.” Then let the child compare, move pieces or direct you. Already aligned pieces are valid for a child-owned relational judgment. Equal pieces may be called equal; do not force longer/shorter. Another valid physical method is welcome.

If position fools the child, align and look again. If width fools them, contrast a narrow long and broad short strip. If access or communication is unclear, repair access before interpreting knowledge. Do not shame a guess or erase a later correction. A different pair can clarify whether the child compared rather than echoed; unfamiliarity is not a compulsory gate.

CLASSROOM: one teacher with a group

Keep the comparison space in the same visible area as the whole group, with familiar safe activities for children waiting or choosing not to join. Briefly model; let pairs explore if safe. Observe one focal child's words/actions while others continue visible play. If supervision requires attention, stop recording and supervise. Shared kits and deferred observations are legitimate; no second adult is assumed. Do not infer every child's understanding from a chorus, correct peer product or shared bridge. Let children opt out, watch or return without penalty.

What to retain

No sight or touch, if wanted: choose one safe straight comparison lane on a tabletop. On its shared start and two clearly different practice points, use one gentle sound and check the child can tell which point lies farther along that lane in either reporting order. For actual length, put A and then B on the same exact lane and starting point one at a time, identify the item and mark its real far end with the same sound while the listener stays put. Repeat either actual placement on request, never using separate sideways lanes, louder/longer sounds or the answer. If relevant practice locations can't be distinguished or a sound is unwanted, offer other safe accessible comparison or pause; unclear sounds aren't equality. Record actual access support and owned response separately. One adult can do this only if group supervision stays safe.

For PK3 notes, label the actual current result (equal, unequal, or unknown) separately from any distinct witnessed child-owned unequal comparison. One equal judgment can show comparability without showing variation. A listening access check or supported model cannot populate that separate observation. No extra trial is compulsory.

Record date, object/dimension, arrangement, exact child action/words, access support, conceptual prompts and who supplied the comparative answer. PK3 concerns understanding length variation/comparability; PK4 concerns recognizing and comparing height OR length. Teacher setup is not teacher ownership of the answer. Adult-announced answer plus echo is supported practice, not independent comparison. Familiar-object success, supported action, self-correction and ambiguous evidence each deserve honest notes; none is automatically durable mastery. A decline or inaccessible trial means that opportunity was not observed, not that prior evidence vanishes.

Finish after a useful discovery or whenever fatigue, refusal or safety calls for stopping. A later willing revisit can use familiar or different objects; record actual date and keep retention or transfer claims bounded to what occurred. No actual learner observations exist in this authored package. SOLO NOT IMPLEMENTED. Printed dimensions and browser checks cannot prove classroom effectiveness.

Technical phase names: ENTRY is the invitation, MODEL the adult demonstration, DURING supported exploration, EXIT an optional closing observation, and REVISIT a genuinely later willing opportunity. None imposes novelty, first-response success or fixed testing quotas.
