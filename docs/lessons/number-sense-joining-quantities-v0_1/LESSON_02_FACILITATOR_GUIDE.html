Exactly which distinct practical mechanism to teach

Visual before/arrival/after causal investigation and optional learner-authored pictorial reconstruction (or PK4 original verbal/object join), with concrete PK3 physical new-piece action.

Full exact printed research source, separate grade boundaries and evidence

PK3.V.B.1: “Child uses objects to demonstrate that adding one or more objects to a set will increase the number of objects in the set” SHA-256 `de67f5aa50984d3a5242cf9fc182e514833852dcbaf2fa6ddc28b0348ca17ddd`. PK3 USES REAL OBJECTS to DEMONSTRATE adding one OR MORE to a named set increases the NUMBER of objects IN THAT SET. Actual child-controlled addition of members, not teacher moving them, image-only overlay or counting all room items.

PK4.V.B.1: “Child uses objects, pictorial models, and/or a verbal word problem to represent adding up to 5 objects” SHA-256 `80f4a477f00150463d88036dc9302c3692aa841da0fb3c21f2ef219e597bcce4`. PK4 USES OBJECTS, PICTORIAL MODELS AND/OR a VERBAL WORD PROBLEM to REPRESENT ADDING UP TO 5 OBJECTS. Inclusive OR means no mandatory all-three modes; represent actual change with appropriate initial/added/now source rather than just a numeral memorization or teacher-chosen answer.

An exact frozen full research-cell SHA-256 is a source integrity receipt, not proof of raw publisher original PDF two-column transcription: that concordance remains pending. Respect source verbs and any inclusive OR; do not elevate PK3 to PK4 or weaken required PK4 source by using adult/cartoons as evidence.

Original authored method's concrete learner actions and feedback model

ENTRY — independent small changed-scene interpretation before any teaching

Begin with two actual identical large blocks a handspan apart; move the same two to a line without bringing new pieces. Do not say “same”; ask “What changed about what was here?” Child can indicate movement, unchanged quantity or uncertainty. Then separately place one new different-texture block off to the side and ask “What might happen if this one joins them?” Record first untouched prior scene vs fresh item response. PK3: offer the option to concretely choose whether and where it should join; don't supply “more” first. PK4: ask child what three blank frames might show to let absent friend understand what happened; not yet require a formal solution.

Do not give PK3 source credit for naming “three” (unless child also demonstrates new addition and growth). Do not give PK4 source credit for drawing three final marks without two-part joining meaning. If child correctly detects old-object repositioning, that may be relevant precursor, not proof of a new-object join.

MODEL — teacher thinks aloud on a separate example (never used in EXIT)

Teacher-only mat, one large circle piece was already there. Adult takes one genuinely new square piece. Say: “Before: this one was here. I am not just moving it. A new square is arriving from outside. After I put it with the old one, there are more objects. I can show all three parts of that event: what was already here, what arrived, and what is together now.” Adult physically demonstrates on separate teacher mat. On PK4 extension adult draws one circle in the left frame, one square in the middle, and both distinguishably together on the right; adult explicitly states a second possible representation is word problem “One toy was there; another arrived; now both are together.” Avoid “two plus…” equation unless student asks; no mandatory reading.

DURING — guided inquiry with changed scene and corrective turns

Child receives two large original pieces plus one genuinely new textured piece held outside the named group. First gently MOVE THE SAME TWO originals farther apart and nearer together inside that same group; never introduce a third piece or describe two pieces as a triangle. Ask what changed and preserve the child's first response. Then reveal the separate new piece and invite the child to PHYSICALLY move it into the set (PK3), or direct neutral motor assistance without adult deciding which piece arrives, and indicate set growth. For PK4 also invite a personally authored joining representation in any one valid medium, including a verbal/AAC problem that asks a still-unanswered all-together question; no filled final result frame compulsory. Record original response and help source H/U/A/X; if child confuses moved old items with newly arriving members, model that difference on separate adult-owned pieces and fade before a new independent probe.

Method-specific instructional rationale and preparation: Original large first-party before/new arrivals/after picture sequence of safe harmless things, separate authentic oversized physical matching props required for PK3, no answer-filled count frame. This is a benign purposeful safe source, not a decontextualized card matching/timing drill, and must be actually available if the official source requires real objects/people/child movement.

ENTRY — uncoached original child source, context and provenance

At ENTRY first show two actually present large real objects, then move those SAME TWO to different positions inside the named set without any new member; listen to the child's genuinely first noticing, without announcing that count is unchanged. Only NEXT show one separate genuinely new tactile object OUTSIDE the group and invite the child to direct its real arrival and notice whether the named set grew (PK3), or express a child-authored old-plus-incoming joining representation through any ONE PK4-valid medium. Images/blank frames are accessible optional scaffolds, NOT preanswered first evidence.

Document actual child first response (spoken/sign/AAC/meaningful self-directed physical action, as appropriate to printed source), grade, which physical/real-source materials or true willing people were available, and what adult said/did beforehand. If no safe real source or no real learner observation, record NOT_OBSERVED, not false child learning or lack of capability.

MODEL — adult demonstrates on different real owned source

Adult on different teacher-owned unrelated safe figurines sketches their own real earlier/arrival/now group but does not reveal child's picture count.

Never model the exact learner EXIT example or pre-answer with a pointing finger/answer cue. The adult's true demonstration is a teaching event and carries ZERO child independent evidence.

DURING — actual child activity, contingent instructional repair and faded hints

Support child to reconstruct previous members and true arrival in named target group on original drawing; PK3 uses matching real objects to show increase, PK4 chooses valid original picture OR other allowed representation up to five, adult fades pointing to new members.

Track U (original learner decision), H (teacher supplied answer/semantic next move), A (neutral learner-directed motor/communication/sensory accessibility), X (unknown). Respond to partial attempts with source-specific feedback and a completely unrelated adult-owned misconception example, then let learner practice without blame. Fade H cues before new EXIT, retain essential disability and physical safety A access.

EXIT — genuinely different independent source action

Brand-new joining event with real separate incoming items (published PK4 adds up to five objects, not a universal combined-total ceiling): PK3 personally directs actual real new counterpart pieces into named group and indicates increase; PK4 independently authors an authentic old-plus-incoming joining model through ONE of pictures, objects or child-origin verbal/AAC word problem, without prefilled teacher diagram. A genuine PK4 story may conclude with an unresolved “how many now?” question; a completed after-result or numeral is NOT a compulsory extra eligibility condition.

The test is NOT the same adult modeled material, prerecorded answer, outline tracing or identical stimulus shuffled. Record child-owned action independently against each exact printed source ID; an observed age-specific action is a source candidate, not verified developmental mastery.

Adversarial misconception and safe exact re-teaching

Child counts total objects across two unrelated pictured baskets after one moves within same target basket and teacher scores addition; no new membership in named set. Require actual new arrival.

Do not invent child participation, observer agreement, educational outcomes, real peer participants or special educator approval to close documents.

REVISIT — genuinely different actual calendar date

A safe changed source, new learner-first response, provenance and adult assistance must be observed on another real day. At authoring: REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 tagged-display EXIT source clarity: The three already-present large tags on three hooks are the OLD set. A fourth large safe tag joins that named TAG set from OUTSIDE, with available display space (hooks themselves are not the added objects). Child may independently tell/draw/model an old-plus-new addition problem ending in a true unanswered how-many question; no after-count and no physical small metal hooks required.

Seven individually accessible pathways conserving the actual printed skill

Attention/executive: One actual named group and clear genuine arrival with meaningful child-controlled pause. Language/communication: Original before/joined/now description via sign/AAC/home language, no fixed equation words. Literacy/phonology: PK4 valid pictorial OR verbal OR object model, PK3 real physical objects not drawing only. Numeracy: PK3 actual increase of named set, PK4 adding up to five, never new invented arithmetic range or all three modes. Autism/sensory: Large stable soft objects, no small seed props or loud forced counting games. Intellectual/developmental: Adult demonstrates separate real source, faded hints, child-directed true newly added membership at EXIT. Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Neutral learner-directed accessible proxy can move real PK3 objects under child authentic direction, not select amount for them.

Independent source under adapted access: Brand-new joining event with real separate incoming items (published PK4 adds up to five objects, not a universal combined-total ceiling): PK3 personally directs actual real new counterpart pieces into named group and indicates increase; PK4 independently authors an authentic old-plus-incoming joining model through ONE of pictures, objects or child-origin verbal/AAC word problem, without prefilled teacher diagram. A genuine PK4 story may conclude with an unresolved “how many now?” question; a completed after-result or numeral is NOT a compulsory extra eligibility condition. Support may improve how a child acts/perceives/communicates, never invent WHO chose the meaningful answer. Where a true human, real physically performed action or exact sensory source is essential and unavailable, the relevant official source remains NOT_OBSERVED.

Four complete but only WRITTEN routes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: future child-controlled virtual before/arrival/after activity may support PK3 conceptual preparation and potentially PK4 learner-authored pictorial/verbal representation, but pixels do not count as PK3.V.B.1 physical objects. Actual PK3 source evidence still requires individually observed learner-directed REAL oversized new-object joining and indication of growth, with access-only motor support if needed. Legacy app cannot substitute; no functioning current SOLO is claimed. CO-PLAY: caregiver enables actual safe context and access while child controls original meaning/action. ADULT-LED: separate adult MODEL, real scaffolded independent DURING, new changed learner EXIT. Witness only 2–3 focal learners per teacher observation interval and mark all others NOT_OBSERVED until witnessed; a projected teacher model or group chorus is not physical PK3 evidence. PK4 can instead author ONE object, pictorial or verbal/AAC joining representation. Preserve privacy, consent, adult safe supervision and child agency.
