The Garden Gathering

Original teacher story cards • Joining quantities: first two, then three more • PK3/PK4

1. Let's prepare our pretend garden stand.

“We have two big vegetables on our display. Where would you put them?”

Teacher: Put two large pieces on the tray. Let the child choose an arrangement. Count only if it naturally fits.

2. A second basket arrives.

“Here are three more vegetables. We want all our vegetables on the display. What could we do?”

Teacher: Before moving, ask whether there will be more, fewer or the same. Invite the child to bring in the three NEW pieces.

3. Discover the whole collection.

“Our first two were here. Three more joined them. How could we find out how many there are all together?”

Teacher: Let the child lead. Count all together only if useful. Rearrange without adding to contrast moving vs joining.

Alternative garden challenge

“We have one flower on a sign, and three more arrive. What would happen if we put them together?”

Teacher: Reset and use different large shapes; treat this as optional novel transfer, not a quiz or grading instrument.

Preparation/safety: Use large sturdy paper figures (or larger classroom blocks/toy food), adult controls scissors, never small beads or seeds. Children may gesture, use communication aids, speak in home language, direct physical assistance, or pass. No learner records. Educator validation pending.
