Method 03 individual facilitator guide — number-sense-joining-quantities-v0_1

Paired complete instructional plan: LESSON_03_TELL_AND_ACT_JOINING.md. This individual method companion is NOT a standalone additional lesson. TEACHER_GUIDE.md is separate uncounted objective-level educator reference. Substantive original method text and stage-specific child activities remain in paired plan, not replaced by a checklist.

Exactly which distinct practical mechanism to teach

Child-created temporally ordered miniature narrative or physical stage showing who was already present and which new objects arrived.

Full exact printed research source, separate grade boundaries and evidence

PK3.V.B.1: “Child uses objects to demonstrate that adding one or more objects to a set will increase the number of objects in the set” SHA-256 `de67f5aa50984d3a5242cf9fc182e514833852dcbaf2fa6ddc28b0348ca17ddd`. PK3 USES REAL OBJECTS to DEMONSTRATE adding one OR MORE to a named set increases the NUMBER of objects IN THAT SET. Actual child-controlled addition of members, not teacher moving them, image-only overlay or counting all room items.

PK4.V.B.1: “Child uses objects, pictorial models, and/or a verbal word problem to represent adding up to 5 objects” SHA-256 `80f4a477f00150463d88036dc9302c3692aa841da0fb3c21f2ef219e597bcce4`. PK4 USES OBJECTS, PICTORIAL MODELS AND/OR a VERBAL WORD PROBLEM to REPRESENT ADDING UP TO 5 OBJECTS. Inclusive OR means no mandatory all-three modes; represent actual change with appropriate initial/added/now source rather than just a numeral memorization or teacher-chosen answer.

An exact frozen full research-cell SHA-256 is a source integrity receipt, not proof of raw publisher original PDF two-column transcription: that concordance remains pending. Respect source verbs and any inclusive OR; do not elevate PK3 to PK4 or weaken required PK4 source by using adult/cartoons as evidence.

Original authored method's concrete learner actions and feedback model

ENTRY — new first experience, before model

Adult gently offers one broad toy block on a blank stage and a separate new block behind the stage divider. Do not say the answer or act the story first. “This toy is here. You can make a little story. What would you do if one new toy wanted to join it?” Record the child's first authentic move and any indication of new-versus-old, separate from teacher's explanations; no mandatory spoken script. Then on a fresh prompt “What if we only moved this same toy to the other side?” Look for whether child distinguishes repositioning without newcomer.

For PK3 source credit at EXIT, the child must later actually add or child-direct the new object and identify increase; simple ENTRY interest is not mastery. For PK4 the entry is only prerequisite: “How would you tell someone who didn't see these two toys what happened?” A full original joined story is not expected until later staged instruction.

MODEL — original adult-only different situation

Use one paper circle already at a pretend stage and two paper triangles waiting outside, not the learner's first block prompt. Adult says exactly: “There was ONE round shape on the stage. TWO new triangles arrived from somewhere else. I moved the two new pieces in, and I did not secretly remove the first. The group grew. Here is the whole event: something was already there, a different group joined, and now they're together.” Physically enact it. For PK4 add: “If I tell this as a story, I can say ‘one round shape was at our display, and two more shapes arrived and joined it.’ That describes an addition event; just saying ‘three shapes’ would not tell the newcomer part.” Do not repeat the model verbatim at later EXIT, or name the target amount to the child right before the scored task.

A child need not echo the teacher's adult word problem to participate; this is teacher demonstration. When learner is ready, ask what they want to act next. No choreographed song/clapping is a substitute for a new arriving set.

DURING — child authors different story and receives responsive teaching

Offer child a choice of fictional clean toy setting (table, imaginary toy home, school art shelf) and large neutral character blocks. Adult invites: “Your world can be different from mine. Choose one or two of your large safe pieces to be ALREADY HERE on a named toy stage. Decide who or what GENUINELY NEW arrives from outside that group. Show or tell the new arrival, and decide if the original group is still here and what changed when the new pieces joined.” Let learner choose and direct actual REAL object arrival for PK3 and indicate more; PK4 may author an original object, picture or meaningful verbal/AAC joining word problem. Before child responds do NOT choose newcomer or dictate the final story. Ask open follow-up only as guided instruction, record help H versus neutral motor/access A, and use novel unlike EXIT to evaluate independent source.

Method-specific instructional rationale and preparation: Voluntary child-invented harmless short original people-or-toy story and large safe concrete object actors, up to five initial/added for PK4; no copyrighted characters or physical crowded peer demonstrations. This is a benign purposeful safe source, not a decontextualized card matching/timing drill, and must be actually available if the official source requires real objects/people/child movement.

ENTRY — uncoached original child source, context and provenance

Before adult example invite child create a meaningful story with a genuine existing named group then personally introduce new safe toy/paper object(s) to group; record child idea rather than repeat educator phrasing.

Document actual child first response (spoken/sign/AAC/meaningful self-directed physical action, as appropriate to printed source), grade, which physical/real-source materials or true willing people were available, and what adult said/did beforehand. If no safe real source or no real learner observation, record NOT_OBSERVED, not false child learning or lack of capability.

MODEL — adult demonstrates on different real owned source

Adult narrates different adult-owned bookshelf example of new teacher own book joining a shelf set, not learner toy narrative.

Never model the exact learner EXIT example or pre-answer with a pointing finger/answer cue. The adult's true demonstration is a teaching event and carries ZERO child independent evidence.

DURING — actual child activity, contingent instructional repair and faded hints

Support child to distinguish existing group, genuine extra member joining vs same member changing location; allow PK3 real action and PK4 word problem/picture/object mode, fade example text and preserve child original ownership.

Track U (original learner decision), H (teacher supplied answer/semantic next move), A (neutral learner-directed motor/communication/sensory accessibility), X (unknown). Respond to partial attempts with source-specific feedback and a completely unrelated adult-owned misconception example, then let learner practice without blame. Fade H cues before new EXIT, retain essential disability and physical safety A access.

EXIT — genuinely different independent source action

New child-authored story about DIFFERENT harmless objects: PK3 independently actually adds real members to named set and demonstrates increase; PK4 independently gives meaningful original object/picture/verbal addition representation with number of added objects up to five, not scripted repetition.

The test is NOT the same adult modeled material, prerecorded answer, outline tracing or identical stimulus shuffled. Record child-owned action independently against each exact printed source ID; an observed age-specific action is a source candidate, not verified developmental mastery.

Adversarial misconception and safe exact re-teaching

Educator dictates full 'three plus one equals four' story, student recites while no actual child object joining PK3 and no personally made valid representation PK4; novel own group change needed.

Do not invent child participation, observer agreement, educational outcomes, real peer participants or special educator approval to close documents.

REVISIT — genuinely different actual calendar date

A safe changed source, new learner-first response, provenance and adult assistance must be observed on another real day. At authoring: REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 source representation versus optional original-story authorship: A teacher may give the new scarf joining situation with one old and three arriving and ask the child to independently represent that given addition by objects, original picture OR genuinely self-formulated verbal/AAC word problem. Do not mistake merely echoing the exact teacher narration for personal mathematical formulation. A child-generated question “How many now?” may remain unanswered and still represent joining. Separately, a stronger optional creative invention probe may invite the learner to choose a new benign setting, old/incoming quantities and joining words without the adult precomposing a plot; no pupil is failed on the official PK4 source solely for not originating every aspect of an otherwise valid given-problem representation. Track content hints H, independently authored semantic relation U, access-only A and unknown X; the later new source must not reproduce the teacher's solved model.

Seven individually accessible pathways conserving the actual printed skill

Attention/executive: One actual named group and clear genuine arrival with meaningful child-controlled pause. Language/communication: Original before/joined/now description via sign/AAC/home language, no fixed equation words. Literacy/phonology: PK4 valid pictorial OR verbal OR object model, PK3 real physical objects not drawing only. Numeracy: PK3 actual increase of named set, PK4 adding up to five, never new invented arithmetic range or all three modes. Autism/sensory: Large stable soft objects, no small seed props or loud forced counting games. Intellectual/developmental: Adult demonstrates separate real source, faded hints, child-directed true newly added membership at EXIT. Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Neutral learner-directed accessible proxy can move real PK3 objects under child authentic direction, not select amount for them.

Independent source under adapted access: New child-authored story about DIFFERENT harmless objects: PK3 independently actually adds real members to named set and demonstrates increase; PK4 independently gives meaningful original object/picture/verbal addition representation with number of added objects up to five, not scripted repetition. Support may improve how a child acts/perceives/communicates, never invent WHO chose the meaningful answer. Where a true human, real physically performed action or exact sensory source is essential and unavailable, the relevant official source remains NOT_OBSERVED.

Four complete but only WRITTEN routes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: future child-led virtual stage and digital narrator can offer meaningful precursor joining practice, but on-screen actors and digital item IDs cannot replace PK3.V.B.1 actual REAL child-directed physical object adding and shown set increase. PK3 real object evidence would require separate genuine witnessed safe tangible addition under learner control; a child-authored PK4 pictorial/verbal digital scene could eventually be eligible in a tested system, not now. No operational or four-mode efficacy claims are made. CO-PLAY: caregiver enables actual safe context and access while child controls original meaning/action. ADULT-LED: separate adult MODEL, real scaffolded independent DURING, new changed learner EXIT. CLASSROOM: a simultaneous 24-child beginner PK3 one-old/one-new stage needs 48 individually available oversized safe physical objects and private named groups plus separate adult model, or smaller stock safely rotated through groups of 4–6; record only 2–3 actually witnessed child-origin joining/growth acts per teacher interval and mark others NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 may create one personally chosen real-object, pictorial or verbal/AAC joining representation without compulsory public performance, not a group chorus or adult-authored puppet script. Real supervision, permission, child agency and safe materials are necessary written duties. These written plans do not claim real-world child learning, independent educator/specialist acceptance, completed real later-day practice, field printing, or four-mode functional/effective equivalence.
