Joining Quantities — developmental teaching from novice PK3 to representational PK4

Mandatory instructional sequence for 3 distinct original methods, two grades, four delivery routes. This is a curriculum-first, universe-neutral E2.2 INTERNAL AUTHOR-REVIEWED DRAFT created October 30, 2026. It is a genuinely multi-session teaching progression, not a one-session all-numbers test. Child and educator validation NOT_OBSERVED; SOLO complete pathway NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Official source text and limits

PK3.V.B.1: “Child uses objects to demonstrate that adding one or more objects to a set will increase the number of objects in the set” (published original source-cell SHA-256 de67f5aa50984d3a5242cf9fc182e514833852dcbaf2fa6ddc28b0348ca17ddd). This asks for child-conducted or authentically child-directed physical addition of genuinely new items AND showing the set has increased. Merely choosing “more” verbally without showing addition, or repeating “three,” does not independently establish this objective. The child need not report an exact combined numeral for PK3.V.B.1.

PK4.V.B.1: “Child uses objects, pictorial models, and/or a verbal word problem to represent adding up to 5 objects” (SHA-256 80f4a477f00150463d88036dc9302c3692aa841da0fb3c21f2ef219e597bcce4). A child independently represents two sets joining with at least one permitted medium: concrete objects OR a meaningful original pictorial model OR an intelligible verbal word problem. Do not invent an “all three media” prerequisite. A correct memorized sum alone is not a joining representation. Text-interpretation caveat: “adding up to 5 objects” does not literally say “the total can never exceed five.” Our staged introductory tasks intentionally choose combined totals of 2–5 to manage novice cognitive load; we do not construe that teaching choice as a normative definition or claim complete coverage of every possible five-object incoming group. Educator and exact printed guideline review remain necessary before full source-equivalence or local assessment claims.

Related but distinct actions: numeral reading, count-word order, one-to-one cardinality, subitizing and subtraction. Do not auto-credit. A correct child-produced group count is useful optional evidence but not by itself proof of a modeled joining operation.

Stage ladder: real teaching across several actual encounters

Short suggested sessions (5–12 minutes, freely adjusted): one stage may be repeated, children can explore higher values without being forced, and lesson use is responsive to demonstrated prerequisite understanding and access instead of rigid birthday thresholds. Each stage starts with a fresh ENTRY before teaching, explicit teacher MODEL on a different example, child-originated DURING supported practice, a new uncued stage EXIT mini-check, and an actual subsequent-date REVISIT planned not recorded. Never reuse an adult-modeled item or the final full-grade exit as the immediate practice card and still call it new. Each method below states its own role-specific delivery.

Stage Teacher language, authentic child action Formative first-response evidence and correction
PK3 J3-1 — New arrival versus moving same pieces ENTRY show 1 large safe object in a tray and another outside: “Something is here. What could change if a different new one arrives?” MODEL using different two cloth pieces: “This was already here. Now I put a new one in. We have more.” DURING child adds/directs a fresh new item to an original one, without demand to count exact total. New two-piece context, child must choose/direct actually new item and show increase in concrete set (point, sign, AAC or safe tactile comparison). If they relocate the same first item, adult contrasts moved vs added on another object set; helped correction does not become an independent EXIT.
PK3 J3-2 — More than one piece can arrive ENTRY show 1 original plus 2 new safe large pieces. MODEL a different 1+1 arrival, contrasting moving old pieces only. DURING: “Now you can bring these two new ones. Does adding change how many are here?” Child chooses and moves or directs both incoming objects. Fresh 2 old + 1 new in altered arrangement (when not previously practiced). Record separate new-item construction and growth awareness. Counting only the new piece is an optional counting gap, not an automatic failure of joining. Correct via model on unrelated set and retry later.
PK3 J3-3 — Growth across shapes/context ENTRY give 2 existing large toy pieces and 2 genuinely new pieces in another basket. MODEL separate 1+2 problem if not previously shown; DURING can now use familiar garden 2+3 only as an optional more complex teacher-led example, never initial PK3 lesson. Physically rearrange same pieces without additions and invite child to notice difference. Independent new unmodeled source EXIT: two large toy cars + one new car on an unrelated shelf; child demonstrates adding a new item increases set. Later-date REVISIT using different props, such as toy houses or blocks. Do not demand a numeric total, and do not claim success if adult physically and conceptually did the join.
PK4 J4-1 — Retrieve real new-object joining ENTRY ask child to choose what is old/new in a simple unfamiliar 1+1 with no adult modeling, then re-teach as needed. MODEL 1+2 new objects with separate scene; DURING contrast genuine new pieces and mere rearrangements. Ask “If someone did not see this, how could you show or tell them what changed?” Child can use one accessible representation to distinguish old and new groups in different novel 1+1 or 1+2 context. If child simply guesses “two” without evidence of actual joining, demonstrate on other numbers then fresh retry.
PK4 J4-2 — From objects to child-authored representational choice ENTRY child sees fresh 2+1 object example and chooses how to show the event. MODEL adult sketches an unrelated 1+1 before/after (separate old/incoming sets and together). DURING child either manipulates real pieces, draws own pictorial model showing old, arriving and now together, or authors a meaningful original verbal/AAC joining word problem that identifies old group plus genuinely new incoming group and may end with an unanswered “how many now?” question. No completed result or after pane is compulsory for a genuine word problem. Do not force pencil use or English speech. On new 1+2 or 2+2 scene (unpracticed in that context) child authors a two-group join, not merely the final three or four marks. Missing joining relation: adults demonstrate a different pair and ask child to show what moved/arrived on next fresh check.
PK4 J4-3 — Varied small joins to five in instructional examples ENTRY observe child represent unmodeled 2+2 on new scene; MODEL use different 1+3 with teacher's own medium; DURING invite child to represent 2+3, 3+1, 1+4 as personally chosen objects/picture/verbal story, with safe large resources. Explain “two parts joining” without requiring formal + or = signs. Fresh 3+2 or 1+4 novel object identities (when not taught), child shows old set, incoming set and their joining. A child who says “five” from an adult voice or past memory without modeling parts has R=NOT_OBSERVED (representation); counting whole set is not substitution.
PK4 J4-4 — Novel independent representation and revisit ENTRY teacher checks changed-context two-group joining and records child's chosen medium; any missing precursor is reteached. MODEL/DURING occur only on separately reserved source practice items. Child may create original picture, story or object sequence, never be forced to switch all modalities for “full credit.” Uncoached full-source-intended EXIT on new context such as 3 on a book table + 1 new, where child independently represents the event; different real-day REVISIT on unfamiliar clothing pegs or toy seating with new numbers/context. Record initial old/new relation, medium, help/access, valid interpretation, or NOT_OBSERVED. Don't claim normative complete coverage solely from one five-total example.

Developmental sufficiency (instructional only): Advance teaching variety after child can identify genuinely new incoming objects across changed circumstances (PK3) or can independently express the joining relationship in a chosen medium (PK4), without equating an early correct first answer with durable skill. No invented pass percentage, speed demand, diagnosis, mandatory daily dose, or demand to master every numerical combination before exploring another. A child can decline and return later; consent/privacy rules apply.

Learning-differences stage supports and unmodeled checks (November 1, 2026)

PK3 requires genuine child-directed physical arrival of distinct new objects and demonstrated increase; PK4 allows independently authored concrete, pictorial OR verbal joining relation. A motor proxy may place a specifically child-selected real new object; it must not choose which pieces arrive. Verbal storytelling is a valid PK4 medium when the child authors its mathematical relation, including through AAC/sign/home language. Do not demand that PK3 give a numeral total or that PK4 use all three media. A wholly adult-authored answer, accessible but prefilled scene or inaccessible presentation is never independent source proof.

Fresh 1+1 physical old/new arrangement, child adds genuinely absent item and communicates growth via own access mode, not just saying two.

New 3+2 or other unseen small combination, child produces meaningful old/new/join relation, correct answer only is insufficient.
PK4 J4-4 Vary material/theme and chosen representation; keep child-controlled access but remove template and hint while preparing novel and actual later-day tasks. Independent new two-part join in one source-valid medium; log first child-owned representation, original-group/new-group event and later true revisit.

Instruction versus assessment: Two-bin organizers, raised-before/after frames, predictable narration, child-choice breaks and AAC word banks support learning. When the adult supplies the correct new item, creates the joining story or draws the target relation, that trial is conceptual HELP, not independent achievement. Retain essential sensory or motor access when fading hints. Learner-specific IEP/IFSP/504 and trained educator/specialist guidance remain required for actual implementation, and refusal/inaccessibility is not a mathematical fail. PK4 staged examples with totals up to five do NOT establish an official total cap; seek authoritative source interpretation before any strong full-outcome coverage claim.

Implement the same stages through three different teaching mechanisms

Method 01 — Garden Delivery (TEACHER_GUIDE.md): physical old/basket/new delivery

  • PK3 J3-1: Begin with one vegetable already on a tray and one genuinely new large toy vegetable in a delivery basket; adult's separate MODEL uses generic blocks, not the exit toy. Child delivers the new toy and shows group growth. J3-2: Deliver two distinct new toy items to a one-item stand; notice that moving the original two pieces within tray is not delivery. J3-3: Teacher can now present original garden 2+3 only as optional challenge; final independent growth EXIT uses a different household toy/changed location, not the 1+2 model or garden kit answer.
  • PK4 J4-1: Check what was already at garden and what came from an unseen delivery, then let child convey it in their chosen method. J4-2: Child demonstrates an old-group basket and new-group basket joining by actual objects OR an independently drawn/verbally told garden event. J4-3: Stage 2+3 using the original first-party garden kit, but record PK4 source-critical R separately from original ancillary T (all-together quantity). J4-4: New fictional toy shelf/fruit cart problem, fully original child action in one permitted medium and genuine later-date unfamiliar revisit; no answer before raw action.
  • False positive: Child says “five” when adult points to original 2+3 example, or drags an old piece to a new position. Neither establishes self-owned joining representation or PK3 physical set increase. Correct by separating old/new physically on a different set and reattempting a novel case.

Method 02 — Before/After Picture Detectives (LESSON_02_BEFORE_AFTER_PICTURE_DETECTIVES.md): visual evidence/reconstruction, not object delivery

  • PK3 J3-1: Child uses real large loose tokens in front of original BEFORE / ARRIVAL / AFTER picture frames; the paper panels have no preset quantity or “more” answer. Child places new token into after group and confirms more. J3-2: Child inspects a picture pair where old pieces were merely relocated; reconstructs with actual pieces that nothing new arrived, then creates a genuinely new 1+2 joining on clear mat. J3-3: Fresh unseen picture context, child makes the physical change with actual pieces and explains/points to growth. Selecting the “after” print alone is not PK3 physical action.
  • PK4 J4-1: Reconstruct a simple 1+1 into a first-party paper before/arrival/after board; child may choose objects, draw own marks, or tell own words. J4-2: Child draws or selects actual blank partitions then authors 1+2 with old/incoming/show-joined relation, not copies solved page. J4-3: Stage unfamiliar 2+3 and a varied non-garden 1+4 on blank picture windows; final before/after must show two sets joined, not only a final five-dot picture. J4-4: Novel coat-pegs or library-book scenario with changed medium/context and a later date revisit.
  • False positive: Child circles a preprinted row of five dots after adult says “five” and adult credits PK4 adding. It has no visible joining event or child-authored content. Reconstruct different two-part event, then fresh blank paper.

Method 03 — Tell-It-and-Act-It Mini Dramas (LESSON_03_TELL_AND_ACT_JOINING.md): child-created narratives and acted new arrivals

  • PK3 J3-1: Adult models a different story, then child chooses a toy character who arrives after one toy was there. Child physically directs/moves a genuinely new toy to existing collection and notes growth; “story sounded happy” is not PK3 action. J3-2: Child creates own two-visitor arrival story, physically demonstrates two new pieces arriving where one existed. J3-3: New unfamiliar human-friendly “one coat on rack then new coat” scene; child shows physical increase. Retell is optional evidence.
  • PK4 J4-1: Recreate a simple changed-story 1+1 physically or verbally, with distinguishable old and new groups. J4-2: Adult tells a separate 1+2 example, then invites child to invent an original verbal word problem or choose objects/drawings to show “there were …, … more joined, now …”; do not require memorized equation or speaking if AAC preferred. J4-3: Child invents a meaningful joining story with a result up to five in these exercises; the story must indicate both groups and arrival, not solely “I have five.” J4-4: Prompt a changed non-dramatic novel context and record child-owned word problem or permitted alternate representation, plus later independent revisit.
  • False positive: Child recites “two plus three makes five” after adult, but never specifies or acts out two existing and three arriving. A memorized fact or speaking fluently isn't valid independent join modeling. Adult shows distinct story on other numbers and invites fresh child-owned scenario.

Four written delivery routes with equal intended objective

SOLO (NOT IMPLEMENTED for this new complete family): Future fully narrated accessible app must offer visual meaning of original/new groups, small J3 first, actual child initiated virtual object movement on a genuinely new object, PK4 selectable objects/drawing/voice-or-AAC story medium (choose ONE), hidden adult model before learner EXIT, misconception feedback differing for “old moved” vs “new arrived,” distinct novel stage checks and true later revisit. Preserve independent first action and aid provenance. The old Garden prototype has a partial unrelated bounded solo route and must not be presented as this new entire three-method child-only implementation.

CO-PLAY: Adult and child choose a meaningful shared narrative/picture/delivery and swap creator/viewer roles. At PK3 first the child physically adds new pieces rather than adult doing the math; at PK4 child authors own old/incoming joining representation while adult demonstrates on separate items and listens. Practice can be helped, independent novel EXIT cannot covertly include adult mathematical content. Include alternative communications and child opt-out.

ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Prepare safe five-or-fewer large stable pieces for introductory tasks and a changed set for EXIT. Use actual method-specific exact language. Separate ENTRY→MODEL→DURING→EXIT→REVISIT; record P3 G/C and P4 R first actions and assistance in a dated anonymous ledger. Model on distinct combination without leaking exam answer. Follow any applicable IEP/IFSP/504 and home language/AAC access under caregiver/educator responsibility.

TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Arrange three learning stations (real delivery mat, before/after picture workshop and child-authored mini-story), each with stage bins for novice and extension; every child has a feasible accessible personal amount/representation opportunity. Project teacher visual if useful, but do not infer 24 mastered children from teacher’s single example or a chorus. Use original 24-seat focus-observation protocol where appropriate; schedule small-group observation appointments and document NOT_OBSERVED for unexamined children. Avoid public moral rankings, personal family data and unsupervised small objects.

All modes: The same exact published source action and depth of teaching, misconception repair, source-valid action and later revisit must be designed across modes. Existing lesson text is written only: delivery effectiveness, functional full SOLO and equal learning value remain NOT VERIFIED.

Assessment provenance, accommodations and materials

PK3 evidence G child independently indicates increase and C child places/directs new physical objects; if one missing, record actual partial/NOT_OBSERVED and next teaching move, never “PK3 mastered.” PK4 R child independently authors a meaningful old+incoming→joined representation in one permitted medium. T optional count of total is a separately recorded related performance, not substitute for R. On EVERY phase log first response raw action, date (future until real), task/method/values, help H, independently child-origin U, access A, unknown X or NOT_OBSERVED. A three-dot final picture with no old/incoming relation or correct memorized sum is no automatic R. Authentic child-directed adult physical proxy is access when the child owns conceptual choices; adult-supplied quantity or joining relationship is instructional help.

Accommodations: large nontoxic pieces, adult-only cutting/print preparation, no beans, nuts, small seeds, choking pieces, real edible garden produce, recordings, identifiable pupil data or pressure to perform publicly; fixed nonslip mat, high-contrast original frames, tactile tokens, AAC, signed/home-language retelling, adult physical proxy without answer provision and child's right to pause/refuse. For actual children responsibly review individualized plans (IEP/IFSP/504); here no such actual review exists. Safe classroom supervision remains required.

Printables: ORIGINAL_GARDEN_KIT.html reused with provenance for delivery method; ORIGINAL_JOINING_STAGE_MATS.html new first-party teacher print for blank before/arrival/after and drama stage material; do not expose final assessment examples on the child’s practice sheet in advance. Digital print check is internal only, not a physical rehearsal.

Acceptance boundary: All three methods are original authored teaching drafts with grade-specific methods and source-aware tests, but no child/educator effect claims. This progression is a meaningful written multi-session upgrade, not a completed learning application, normalized instrument or real-world effectiveness study.
