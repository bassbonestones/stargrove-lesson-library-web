Garden Gathering — Before/During/After learning evidence cycle (MT-PK-04)

Developmental correction (October 30, 2026): Use the current family developmental sequence, not the optional archived pilot audit (not in reviewed library). PK3.V.B.1 requires child-directed actual addition of genuinely new objects and demonstration of growth; PK4.V.B.1 permits a genuinely authored joining representation in objects OR pictures OR verbal word problem (source adding up to five objects). The original legacy tablet implements only a bounded separate pilot; this active family is a written design without a completed SOLO implementation.

E2.2 historical lesson-pilot extension (2026-10-27): The original assessment is a prospective, non-normed teacher guide; the selected active family currently uses adult-led, classroom and co-play written assessment routes. Mentions of a tablet below are historical observations about the separately archived pilot (not in reviewed library), not working software evidence of a complete current-family SOLO. Common recording standards: education/planning/EDUCATION_E2_CONTINUOUS_ASSESSMENT_AND_EVIDENCE_STANDARD_PK_G3_V0_1.md.

1. Opening check / prior learning (ENTRY: ~1 minute, before any model)

  • Teacher-led full class: Hand each child a large safe mat and one original garden counter plus one NEW counter kept outside the circle. Say: “If this new one joins, will our garden have more, fewer, or the same number?” Have children first respond individually by pointing/card/gesture/drawing, not by copying a choral answer. Keep the first response if genuinely observable. The classroom lesson can then proceed to 2 original + 3 new, as currently planned. Don't turn 24 simultaneous responses into 24 scored students if nobody saw whose work was whose.
  • Small group or individual: Same question, 1+1 objects. Ask “Show me what happens” with accessible materials; mark support, not only correctness.
  • HISTORICAL ARCHIVED pilot tablet only; no current-family SOLO: The old bounded device proposal begins with optional 1+1 before adult model and may record local clicks/moves, but does not establish real PK3 physical object action or who helped; skip/uncertain remains NOT_OBSERVED, and no current lesson must operate it.
  • Prior-topic retrieval: Optionally ask “If we only move the SAME counter, did a NEW one arrive?” to reactivate comparison from prior lessons; this is an assessment of that specific related idea, not automatically a check of addition.

2. During the lesson (DURING: integrated, zero separate exam minutes)

For a class of 24, the educator can offer separate child-owned safe large-object opportunities in scheduled 2–3-child observation groups, reusing a limited supply across rotations, or preprepare up to 120 large safe pieces for simultaneous exploration (five per child when stage-appropriate) with supervised access; no claim one adult observes 24 independent actions at once. Use current local blank Stage-Preparation and Evidence Card or an authorized private log, not a mandatory legacy form. The separate archived 24-seat blank sheet (not in reviewed library) may be consulted as historical optional planning. Unwitnessed children remain NOT_OBSERVED. The 2+3 kit belongs to a later developmental stage; keep novice PK3 1+1 real joining. Record child prediction, true new members added, growth/child-authored PK4 representation, support provenance; chorus answers do not count.

The adult may ask a harder question or reteach immediately. Record supported performance instead of omitting it. For co-play, the teacher/parent rates the child's observed decisions separately from the adult's prompts; device click telemetry alone can't establish which partner tapped.

3. Closing check (EXIT: ~1–2 minutes, alternate parallel task)

Teacher-led: Use a changed context: ONE flower already at the garden sign and TWO new leaves arriving. Do not demonstrate the answer first. Ask child to (a) indicate more/same/fewer, (b) choose which new pieces join instead of simply moving the old flower, and (c) show total three if representing combined quantities is also an appropriate objective. This is 1+2, deliberately different from the taught 2+3. Each child can work on their own small mat or with a teacher group; paper/photo work sample can be attributed only if policy/consent permits.

HISTORICAL ARCHIVED PILOT TABLET, not the current selected family: An old 1+2 in-memory trace can distinguish click sequences, but does not prove physical child source, offscreen help, individualized teacher confirmation, or deployed new family SOLO. The described earlier 1+1 entry trace and 1+2 exit trace are separate pilot examples, not measured learning gains.

Teacher record: If child independently shows growth and creates joined group but has not demonstrated 3 as a numeral, mark criterion G=2/U, C=2/U, T=? or 1/U as appropriate. Do not collapse PK3 growth concept and PK4 quantity representation into a binary “pass/fail.”

4. Short criterion-specific rubric

? not observed/uncertain; 0 not demonstrated on this task; 1 partial/inconsistent; 2 demonstrated clearly on this task. These are unvalidated pilot teacher-level descriptors, not a normed or legal reporting scale.

Criterion 0 (not yet demonstrated in this task) 1 (partial/inconsistent) 2 (clear demonstration in this task)
G — Growth prediction Child indicates no increase after new nonzero objects join, with an intelligible accessible response Chooses or explains inconsistently, or answer changes before any hint Child independently represents/predicts more from genuinely new additions
C — Constructs joining (not rearranging) Child moves only old pieces, no new piece joined, and task objective is not demonstrated Begins joining but omits objects or confuses old/new before recovery Child directs or physically moves genuinely new pieces into one combined group
T — Represents all-together quantity (PK4 extension when appropriate; not a prerequisite to PK3 growth) Provided an accessible opportunity but does not show combined total Shows partial/arrivals-only quantity or inconsistent count Correctly indicates combined total of the actual collection by meaningful method

Separate assistance tag for EACH criterion: U unprompted / first attempt; H teaching hint, model or answer-providing guidance preceded response; A physical/speech/perceptual access support without adult making mathematical choice; X unknown. An outcome like 2/H is STILL valuable, and different from 2/U. ? is never a zero. A child's unfamiliar communication modality must not be confused with inadequate conceptual knowledge.

October 30 stage-specific assessment addendum — PK4 R (authoring only, not the running app)

The prior G (predicts growth), C (constructs a join), T (all-together quantity) rubric remains a transparent descriptive pilot, NOT a complete PK4.V.B.1 source assessment. For future adult/classroom review explicitly observe a separate R = CHILD-AUTHORED JOINING REPRESENTATION, showing or explaining the initial group, genuinely new incoming group and their joining relation in one personally authored source-permitted medium. In this selected introductory 2+3 activity the combined total happens to be five or fewer, but the published PK4 wording is adding up to five objects; do not turn the example's small resulting total into a universal official ceiling or reject a source-valid child-generated representation solely because a different appropriate starting group produces a result above five. This is not a new official Texas source code and is not a normed scale. It can use physical objects OR pictures OR a verbal word problem as available in the source; one authentic eligible medium is sufficient, with child-origin ideas separately logged from a physical proxy or adult-led example. A three-dot final drawing with no visible two-part joining representation does NOT automatically satisfy R; a response repeating “five” likewise does not. Record R=NOT_OBSERVED until a source-valid independent new task is observed; guided practice is separately labeled H, access A.

Runtime parity limitation: The original index.html reports G/C/T-like interactions but has NO complete independently reviewed PK4 R source validity instrumentation or multi-session J4 progression. Do not silently fabricate R or a verified solo grade from these interaction logs. A real educator and child access specialist must review this future scoring proposal before external acceptance.
