PK3 participation can be modeled and supported. PK4 identification requires the actual English onset and intact rime, a familiar one-syllable word and child-owned identification, not independent target selection, novel vocabulary, perfect pronunciation or spelling. English-only describes the sound task; concept-correct home-language, sign, AAC or picture responses may identify the English referent. Page 35 explicitly permits selecting from several pictures. Do not hide all images or remove communication access: prevent target highlighting, a sole correct item or immediate adult whole-answer echo from falsely earning independent identification. Pictures of familiar alternatives can remain visible throughout.

These opportunities are not a mandatory ladder, readiness checklist or curriculum. Choose a useful entry point; return to earlier playful forms when helpful. Models may precede a first child turn. Previously taught words are valid for current response evidence. Use changed known words only when exploring a stronger transfer claim; novelty is not compulsory. A later return can support learning but no calendar change automatically establishes retention. No actual child observations have occurred here.

PK3 opportunities

PK3 O3-1 — Join a simple sound bridge

ENTRY: invite listening or play, without requiring a first test. MODEL: one adult says /k/ ... at, cat. DURING: child may join the rime, co-blend or coordinate a meaningful agreed gesture with sound play. EXIT: describe actual participation, even if modeled; mere presence is not demonstrated engagement. REVISIT: repeat when welcome; NOT_OBSERVED at authoring.

PK3 O3-2 — Enjoy changing a beginning

ENTRY: recall a familiar sound game. MODEL: /k/ ... at and /h/ ... at. DURING: choose a sound turn or join adult play while keeping the ending intact. EXIT: participation does not require a correct whole-word answer. REVISIT: follow interest, not a fixed quota.

PK3 O3-3 — Try a playful message

ENTRY: offer a friendly optional puppet or plain voice. MODEL: /m/ ... ap, map. DURING: child responds to the sound message in their preferred accessible way. EXIT: note the child's actual contribution and help; adult puppet motion alone is not child participation. REVISIT: optional familiar game, not a compulsory novel target.

PK3 O3-4 — Explore other known words

ENTRY: let interest guide a familiar theme. MODEL: offer another intact-rime word when useful. DURING: supported sound play, not a timed identification quiz. EXIT: record participation, not inferred generalization from a decorative change. REVISIT: future observations remain NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 opportunities

PK4 O4-1 — Identify a familiar word

ENTRY: establish word meanings through ordinary experience or vocabulary teaching. MODEL: one adult demonstrates joining chunks. DURING: /k/ ... at; child identifies cat through voice, sign, AAC, home-language label or balanced picture choice. EXIT: a genuine identification on a taught word is possible; immediate adult answer echo is supported learning. REVISIT: record actual time and help if observed.

PK4 O4-2 — Notice a changed beginning

ENTRY: retain accessible meanings. MODEL: compare /k/ ... at with /h/ ... at. DURING: vary one onset with the ending intact. EXIT: distinguish child response from target pointing or a fixed-position guess. REVISIT: repeating known material can be worthwhile practice.

PK4 O4-3 — Explore another rime

ENTRY: choose a familiar word such as map. MODEL: /m/ ... ap as two groups, not three phonemes. DURING: identify the referent using established response supports. EXIT: no required verbal explanation or printed decoding. REVISIT: broaden only as useful.

PK4 O4-4 — Clarify evidence under access

ENTRY: check audibility and known alternatives. MODEL: demonstrate if needed, keeping instruction distinct from later claims. DURING: provide neutral replay and familiar response array without pointing to the answer. EXIT: unknown gaze or peer-led response stays uncertain; do not strip needed access. REVISIT: offer a fair opportunity later, not forced remediation.

PK4 O4-5 — Optional generalization

ENTRY: choose known words with a different rime or context if the child is willing. MODEL: teach as needed; instruction is not proof of transfer. DURING: sound joining remains the task. EXIT: claim only the change genuinely observed, not permanent mastery. A child's valid familiar response remains valuable even if transfer is not attempted. REVISIT: delayed evidence must actually occur before claiming retention.

Delivery and proportionate interpretation

ADULT-LED and CO-PLAY use one adult, voice and a child, with optional neutral mat and familiar choices. CLASSROOM uses visible shared play and a few focal observations; no second adult or unsupervised centers assumed. Group response is practice, not every individual's identification. Keep a private fair rotation list and record NOT_OBSERVED for missing opportunities. Retain hearing support, quiet, AAC, gaze/sign and safe large response choices. If actual English sound contrast is inaccessible, teach a related accessible goal and defer that inference rather than assert sensory equivalence.

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED. No software, empirical parity, physical print trial or child outcome is claimed. The current public-scope task is two excellent human paths, not four finished digital modes. A familiar word game is one part of rich early learning, not a whole program.
