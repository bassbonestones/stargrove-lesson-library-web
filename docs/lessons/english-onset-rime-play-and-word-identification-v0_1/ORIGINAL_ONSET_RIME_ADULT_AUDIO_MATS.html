1 · ADULT ONLY: actually spoken English onset + intact rime

Adult script: these symbols and spelling are NOT shown to learners as their answer. Actual audio uses familiar, natural English and a meaningful short pause between the onset and the ENTIRE intact rime.

/k/ ... /æt/ → CAT

One spoken onset and one whole vowel-and-final-consonant rime. For an uncued PK4 response, say these two chunks without immediately giving the whole answer. Establish vocabulary through ordinary talk or earlier teaching.

/h/ ... /æt/ → HAT

Same complete rime with a different beginning sound. This is not separate /h/ /æ/ /t/ phonemes or printed H-A-T spelling.

/m/ ... /æp/ → MAP

Different real familiar one-syllable English word and intact rime. Balanced familiar picture or AAC options may be visible throughout; do not highlight the answer.

/s/ ... /ʌn/ → SUN

Natural different one-syllable English sound bridge where appropriately pronounced and truly familiar.

PK3: child genuinely participates in real playful English oral onset–rime blending, with support and without perfect independent accuracy required. PK4: child identifies familiar one-syllable English WHOLE WORD from separated onset plus INTACT rime spoken first, no immediate whole-answer echo or sole-answer visual cue; balanced familiar picture choices are permitted. This outcome is explicitly ENGLISH ONLY, not a deficit label for learners of other languages.

2 · Child-facing safe unmarked sound bridges and choices

These two blank large sound spaces stand for real sound groups heard orally: first onset, second intact vowel-plus-end rime. Child does not have to read IPA symbols, spell letters, identify individual phonemes or know a word shown in advance.

Sound group one then sound group two

Adult speaks English sound groups separately FIRST; child responds with choice of voice, sign/AAC or adapted motor gesture. Optional gesture supports sound play; no exact movement or response quota is required. Teacher motion alone is not child evidence. No small loose pieces.

Unmarked neutral alternatives

These blank spaces are not supplied picture answers. Use two or three familiar large pictures, safe objects or established AAC choices if helpful; all may be visible before the sounds. Do not highlight or point out the target. Replay and quiet remain available.

An adult-only demonstration is not child evidence. PK3 may legitimately participate by joining a model; an immediate whole-answer echo alone does not establish independent PK4 identification. Home-language support and bilingual learning are welcome as separate goals, but do not substitute them for the official English-only task.

3 · Four PK3/five PK4 stages and authentic observation limits

PK3 participates: four steps

O3-1 optional CAT sound bridge game; O3-2 HAT with new onset and same rime; O3-3 original MAP puppet sound turn; O3-4 learner-chosen participation with another or repeated English word. No independent perfect-answer quota.

PK4 identifies: five steps

O4-1 identify CAT after /k/ ... /æt/; O4-2 HAT after /h/ ... /æt/; O4-3 MAP after /m/ ... /æp/; O4-4 RUG after /r/ ... /ʌg/ with no target pre-cue; O4-5 optional transfer to a different familiar English one-syllable word; familiar taught words remain valid for current identification.

Five phases

ENTRY invitation (model first if useful) → MODEL one adult joins sounds → DURING responsive play/identification → EXIT voluntary current response → REVISIT optional practice or an actual later observation.
