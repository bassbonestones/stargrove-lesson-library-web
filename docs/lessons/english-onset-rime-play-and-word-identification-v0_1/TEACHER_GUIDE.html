Objective-wide reference, not a counted lesson

Use DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md for accurate source boundaries and flexible grade opportunities. The current DOT21 revision is the CAT–HAT–BAT candidate, not acceptance of any sibling.

  • Method 01: CAT–HAT–BAT, companion. One constant intact “at” ending with changed beginnings; one-adult and class flows are fully specified in its plan.
  • Method 02: puppet messages (not yet in reviewed library), companion (not yet in reviewed library). Optional imaginary messenger with MAP/SUN. Older drafts remain unqualified and need independent review; a puppet is a teaching invitation, not an observed child.
  • Method 03: other sound bridges (not yet in reviewed library), companion (not yet in reviewed library). Optional changed-word/rime exploration. Older drafts remain unqualified; changed words support a stronger transfer investigation, not a compulsory gate for basic participation/identification.

Texas 2022 pp.34–35: PK3.III.B.8 participation in English onset-rime blending; PK4.III.B.8 familiar one-syllable word identification from onset/rime. P35 allows target selection from several pictures; p8 forbids turning guidelines into an assessment checklist. Use one adult, accurate sounds, familiar meanings and accessible child responses. Do not require a second adult, novel items, pre-model testing, English expressive speech or a fixed later calendar date for basic evidence.

ENTRY may begin with a model; MODEL is teaching; DURING welcomes supported practice; EXIT records the actual child response and support; REVISIT is future practice or an honestly documented later observation. Keep access support distinct from conceptual answers and uncertainty. Avoid ambiguous U/H/A/X shorthand. A balanced response array is valid; a sole answer cue or immediate echoed whole answer does not prove independent PK4 identification. Modeled PK3 participation is legitimate. No mastery cut score or diagnosis.

First-party resources are optional adult script/neutral mat and private blank observation forms. They are not supplied word-picture sets, recorded audio or physical/tactile tested artifacts. No SOLO app or empirical equivalence is claimed. Follow the selected lesson's complete preparation and feedback, not this index as a teaching script.

R04 synthetic helper input contract

The optional Python helper is not a teacher assessment or a requirement for sound play. Every Boolean judgment must be an actual literal True/False, never a string, number or omitted guess. Grade and source must be exact matching strings. All inputs, including grade-specific warning/unused fields, are validated before inference; malformed records raise input errors rather than child scores. Decline/access judgment must be explicit: missing/unknown raises an input error and must not be filled False merely to run a result. Actual unknown observations remain unknown in descriptive teacher notes; this small synthetic helper does not represent every teaching record. Literal False is a legitimate explicit judgment, not missing data. PK3 modeled participation remains valid, PK4 identification needs attributable response, balanced picture/AAC options remain valid, and familiarity/optional novelty remain distinct. The PK3 independent-demand warning describes an instructional concern, including when opportunity is unobserved, not an observed learner outcome. These shared input repairs do not qualify either sibling lesson or certify adult-supplied judgments as truthful.
