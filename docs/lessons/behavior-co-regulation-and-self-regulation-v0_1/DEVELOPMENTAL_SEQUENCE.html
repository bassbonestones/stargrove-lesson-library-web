Supported behavior management and increasingly self-directed safe regulation

Exact published 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines Social and Emotional Development I.B.1.c, official original research page index 11: PK3.I.B.1.c — “Child manages own behavior with adult guidance and assistance.” SHA 7ce96073d9895dbe53d059a144e6fa7873c195d3262ab1cc801bc87b442d5d7c. PK4.I.B.1.c — “Child regulates own behavior with occasional reminders or assistance from adults.” SHA 43c84a7d7cc3217606c337031a3803012c739f7f473cea574ed4ec36c0c79ca3. What the official source means and what it does not require

PK3 child manages OWN behavior WITH adult guidance and assistance, which may include co-regulation, accessible options, naming choices, reasonable routine expectations and helpful adult support. A child who needs help is not below the printed source solely for that reason. The adult acting alone or answering a cartoon "good behavior" card does not show learner's real child-involved behavior management.

PK4 regulates OWN behavior WITH OCCASIONAL reminders OR assistance from adults. The conjunction is OR in the printed wording; never invent a no-help requirement, zero reminders, strict reminders-per-minute cap or demand a child always regulate without another person. Qualitative occasional help differs from adult continuously directing each step. Authentic behaviors can include choosing a safe alternative, asking for quiet/help, managing shared space, waiting voluntarily, pausing, rejoining as child chooses, and responding respectfully to someone else's genuine request. Suppressing emotions, always smiling, staying silent, remaining still, compulsory eye contact, physical suppression, shame or withholding basic support are not self-regulation evidence.

Source-valid familiar behavior and legitimate guidance: Publisher PK3.I.B.1.c permits genuine child-managed behavior WITH adult guidance/assistance; child need not invent a strategy if a trusted adult proposes a real choice and learner meaningfully enacts it. PK4.I.B.1.c permits OCCASIONAL behavior-content reminders or assistance, while continuous NEUTRAL AAC interpretation, sensory/motor access and supervision are not continuous adult direction. A genuinely observed meaningful real safe response in a familiar ordinary context is source-relevant even without a new setting; changed context is optional stronger transfer only if actually witnessed. No natural need/opportunity means NOT_OBSERVED, never difficulty/diagnosis. Do not engineer distress, require forced quiet, eye contact or perfect calmness, or exclude child-led continuation/opt-out.

Method02 real shared-resource specificity: An actual ordinary OPTIONAL school-shared (not personally owned, sensory/communication aid or necessary accommodation) material may naturally be in legitimate use. Children needn't share private property or favor an adult-declared “equally attractive” substitute, wait a prescribed time or suppress normal disappointment. A PK3 learner with genuine co-regulated participation may request later turn, another accessible choice, pause, help or safe continuation. PK4 child can direct such a response with occasional meaning-bearing behavior help and continuing neutral AAC. A familiar genuinely enacted safe response meets possible source; different actual safe setting only optionally strengthens transfer, never publisher's required new context. Do not stage an absence to fill source sheet; no naturally relevant occasion = NOT_OBSERVED.

Method03 authentic TWO-BRANCH source guard: A real voluntarily present peer OR focal child can spontaneously express an actual personal-space preference; respect BOTH children's consent and own safe boundary without staged rejection/required apology, hug, eye contact or compelled return. Separately a normal genuinely FOREWARNED gentle transition may naturally create a meaningful processing/help/space need; do not surprise, frighten, use loud timer or stage schedule disruption. Neither branch must be performed simultaneously or in a new setting, and absence of real meaningful need is NOT_OBSERVED, not dysregulation. PK3 child co-regulation with adult-origin suggestions may be valid when child actually contributes; PK4 child directs meaningful action with occasional behavior reminders and ongoing neutral AAC/mobility/sensory access. Private observation never claims global behavioral trait or field efficacy.

No engineered distress: Do not surprise/frighten a child, require anger, induce fatigue, remove a disability accommodation, create an impossible waiting task, withhold food/water/care, block exits or force proximity to others. Use authentic mild ordinary safe choices and transitions only; child can opt out, ask for adult and take a break. Privacy: never collect mental-health diagnoses, identity videos or detailed behavioral labels, and don't interpret disability-related support as misconduct. Child's home-language/sign/AAC can express genuine autonomous plan/help; for real behavior source also observe their feasible actual self-chosen response, including child-directed support actions. Qualify real classroom practices with educator/family and actual IEP/IFSP/504 team where relevant; not a clinical treatment plan.

ENTRY before any adult example in an actual gentle accessible new situation, observe child first safe behavior choice/action and true support. MODEL adult acts on DIFFERENT original safe art/reading situation and narrates own pause/alternative/support request, not script a child answer. DURING supported co-regulation, H conceptual next-action decided by adult, A motor/AAC access under child's intent, U child-origin first appropriate self-choice, X unclear. EXIT fresh genuinely changed situation with PK3 adult-guided actual child contribution vs PK4 occasional adult reminder/assistance plus meaningful own response, not puppet-only. REVISIT later distinct real calendar day, NOT_OBSERVED at authoring. No single observed calmness = global mastery.

PK3 four graduated learning stages

PK3 C3-1 — Recognize a benign need and get supportive co-regulation

ENTRY: In a low-pressure voluntary safe art activity, child may show they need quiet, a break or another large prop; adult recognizes and gently helps child choose an appropriate safe action without making distress necessary. MODEL: Teacher on DIFFERENT own activity MODELS recognizing their own need to pause and asking another adult for space, not labeling learner's feelings. DURING: Practice adult-guided child choice of appropriate behavior/transition, meaningful learner contribution, H if adult entirely decides and acts, A access-only as learner chooses. False credit when adult directs/does every behavior or a story puppet chooses 'calm' without child engaging; correct with other adult example then new real child-owned appropriate option with adult support. Fresh independent EXIT: Actual real familiar or changed benign need/choice, learner meaningfully manages a behavior with adult guidance/assistance; can accept educator-proposed appropriate option and enact without forced independent strategy invention, not puppet empathy icon. REVISIT: real later different date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 C3-2 — Choose a safer available response with adult help

ENTRY: Optional group art has a real benign conflict of available large tools; adult offers mutually safe choices and child may choose a turn, alternate material, space or ask for help rather than forced sharing. MODEL: Teacher MODELS on separate adult-owned craft project how to pause and choose an alternate safe big card when one isn't available. DURING: Adult can help name alternatives and accompany learner response; emotional state not graded, support may be significant as PK3 source allows. False credit when adult directs/does every behavior or a story puppet chooses 'calm' without child engaging; correct with other adult example then new real child-owned appropriate option with adult support. Fresh independent EXIT: Novel accessible real safe context and child actually selects/implements kind safe response with adult guidance, not simply says 'be good'. REVISIT: real later different date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 C3-3 — Manage a real gentle transition using adult support

ENTRY: A quiet nonpunitive changed classroom transition invites learner to put down safe activity or choose accessible continuation/break with calm adult assistance. MODEL: Teacher demonstrates on DIFFERENT teacher-owned book that they can ask for processing time then move to another benign activity. DURING: Child can request time or help and actually enact safe adaptation; no compulsory sudden sound, timer or masking own distress. False credit when adult directs/does every behavior or a story puppet chooses 'calm' without child engaging; correct with other adult example then new real child-owned appropriate option with adult support. Fresh independent EXIT: Fresh optional real activity change, child with suitable adult help makes real safe behavior choice/transition, not body restraint or command-only obedience. REVISIT: real later different date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 C3-4 — Transfer supported behavior management to new situation

ENTRY: An unlike safe real art/book or social routine offers child an opportunity to choose own appropriate safe action with responsive adult guidance when needed, before teacher model. MODEL: Teacher models unrelated adult alternative/help request after first entry, not child response. DURING: Adult can offer co-regulation without requiring self-contained calm, preserve real need for breaks and support; record child's actual choice/action. False credit when adult directs/does every behavior or a story puppet chooses 'calm' without child engaging; correct with other adult example then new real child-owned appropriate option with adult support. Fresh independent EXIT: New real context, meaningful child-owned adjustment to safe action with adult guidance and assistance; no zero-help mandate or clinical emotion rating. REVISIT: real later different date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 five graduated learning stages

PK4 C4-1 — Retrieve child-origin safe response with optional prompt

ENTRY: Child encounters quiet benign new material choice/transition and independently begins a safe response, may receive a timely simple reminder or adult assistance. MODEL: Teacher separately MODELS with other adult materials a quiet pause and request for help without pressure. DURING: Fade repetitive per-step commands, retain appropriate access and comfort supports, teach child reason for behavior rather than compliance. If continuous teacher direction, forced calm or withholding support is credited as occasional adult help, reteach on unrelated safe adult example and reobserve new real voluntary child response. Fresh independent EXIT: Actual safe familiar or different ordinary situation with child-directed appropriate action and optional occasional meaningful adult help; new context strengthens transfer only, not the printed source baseline. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 C4-2 — Identify own helpful strategy in a calm context

ENTRY: In an optional art or story project, child chooses strategy for managing an ordinary benign impulse to interrupt activity (request turn, choose alternate, set safe pause) without needing an emotional crisis. MODEL: Teacher on DIFFERENT safe scene MODELS noticing own urge to change plan and asking a colleague for a short pause. DURING: Teach meaningful choice and real response, not mandatory deep breathing, fixed 'calm face' or silence, adult occasional prompts as needed. If continuous teacher direction, forced calm or withholding support is credited as occasional adult help, reteach on unrelated safe adult example and reobserve new real voluntary child response. Fresh independent EXIT: New safe ordinary situation, child actually originates and enacts a strategy suited to their own need with occasional adult reminder/assistance. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 C4-3 — Regulate a safe shared-space response while respecting others

ENTRY: A genuine willing partner says they need room at optional table, child may choose moving own accessible hand or asking for another spot, remaining engaged or opting out safely. MODEL: Adult and other real willing adult separately MODEL boundary-respecting cooperation on unrelated objects, no forced peer contact. DURING: Child makes appropriate actual safe choice with no more than occasional reminder or help, no adult total takeover or social conformity score. If continuous teacher direction, forced calm or withholding support is credited as occasional adult help, reteach on unrelated safe adult example and reobserve new real voluntary child response. Fresh independent EXIT: New genuine safe real partner boundary/turn moment, child chooses and follows through an appropriate safe response with occasional reminder or assistance. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 C4-4 — Recover from a benign unexpected change with limited cueing

ENTRY: A new simple quiet schedule change or material being unavailable occurs without inducing distress, and child can decide another safe plan, ask for break or request help. MODEL: Teacher on separate adult-owned project MODELS adapting after favorite clean card unavailable, choosing different one without labeling child. DURING: Adult may offer occasional reminder or assistance rather than continuous dictates; learner personally chooses actual response, can request accessibility supports. If continuous teacher direction, forced calm or withholding support is credited as occasional adult help, reteach on unrelated safe adult example and reobserve new real voluntary child response. Fresh independent EXIT: New safe changed expectation, child actually adapts behavior meaningfully with occasional adult input; no imposed distress tolerance or coercive delay. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 C4-5 — Full changed-context constructive self-regulation

ENTRY: Across genuinely new optional safe real classroom situations, child makes child-owned relevant actions/requests to manage behavior with occasional adult reminders OR assistance as needed, not adult policing every step. MODEL: Teacher after child-first entry MODELS unrelated own strategy and then DURING responsive practice, no claim of therapy treatment. DURING: Record actual voluntary child response, independent intent, any adult cue/support; respect sensory, motor and language variability and right to pause/stop. If continuous teacher direction, forced calm or withholding support is credited as occasional adult help, reteach on unrelated safe adult example and reobserve new real voluntary child response. Fresh independent EXIT: Fresh independently child-chosen safe self-regulation behavior in changed real situation with at most occasional adult reminders/assistance, no forced calm or unassisted perfection. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

Three different learning mechanisms

01 Child-owned safe pause/self-advocacy. 02 Ordinary available resource/turn and meaningful safe alternative. 03 Respectful consent-boundary or changed classroom transition repair. None requires emotion suppression or unsafe adult control, each differs in actual situational purpose.

Four complete WRITTEN teaching modes (intended equitable opportunities; parity untested)

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: Future genuinely prereader-operated child-only tablet could offer safe narrator-led original low-stakes scenario choices and nonreading AAC/sign/voice responses where virtual learner may choose a break, safe alternative, ask help or respond to a simulated partner's clear boundary. This is valuable solo practice in self-guided planning and emotion-respecting choices; a cartoon choosing "calm" is not real child self-regulation under genuine classroom conditions. Real functional app not implemented. No camera/video/emotion tracking or stranger communication required. Actual real-world source would require observed child-involved behavior in an accessible situation under appropriate grade adult help conditions, first ENTRY, different MODEL, helped DURING, new real EXIT and genuine later REVISIT. CO-PLAY: Caregiver and learner choose real safe large clean art/book activity and mild ordinary choices, child can request break, alternate item, turn or privacy. PK3 caregiver guides/regulates together with child and notes own child action; PK4 occasional reminders or assistance without caregiver dictating every choice. MODEL unrelated adult example after first ENTRY; new real EXIT and later-day REVISIT. No manufactured stress or mandatory emotion suppression. ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Educator privately checks safe comfortable child-chosen participation, sensory/motor, language and AAC access, actual support plan/IEP as appropriate. First real behavior choice before unrelated MODEL, assisted DURING with H vs access A. PK3 actual child participation WITH co-regulation and adult help. PK4 self-chosen safe responses with occasional adult reminder OR assistance, not no-help. New unmodeled safe EXIT and only actual later-day REVISIT. TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: Whole group puppet feelings/rules demonstrations are unscored teaching. Each learner has safe voluntary authentic class activity/transition and chance to choose appropriate behavior, partner consent if relevant; teacher respects child break/assistance needs. PK3 adult guidance and assistance permitted, PK4 occasional reminders or assistance. Privately log actual child-origin response vs adult takeover and H/A/U/X, changed real EXIT and genuine later observed REVISIT. No public behavioral labeling, normative posture/eye contact, forced peer interaction, punitive obedience or simulated learning equated with real self-regulation.
