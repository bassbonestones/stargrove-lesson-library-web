Objective-level educator guide — Adult-guided and increasingly self-directed behavior management

Role: Overarching source-bound educator guide, NOT one of the three lesson plans. Internal published Texas 2022 SEL source-cell SHA-256 receipts verified in authoring inventory; original PDF two-column visual concordance and independent expert review remain pending.

PK3.I.B.1.c — Child manages own behavior with adult guidance and assistance.

Official cell SHA-256 7ce96073d9895dbe53d059a144e6fa7873c195d3262ab1cc801bc87b442d5d7c. In real low-pressure child-chosen activity, the child may NEED adult guidance and support to manage safe interpersonal/material/transition behavior. Adult co-regulation and meaningful guidance are part of the actual published skill, NOT a failure, and without help child need not demonstrate an unsupported PK4-like autonomy. True learner action may include meaningful pause/help request, choosing a safe alternate, respecting another's boundary WITH adult help; the adult fully physically directing child and speaking entire choice is not child managing. Never force distress or withhold needed accommodations to create a “challenge.”

PK4.I.B.1.c — Child regulates own behavior with occasional reminders or assistance from adults.

Official cell SHA-256 43c84a7d7cc3217606c337031a3803012c739f7f473cea574ed4ec36c0c79ca3. In another ordinary real low-pressure situation, the child originates a relevant safe adjustment or sustained meaningful response to a personally arising need, with perhaps OCCASIONAL adult reminders or assistance. “Occasional” is not zero and has no invented numeric cap. It contrasts with continuous teacher-by-teacher directing, adult script repetition, compliance under coercion or a simulated game response. A child taking a consensual comfortable break, asking for a safe alternative, setting boundaries, or calmly resuming on their OWN choice may show an appropriate regulation action. Rest is not a penalty or something to withdraw to prove progress.

Three distinct complete plans and facilitator companions

  1. Lesson 01: A Place for Pause — authentic learner-supported help and safe continuation with facilitator 01: during naturally occurring ordinary mild need/choice, child can safely request help/break/space, collaboratively accept adult-proposed choice or meaningfully continue/re-engage. PK3 adult guidance and assistance can generate a real co-regulated child contribution; PK4 child directs action with occasional behavior-content adult assistance, and ongoing neutral AAC/sensory/mobility access remains allowed. Familiar real source valid; changed context only optional stronger transfer, not official gate; no manufactured distress, forced calmness, punitive isolation or fictional icon credit.
  2. Lesson 02: Our Shared Maker's Table, real optional resource choice with facilitator 02: during an actual voluntary familiar or different creative task, when an OPTIONAL classroom-shared material is naturally/legitimately in use, protect personal possessions, essential aids and current consenting user; never engineer scarcity or force another learner to surrender a tool. Learner can meaningfully enact a safe accessible alternate, willing later turn/request, supported pause, continuation in another manner or opt out, including safely expressed disappointment. PK3 adult suggestion/co-regulation may lead to genuine personally enacted child behavior; PK4 child meaningfully directs with occasional behavior-content help and continuing neutral AAC/mobility/sensory access allowed. No mandated equally preferred substitute, waiting time, phrase, public emotion performance or different resource scenario: familiar REAL event is source-valid and changed context only stronger transfer if naturally actual. Blank picture mat alone and a teacher performing all behavioral steps cannot count.
  3. Lesson 03: Room for Everyone, personal space or forewarned gentle transition with facilitator 03: TWO independent genuine real source paths. (A) IF focal child or a genuinely willing peer independently communicates actual OWN/OTHER personal-space preference during normal voluntary art/story activity, a child may enact respectful safe distance/help/own STOP request; never recruit partner to act upset/reject, force touch, apologize, hug, eye contact or reconciliation. (B) During NORMAL PREDICTABLY FOREWARNED benign schedule transition, child may need/choose accessible time/help/quiet/continuation/route, not a staged abrupt routine disruption. Only an authentic meaningful enacted child action is a bounded source; no event, NOT_OBSERVED. A familiar genuine context is valid, unlike setting is optional stronger transfer, never publisher compulsory. PK3 adult guidance may propose real safe strategy with meaningful child action; PK4 child directs with occasional behavior-content help and continuing NEUTRAL AAC/sensory/motor access, not full adult physical compliance. No peer may be forced to join/continue and no global trait or clinical outcome inferred.

Pedagogy, false credit and source-specific EXIT

PK3 C3-1–C3-4 and PK4 C4-1–C4-5 in DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md sequence supportive self-advocacy, safe material alternatives and boundaries before more self-directed safe decisions. For ENTRY, watch a first actual authentic choice in new benign context BEFORE adult modeling. MODEL on adult's separate ordinary art/book materials illustrates choosing a pause/asking for help respectfully; adult model is NOT learner performance. DURING genuine optional practice, adult offers access and guidance, gradually fades unneeded scripts while never withholding necessary supports. EXIT must be an actual child-managed safe behavior response in a naturally occurring real familiar or changed context (changed context optionally tests stronger transfer, not a printed source requirement); PK3 records actual supportive guidance and learner part; PK4 records learner-directed action with occasional reminders/assistance. REVISIT means genuinely another calendar date with actual contextual behavior, NOT_OBSERVED until real; it need not be forced into a different setting. Preserve U child action, H adult content/script, A neutral access, X uncertainty. If no actual need/safe opportunity, report NOT_OBSERVED rather than manufacture a behavioral deficit. A child who calmly continues a chosen activity in response to a real manageable choice may show regulation; simple quietness with no actual opportunity does not. In PK3 an adult-suggested, child-accepted and enacted safe choice is not invalid simply because the adult originated a strategy. Continuous neutral communication, mobility and sensory access is not continuous PK4 content-bearing behavior direction.

When a child refuses a safe task, adults should respect a meaningful request for different access, not define refusal as “dysregulation.” If a peer asks for space, help both parties respect consent without forcing proximity. If child needs adult help, don't deny it to test independence. A fictional multiple-choice “take a break” selection is teaching preparation, not actual self-management. Natural variation in self-regulation never supports diagnosis or global rating.

Seven differentiated pathways and four modes

Attention/executive: predictable quiet task choices, optional low-stakes pauses and neutral fading reminder; language/communication: personally originated AAC/sign/home language help/break requests and behavior choices; literacy/phonology: no read-the-rule card prerequisite; numeracy: no count-based “three warnings” threshold; autism/sensory: offer comfort/quiet/solo option and protect opt-out, never demand eye contact; intellectual/developmental: adult-guided safe alternative with gradual self-choice; physical/motor/hearing/vision: reachable material, safe learner-directed access and individualized qualified professional consultation. Avoid therapy-like graded emotion suppression, shame and unsafe distress engineering. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: fictional avatar selecting break icon cannot prove real-life source response. CO-PLAY: trusted adult supports real learner behavior without doing it for child. ADULT-LED: teach genuine safe routines and distinguish support level by grade. CLASSROOM: privately observe original learner decisions, never create peer rejection to grade. Child learning, real later REVISIT, expert approval, physical material access, functioning SOLO and cross-mode parity remain NOT VERIFIED.
