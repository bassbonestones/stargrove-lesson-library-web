Method 03 — Conversational Repair, Different Ideas and Respectful Stop Signals

Exact published original sources, 2022 Texas Domain II.B.2: PK3.II.B.2 “Child begins to use appropriate language, style, and nonverbal cues during communication with familiar adults and peers.” SHA 60f155c8d46353c515fbaa728cc28eec0a7cd930a821e9fe23555d5da2d95866; PK4.II.B.2 “Child engages in conversations in appropriate ways, demonstrating knowledge of verbal and nonverbal conversational rules.” SHA b6f150a5930ad5b59eb27ffcebde9bd2aa9489d3a720121f10eb90b22c6b4939. Original printed literacy research PDF index 20 verified against registry, alternate-PDF concordance pending. See DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md.

Distinct mechanism: a genuinely willing actual human partner might misunderstand a harmless intended message, offer a different project preference or express desire to pause. Child learns how a NATURALLY OCCURRING harmless misunderstanding can be clarified and how a DIFFERENT valid personal preference or wish to stop can be honored without compulsory correction or apology. This distinct REPAIR/BOUNDARY METHOD is richer than publisher II.B.2 source; clear genuine real-human conversation without any misunderstanding remains source-relevant, not failed repair. PK3 actual familiar adult and real willing peer contexts should each be reported separately across genuine opportunities, with one real observed setting LIMITED source evidence and the absent one NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 needs genuine reciprocal human conversational rule use, not a mandated extra question/conflict. Unlike Method01 co-design or Method02 unknown fact transfer, only a truly arising ambiguity justifies actual repair.

Teacher preparation, healthy boundaries and real human social context

Create optional quiet shared safe large-object art project. Both adult and peer invitations must be consensual, learner free to say stop and partner free to leave; no forcing touch, apology, agreement, eye gaze, social mask or speech. Use neutral benign design disagreement (“I thought the big square might go beside the tray; what do you think?”), not disputes about personal family, race, disability, religion, medical needs, secrets or peer status. ONLY the adult-owned unrelated MODEL may intentionally demonstrate harmless ambiguity with two consenting adults on THEIR materials, plainly labeled a demonstration. Never deliberately mishear, tease, reject, confuse or socially embarrass the assessed child or an actual peer to manufacture a source trial. A true different art preference is NOT itself a misunderstanding or an error requiring apology. The lesson reinforces real different opinions can coexist and understanding can be repaired. A volunteer's stop cue must always be honored, even when this interrupts evidence collection.

ENTRY before MODEL: In familiar or changed actual voluntary safe conversation, a real partner may naturally need clarification, express an independent different preference, or say stop. First observe what learner REALLY communicated using own accessible language/style and natural sign/AAC/turn cue, and whether human meaning was exchanged; if no misunderstanding occurred, do not manufacture one. Distinguish a genuine unclear meaning (repair target) from simply different valid artistic preference (respectful disagreement) or voluntary boundary (honored stop). PK3 independently report actually witnessed adult and peer listener settings, other type NOT_OBSERVED; PK4 genuine reciprocal response may be brief without question. Adult-demanded sorry is H, not U.

MODEL on entirely other teacher-owned situation: Adult colleague suggests a different large square position, teacher replies "I heard you wanted it BESIDE the tray; I was thinking ON the mat. Which should we try?" Actual colleague responds and they agree to stop/continue; teacher visibly offers safe shared object, gives an optional AAC turn signal or says stop respectfully without required eye contact. No one is forced to concede; socially appropriate can include asserting a boundary. Adult highlights what partner actually said and how response followed, not count of scripted words.

DURING: Offer genuine optional real consenting partner dialogue. If confusion NATURALLY arises, educator neutrally invites child to restate, point or select meaning by sign/AAC without supplying exact repair; if simply different preferences, neither child nor peer is required to concede or apologize. If real partner says stop, learner may meaningfully acknowledge and both stop; record any human exchange ALREADY observed at its limited scope, leaving unperformed extended repair or transfer NOT_OBSERVED, not learner failure. A forced third turn, scripted sorry, eye gaze or sharing private item are not conditions. Child-led meaningful clarification, listening, explanation, or respectful boundary may be richer local method evidence when genuinely arising.

EXIT — real child-authored human conversation with OPTIONAL naturally arising repair: Observe only actual safe freely willing human exchanges, familiar or changed. PK3 each genuine familiar adult or familiar peer language/style/natural sign/AAC turn setting is separately reported; one witnessed setting is LIMITED source evidence while other NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 actual real responsive meaningful human conversational language/turn/closing is source relevant without mandatory conflict, clarifying question, unexpected idea or fixed length. For distinct REPAIR METHOD strength, look only for an actual naturally occurring benign message ambiguity and child-authored appropriate clarification, or healthy respectful response to independently different preference/stop, never engineered misunderstanding. A new real context can show stronger transfer only if truly witnessed, not compulsory publisher source. No coerced apology, agreement, personal touch or private friendship. Actual later-day REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED at authoring.

PK3 separate developmental stage work

PK3 C3-1: ENTRY familiar willing adult expresses authentic harmless question/preferences, child chooses actual response plus voluntary cue, MODEL different teacher adult demonstration of gentle misunderstanding, DURING child practices noncoercive clarification, EXIT new real adult and child-origin context-appropriate language/style/nonverbal cue. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-2: ENTRY genuine consenting familiar peer offers new safe art suggestion, child may reply with own words/sign/AAC and voluntary offering gesture; MODEL adult colleague disagreement on other material, DURING supported peer interaction only by choice, EXIT other actual peer context with child-origin message/gesture and peer response. Forced peer greeting invalid. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-3: ENTRY separately new adult and willing peer project decisions, learner uses suitable own language/style/cues, MODEL unrelated teacher repair, DURING feedback without dictating exact phrase, EXIT new real separate adult and peer interactions with independently chosen relevant language/gesture, not card choices only. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-4: ENTRY real willing familiar person contexts as naturally available, MODEL unrelated two-adult gentle repair, DURING optional respectful clarity, and STRONGER cross-listener EXIT only when actual child-owned language/style/turn cues with BOTH familiar adult and consenting real familiar peer are separately witnessed. One observed type is LIMITED and other NOT_OBSERVED, not failed child. Real misunderstanding/repair is not a source precondition; REVISIT true future date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 real responsive conversation stages

PK4 C4-1: ENTRY new real optional shared project adult/peer asks question, child independently answers and voluntarily signals turn; MODEL different teacher colleague repair, DURING supported, EXIT fresh real partner child relevant response and cue. REVISIT later. PK4 C4-2: ENTRY partner really proposes an unexpected safe alternative, child meaningfully acknowledges/replies, MODEL teacher colleague offers contrasting idea, DURING learner practices content-responsiveness not copying phrase, EXIT new actual human alternative and child own contingent reply. REVISIT later. PK4 C4-3: ENTRY if real partner NATURALLY does not understand a harmless message, learner may genuinely clarify own meaning; if partner merely wants different safe art arrangement, learner can acknowledge and respectfully disagree without apology. MODEL unrelated teacher-owned ambiguity, DURING support without writing the learner words, EXIT meaningful actual human exchange with natural turn/stop, optionally authentic repair when needed. No compulsory question or novel conflict; REVISIT later. PK4 C4-4: ENTRY new real genuine conversation on safe project, child responds across actual partner feedback, respects stop and may use child-chosen access/turn signal; MODEL teacher with separate consenting partner, DURING helps semantic repair, EXIT new genuine reciprocal responsive exchange beyond rigid memorized lines. Honor partner wish to stop (do not mark child failed), REVISIT later. PK4 C4-5: ENTRY safe real familiar or changed willing person without pre-script, MODEL teacher separate adult example after diagnosis, DURING supportive practice, and source EXIT child-origin real human meaningful responsive language/sign/AAC with culturally valid natural turn/pauses/closing. A real need to clarify, another peer independent preference or stop can invite richer ethical repair/boundary method evidence ONLY if spontaneously happens, not required to award source. New topic/person adds transfer only when observed; REVISIT true later date NOT_OBSERVED.

Until implemented, future SOLO is practice with source component NOT_OBSERVED; no fabricated efficacy parity, no working app now. Child first-origin U/H/A/X and genuine later-date REVISIT required. Adult moving AAC board at child's chosen message A vs creating message H; new unmodeled exit and later true date. ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Teacher privately observes actual new adult interaction before MODEL, demonstrates with different teacher/partner materials, DURING gives separate gentle practice, and arranges fresh safe real PK3 familiar adult+peer interactions or PK4 real reciprocal human interaction. PK4 authentic child-origin meaningful human response/turn can count without engineered confusion, surprise or question. Adult intervenes in true bullying/denial of shared school resources but cannot demand personal friendship, apology, touching a peer or continuing conversation after stop. Neither teacher puppet chorus nor teacher-directed group phrase is source.

Method-specific adversarial defect and concrete repair

False-positive: Adult and puppets perform “Hello! How are you? Fine!” while child taps scripted cue; an actual teacher/peer is coached to purposely misunderstand child or stage a rejection; or a real partner says stop and assessor pressures them to continue for sufficient turns. None is authentic spontaneous repair or consent-respecting source. Why invalid: scripted/fake people, adult-origin contents and unsafe refusal of partner boundary—not real child reciprocal interaction. Repair: model an harmless adult-only ambiguity with genuinely consenting colleagues and unrelated materials, then separately observe naturally available real child-authored language/turn responses with familiar adult or willing peer PK3; if a person is absent record limited actual human setting and other NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 genuine human conversation and natural stop do not require original follow-up or real conflict. For stronger repair METHOD, record only naturally arisen unclear meaning and child-owned clarification; a legitimately different preference is not misunderstanding. If partner stops after already interacting, preserve bounded source and honor no continuation. Limit: real-life family/peer availability and authentic surprise require human contextual evaluation; a fake simulated dialog cannot pass even if structurally perfect.

November 1 retrospective reciprocal-communication remediation

PK3 — genuine person, learner-owned contextual reply

TEACH / ACCESS / FADE: Use safe respectful real listener difference, adult and separate willing familiar peer; learner may clarify own accessible meaning or signal stop without compulsory apology, eye contact or confrontation.

UNMODELED real-human response: New benign listener difference with child-origin clarification or respectful boundary and voluntary expressive/nonverbal turn cue to actual adult and real familiar peer.

False credit and autonomy: Forcing reluctant peer participation or requiring a gaze-and-nod after adult dictates correction is social compliance, not independent source.

PK4 — genuine person, learner-owned contextual reply

TEACH / ACCESS / FADE: Arrange genuine small harmless different preference with consenting real human, then allow partner to correct, pause or stop; child responds meaningfully through own voice/sign/AAC rather than exact apology script.

UNMODELED real-human response: Novel actual human content/misunderstanding elicits independent relevant child clarification/alternative and respectful ending signal when listener asks to stop.

False credit and autonomy: Adult demands invented minimum turn count after partner requests stop or scripts “fine thank you” buttons, defeating conversational rule/source.

A real willing person is needed for full authentic source observation. CO-PLAY caregiver can provide real adult PK3 context and real PK4 reciprocal exchange; a caregiver imitating another child is not a familiar peer. ADULT-LED must arrange distinct real willing familiar peer for PK3 (or leave NOT_OBSERVED) and support AAC without selecting learner content. CLASSROOM can observe private individual authentic consented conversations with actual real people, not chorus or forced partner participation. Child and partner may stop, culturally varied non-gaze cues are valid, and future real dates, external educator review, operational SOLO and four-mode parity remain unverified.

Companion 03 — Real language misunderstanding and self-authored repair

Pair: Standalone lesson 03; objective educator guide. This is specific adult teaching guidance, NOT an additional counted plan.

Specific mechanism

The DISTINCT REPAIR METHOD arises if a real willing human genuinely misunderstands a benign ambiguous message and child clarifies own meaning; a legitimately DIFFERENT preference is not an error requiring apology, and a personal stop must be respected. Publisher II.B.2 source can occur in a real human conversation without any repair, so absence of disagreement does not indicate learner deficiency. This practice teaches communication repair and boundary respect, not forced apology, compliance or staged social rejection.

Real safe materials and participant consent

Prepare large benign selectable art arrangements with legitimate alternative preferences, safe extra materials, consented adult plus familiar peer for PK3 at different opportunities, quiet/no-touch option and personal AAC/sign/home-language access. Do not intentionally ridicule, reject, deceive or pressure anyone to misunderstand or refuse the learner; never engineer a real child repair occasion, never make conflict that targets identity, health or fear. Adult MODEL may use a fictional ambiguous phrase with another consenting adult on unrelated props, kept separate from the real learner EXIT.

ENTRY, MODEL, DURING, novel independent EXIT and later REVISIT

ENTRY: accept a naturally occurring genuine low-stakes clarification opportunity in a willing real conversation; do not require child to experience confrontation. MODEL: on unrelated adult art, one adult says “put it there,” other genuinely asks which of two safe places, adult clarifies and honors listener choice. DURING: let child independently restate, point, sign or compose AAC message if listener didn't understand, listen to real listener's reply and adjust safe shared decision; fade 'say please' and scripted apology demands. EXIT: independently witness any genuine safe real human conversation, familiar or changed; PK3 adult/peer familiar listener types recorded separately, one limited source while unavailable other NOT_OBSERVED; PK4 real meaningful contingent human response and natural turn/stop without compulsory clarification or conflict. If a benign misunderstanding actually arises, observe optional child-owned repair, and if partner has a different valid preference accept respectful nonconvergence. Novel situation strengthens transfer only if observed, not printed source cutoff. REVISIT real different date, new willing partner and harmless misunderstanding/context, NOT_OBSERVED.

Feedback for false credit and authentic misconceptions

If learner or real partner wishes to stop, honor. If no real conversational exchange happened, that context is NOT_OBSERVED, not failed child; if they already exchanged genuine meaning, preserve bounded witnessed real source, and leave unperformed extended clarification/transfer NOT_OBSERVED. A forced teacher-written apology is not child-authored reciprocal conversation. If partner isn't familiar/willing for PK3, don't falsely claim both types. Do not turn silence, indirect gaze or AAC choice into social pathology.

Grade-specific source and original outcome boundaries

PK3.II.B.2: Child-origin appropriate language/style and natural voluntary nonverbal/sign/AAC turn cues during real willing familiar adult and real familiar peer contexts are observed SEPARATELY as available; one witnessed human setting is limited publisher source, other NOT_OBSERVED, no simultaneous new EXIT prerequisite. Adult impersonating a peer is invalid. No requirement to stare at faces, speak English only, shake hands, or expose private personal details. PK4.II.B.2: Child actually engages in a reciprocal natural conversation with a REAL willing human, respecting meaningful verbal/nonverbal rules in the chosen cultural/communication context. Real partner independently chosen meaning, child personally responsive language/turn and naturally appropriate stop are essential; an original question, clarification, changing partner idea or apology is optional richer REPAIR method evidence, never universal publisher eligibility. A memorized line count or cartoon speech-bubble choice cannot prove authentic conversation. Partner and learner retain the right to stop. U child-origin meaning, respectful disagreement/turn choice; H adult-authored apology/content; A faithful child-directed interpreter or physical AAC activation without inventing meaning; X uncertain. Source familiar context possible; changed actual partner/material stronger transfer, future REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. The classroom play is instruction, not proof of real child learning.

Seven differentiated access pathways and four written delivery modes

Attention/executive: predictable accessible turns and optional break, fade turn cards; language/communication: child-origin expressive home-language, sign or AAC with meaningful real human understanding; literacy/phonology: reading/speech-bubble decoding NOT a prerequisite; numeracy: no rigid count of lines or gaze seconds; autism/sensory: low-arousal quiet partner opportunities and no forced gaze or touch; intellectual/developmental: adult-only concrete modeling before independent actual new dialogue; physical/motor/hearing/vision: accessible materials, neutral hearing/vision/communication support, genuine learner-directed turn signal and individualized specialist verification without claiming equivalence from adult-authored words. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: prerecorded/chatbot people can rehearse but do not qualify as real consenting human conversation for source. CO-PLAY: real caregiver may satisfy adult conversation; PK3 still needs distinct genuine familiar peer participation. ADULT-LED: actual non-scripted listener rather than teacher cue completes adult opportunity, with peer opportunity separately for PK3. CLASSROOM: under actual available staffing supervise all others and observe only feasible focal genuine individual human exchanges, other learner or listener contexts deferred NOT_OBSERVED. No staged class misunderstanding, coerced apologies, choral source or public rankings. No actual child outcome, professional acceptance, performed later REVISIT, field print or functional four-mode parity is claimed.
