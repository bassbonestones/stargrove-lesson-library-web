SHARE a safe large object

USE AAC/sign to signal a turn

02 · Different ideas, clarification and stop

Deliberate adult-only practice example, not actual child performance · Original E2.2 II.B.2 adult teaching/observation packet · NOT child data

ADULT model — two genuinely consenting humans

Adult A: “I thought the large circle could go beside the tray.” Adult B (with own safe preference): “I was wondering about putting it on the mat.” Adult A: “I heard your idea. Which should we try?” Adult B may genuinely answer or choose to stop; both are respected.

The lesson teaches how a message may adapt to a real response, not a universal politeness script or fixed number of dialogue lines. If partner says stop, listening to that boundary matters more than extracting one more assessment turn.
