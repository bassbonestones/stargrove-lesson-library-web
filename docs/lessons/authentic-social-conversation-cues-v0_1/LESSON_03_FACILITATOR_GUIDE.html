Companion 03 — Real language misunderstanding and self-authored repair

Pair: Standalone lesson 03; objective educator guide. This is specific adult teaching guidance, NOT an additional counted plan.

Specific mechanism

The DISTINCT REPAIR METHOD arises if a real willing human genuinely misunderstands a benign ambiguous message and child clarifies own meaning; a legitimately DIFFERENT preference is not an error requiring apology, and a personal stop must be respected. Publisher II.B.2 source can occur in a real human conversation without any repair, so absence of disagreement does not indicate learner deficiency. This practice teaches communication repair and boundary respect, not forced apology, compliance or staged social rejection.

Real safe materials and participant consent

Prepare large benign selectable art arrangements with legitimate alternative preferences, safe extra materials, consented adult plus familiar peer for PK3 at different opportunities, quiet/no-touch option and personal AAC/sign/home-language access. Do not intentionally ridicule, reject, deceive or pressure anyone to misunderstand or refuse the learner; never engineer a real child repair occasion, never make conflict that targets identity, health or fear. Adult MODEL may use a fictional ambiguous phrase with another consenting adult on unrelated props, kept separate from the real learner EXIT.

ENTRY, MODEL, DURING, novel independent EXIT and later REVISIT

ENTRY: accept a naturally occurring genuine low-stakes clarification opportunity in a willing real conversation; do not require child to experience confrontation. MODEL: on unrelated adult art, one adult says “put it there,” other genuinely asks which of two safe places, adult clarifies and honors listener choice. DURING: let child independently restate, point, sign or compose AAC message if listener didn't understand, listen to real listener's reply and adjust safe shared decision; fade 'say please' and scripted apology demands. EXIT: independently witness any genuine safe real human conversation, familiar or changed; PK3 adult/peer familiar listener types recorded separately, one limited source while unavailable other NOT_OBSERVED; PK4 real meaningful contingent human response and natural turn/stop without compulsory clarification or conflict. If a benign misunderstanding actually arises, observe optional child-owned repair, and if partner has a different valid preference accept respectful nonconvergence. Novel situation strengthens transfer only if observed, not printed source cutoff. REVISIT real different date, new willing partner and harmless misunderstanding/context, NOT_OBSERVED.

Feedback for false credit and authentic misconceptions

If learner or real partner wishes to stop, honor. If no real conversational exchange happened, that context is NOT_OBSERVED, not failed child; if they already exchanged genuine meaning, preserve bounded witnessed real source, and leave unperformed extended clarification/transfer NOT_OBSERVED. A forced teacher-written apology is not child-authored reciprocal conversation. If partner isn't familiar/willing for PK3, don't falsely claim both types. Do not turn silence, indirect gaze or AAC choice into social pathology.

Grade-specific source and original outcome boundaries

PK3.II.B.2: Child-origin appropriate language/style and natural voluntary nonverbal/sign/AAC turn cues during real willing familiar adult and real familiar peer contexts are observed SEPARATELY as available; one witnessed human setting is limited publisher source, other NOT_OBSERVED, no simultaneous new EXIT prerequisite. Adult impersonating a peer is invalid. No requirement to stare at faces, speak English only, shake hands, or expose private personal details. PK4.II.B.2: Child actually engages in a reciprocal natural conversation with a REAL willing human, respecting meaningful verbal/nonverbal rules in the chosen cultural/communication context. Real partner independently chosen meaning, child personally responsive language/turn and naturally appropriate stop are essential; an original question, clarification, changing partner idea or apology is optional richer REPAIR method evidence, never universal publisher eligibility. A memorized line count or cartoon speech-bubble choice cannot prove authentic conversation. Partner and learner retain the right to stop. U child-origin meaning, respectful disagreement/turn choice; H adult-authored apology/content; A faithful child-directed interpreter or physical AAC activation without inventing meaning; X uncertain. Source familiar context possible; changed actual partner/material stronger transfer, future REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. The classroom play is instruction, not proof of real child learning.

Seven differentiated access pathways and four written delivery modes

Attention/executive: predictable accessible turns and optional break, fade turn cards; language/communication: child-origin expressive home-language, sign or AAC with meaningful real human understanding; literacy/phonology: reading/speech-bubble decoding NOT a prerequisite; numeracy: no rigid count of lines or gaze seconds; autism/sensory: low-arousal quiet partner opportunities and no forced gaze or touch; intellectual/developmental: adult-only concrete modeling before independent actual new dialogue; physical/motor/hearing/vision: accessible materials, neutral hearing/vision/communication support, genuine learner-directed turn signal and individualized specialist verification without claiming equivalence from adult-authored words. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: prerecorded/chatbot people can rehearse but do not qualify as real consenting human conversation for source. CO-PLAY: real caregiver may satisfy adult conversation; PK3 still needs distinct genuine familiar peer participation. ADULT-LED: actual non-scripted listener rather than teacher cue completes adult opportunity, with peer opportunity separately for PK3. CLASSROOM: under actual available staffing supervise all others and observe only feasible focal genuine individual human exchanges, other learner or listener contexts deferred NOT_OBSERVED. No staged class misunderstanding, coerced apologies, choral source or public rankings. No actual child outcome, professional acceptance, performed later REVISIT, field print or functional four-mode parity is claimed.
