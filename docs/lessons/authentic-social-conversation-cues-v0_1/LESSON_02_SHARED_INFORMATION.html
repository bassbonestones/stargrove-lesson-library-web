Method 02 — Actual Partner-Only Information Gap and Listener Confirmation

Exact published original sources, 2022 Texas Domain II.B.2: PK3.II.B.2 “Child begins to use appropriate language, style, and nonverbal cues during communication with familiar adults and peers.” SHA 60f155c8d46353c515fbaa728cc28eec0a7cd930a821e9fe23555d5da2d95866; PK4.II.B.2 “Child engages in conversations in appropriate ways, demonstrating knowledge of verbal and nonverbal conversational rules.” SHA b6f150a5930ad5b59eb27ffcebde9bd2aa9489d3a720121f10eb90b22c6b4939. Original printed literacy research PDF index 20 verified against registry, alternate-PDF concordance pending. See DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md.

Distinct mechanism: an authentic human listener does not yet know a safe source fact or project change, and learns it through the child's independent language; conversely child may need a real partner's information and respond to what was genuinely learned. This DISTINCT METHOD explores truthful child-owned information crossing an actual benign knowledge gap, but a listener already knowing the fact does not erase a separately observed genuine publisher-relevant human conversation. PK3 actual familiar adult and consenting familiar peer contexts are documented independently across available real opportunities: one witnessed type yields LIMITED real communication evidence, missing other remains NOT_OBSERVED, not failure. PK4 real contingent conversation need not include a new question or surprise. Unlike Method01 free joint preference or Method03 repair, true unknown-to-listener information adds a specific higher-ceiling mechanism, not a new official eligibility gate.

Real safety and precise setup

Use large clean safe tactile shapes, stable board and accessible receiving zone. Let child independently perceive/choose a benign fact about large object arrangement through their own accessible visual, tactile or other safe modality. Real consenting familiar human listener may voluntarily turn away during a nonprivate change, WITHOUT blindfolding, frightening or testing private information. Before labeling this METHOD a genuine knowledge gap, an educator neutrally confirms the listener did not already see, hear or learn the specific benign fact; never ask another child to pretend ignorance or script the listener question. Both may withdraw. Ask an optional willing familiar peer separately for PK3 peer opportunity; never blindfold or force anyone, and no private identifying/home facts. Child witnesses harmless large object move so their information genuinely differs from listener's, then may independently communicate “The round tile is beside the box,” sign/expressive AAC equivalent, and point/offer large tile as voluntary cue (not compulsory eye contact). A pretend puppet “who didn't see” is not live source. For PK4 a listener MAY naturally ask “Which box?” or offer another genuine reply and learner can respond; no original question, precise preselected spatial word or unexpected surprise is REQUIRED by the official PK4 conversation source. Child-origin language/sign/AAC and culturally accessible natural turn/pauses/closing with a real independent human response are central. A correct location alone does not establish conversation; if the child uses a different truthful safe description or tactile fact, evaluate actual meaningful exchange separately from exact author tile-placement success. Record actual listener words/signs and learner-owned response.

ENTRY before MODEL: Set a new human source gap with safe objects and one willing partner. Without supplying desired wording, adult offers open invitation “You can show/tell me what changed if you want.” Observe child original message, how listener responded, own nonverbal attention/turn cue and whether any adult staged the source content. PK3 record familiar adult context and later separately real familiar peer; if peer never willing mark NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 record actual first live contingent conversation, not a fiction of partner prewritten response.

MODEL using completely DIFFERENT teacher-owned safe scene: Teacher hides another large textured star behind a new box while different real colleague did not observe, then truthfully informs colleague and follows their actual question. Demonstrate appropriate voluntary pointing to common object and handover cue, but no gaze demand. Adult explains that messages can help somebody learn what really happened and may need clarification; teacher-owned adult model not child-source evidence.

DURING: Child can send accurate information about different safe item to genuinely unaware adult/peer and see listener check it, or ask partner relevant question and adjust message after real answer. Teacher should not mouth words, point to correct item, dictate social phrase or operate AAC semantic choice. A teacher may help motor-device location only as child directs A, not select actual sentence H. If child merely repeats a teacher report, H practice; later new source. If listener already knew the fact or was scripted, the distinctive information-gap METHOD has NOT been demonstrated; if they genuinely converse with the child, retain only that actual bounded II.B.2 source evidence and never award a performed knowledge-gap success. Offer another optional neutral benign fact with truly independently uninformed consenting listener, without claiming that a new task/partner is official prerequisite. A child may decline, partner may stop, both respected.

EXIT — actual independent communication with truthful OPTIONAL method gap: When safe willing human listeners naturally exist, separately record what PK3 child personally communicates with real familiar adult and with real familiar peer, even across different occasions; absent type NOT_OBSERVED, never a child failure. PK4 child engages an ACTUAL person with meaningful independently chosen partner content, child-origin relevant reply and culturally natural sign/AAC/turn/pause/closing; a spontaneous listener question and learner follow-up may extend this lesson, but are not compulsory source tests. For DISTINCT information-gap METHOD success, additionally verify safe benign fact truly known to child through accessible route and NOT already known to partner, and record genuine human information transfer; if listener already knew, method inference not earned while real conversation may still be limited source. A familiar topic/person can be a valid II.B.2 observation; new fact/listener/topic tests stronger transfer only if actually performed. Either person may stop. REVISIT on truly later calendar date remains NOT_OBSERVED now.

PK3 authentic developmental routes

PK3 C3-1: ENTRY real familiar adult unaware of safe object change, child may independently offer language and voluntary cue, MODEL separate adult-only information gap, DURING practice with alternate object, EXIT another actual adult and new true message/cue, not teacher words. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-2: ENTRY willing familiar peer truly did not see benign object move, child communicates meaning/style and cue only if willing, MODEL different adult colleague setup, DURING adult supports access without giving text, EXIT new actual peer situation and child-authored language/nonverbal turn signal, with response verified. Peer may refuse safely. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-3: ENTRY different true info gaps separately for real familiar adult and familiar consenting peer, MODEL another unrelated adult original, DURING teacher demonstrates how message can adapt to listener's actual confusion without exact “please”, EXIT new contexts with both real people and first child-origin cue/message. Peer not there = missing component. REVISIT later. PK3 C3-4: ENTRY actual safe familiar adult/peer opportunities as available, MODEL unrelated teacher-only partner after first response, DURING supported, and STRONGER cross-context EXIT documents authentic child-owned meaningful communication/style and naturally voluntary turn cues with BOTH familiar listener types only when each occurs. One actual listener remains limited source, missing other NOT_OBSERVED; a human listener who already knew the fact can still provide actual communication but not proven information-gap-method success. REVISIT different real date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK4 reciprocal developmental routes

PK4 C4-1: ENTRY real willing partner shares harmless object fact, learner responds with original relevant language/AAC and voluntary turn cue, MODEL another adult listener, DURING supported meaning, EXIT new real first responsive partner event, foundational. REVISIT later. PK4 C4-2: ENTRY partner unexpectedly reports own safe object observation, learner actually acknowledges that specific information, MODEL on separate adult exchange, DURING practice with truthful source, EXIT changed real human statement and independently meaningful response/cue. A fixed “yeah!” for everything doesn't show contingent understanding. REVISIT later. PK4 C4-3: ENTRY real consenting partner may ask a genuinely motivated question about a benign unknown fact and child can respond accurately through own accessible language; learner MAY ask relevant original follow-up as richer information-gap dialog. MODEL other adult separate truthful gap, DURING supported practice H, EXIT real relevant human contingent response without mandatory question, new partner or exact spatial word. Source and method inference separately recorded; REVISIT later real date NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 C4-4: ENTRY real partner shares alternative safe location for project, learner hears content and engages in interactive decision with meaningful voluntary turn cues and consent to pause, MODEL different teacher other source, DURING supports, EXIT new reciprocal partner exchange beyond one scripted turn. The “two child contingent turns” is authored representative evidence, not official numerical Texas target. REVISIT later. PK4 C4-5: ENTRY true consenting human conversation before adult MODEL, DURING help after diagnostic, source EXIT child initiates/responds meaningfully to a REAL person and respects conversational turns/pauses/closing in accessible language. If a genuinely child-known safe fact crosses an independently verified unaware partner, the information-gap method earns its distinct richer inference; unexpected reply, self-origin question and new partner are OPTIONAL extensions, not publisher thresholds. REVISIT later true date NOT_OBSERVED.

Until implemented, future SOLO is practice with source component NOT_OBSERVED; no fabricated efficacy parity, no working app now. Child first-origin U/H/A/X and genuine later-date REVISIT required. Adult moving AAC board at child's chosen message A vs creating message H; new unmodeled exit and later true date. ADULT-LED ONE CHILD: Teacher privately observes actual new adult interaction before MODEL, demonstrates with different teacher/partner materials, DURING gives separate gentle practice, and arranges fresh safe real PK3 familiar adult+peer interactions or PK4 real reciprocal human interaction. TEACHER-LED CLASSROOM: An actual available adult supervises all pupils while a teacher observes a FEASIBLE few focal real human conversations per interval; others safely choose quiet or independent art and are not falsely marked. Record only truly individually witnessed PK3 adult context and real willing familiar peer context separately (missing type NOT_OBSERVED), or PK4 actual human responsive meaning/culturally appropriate turn without mandatory question. A harmless actual gap requires child-known accessible fact and verifiably unaware voluntary listener, NOT a coerced peer or scripted actor. Rotate/defer when willing people, supervision or access unavailable. Never infer 24 source actions from attendance, chorus, fictional puppet or global teacher model.

Safety, accessibility and integrity in every mode

ENTRY before any teacher MODEL, MODEL on different object/listener interaction, supported DURING, genuine independent new EXIT with real willing human partner(s), actual future-date REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. Grade PK3 must show emerging appropriate child language/style and meaningful nonverbal/turn cue with a familiar willing adult AND a willing familiar peer in real encounters; grade PK4 must actually show meaningful reciprocal contingent conversation with another consenting human including verbal/sign/AAC content AND nonverbal/turn-taking signals (eyes/gestures not dictated). First-origin U independent, H conceptual/social content supplied, A only motor/device access under child meaning, X unclear. Respect right to stop and cultural/individual communication norms; signed language or AAC can itself contain appropriate nonverbal attention/turn signals, no arbitrary gaze or extra unnecessary gesture required. New real partner sources never replaced by fictional AI/social game. Only internal authored files and synthetic tests; no actual children, independent qualified educator review, real future retention, physical print, operational SOLO or cross-mode effectiveness proven.

Method-specific adversarial false-credit and correction

False-positive: A child truthfully tells a willing adult “the square is over there,” but the adult already watched the move: this MAY be a real communicative exchange yet is NOT evidence of the distinctive unknown-to-listener knowledge-gap method. A different wrong case is a cardboard “peer” voiced by a teacher, which cannot be genuine familiar peer communication. Repair: teacher models with unrelated genuine adult and checks an accessible benign fact the learner actually knows while a voluntary real human listener truly does NOT; child originates information and meaningful natural turn cue without memorized phrase. Distinguish real publisher language/conversation evidence from stronger verified transfer of unknown fact. PK3 actual adult and peer contexts recorded separately over available opportunities; PK4 genuine contingent dialog without compulsory follow-up question. Partner content and actual response recorded. Limitation: hidden knowledge, real consent and meaning attribution need competent observation; low-quality actor script can fake reciprocity despite tests, so external live child trial essential.

Companion 02 — Authentic information gap and contingent response

Pair: Standalone lesson 02; objective educator guide. This is specific adult teaching guidance, NOT an additional counted plan.

Specific mechanism

The learner knows a harmless actual object arrangement change that an independently willing human listener has not genuinely seen; an accurate personally composed message closes real shared information gap. If the listener naturally asks a genuine question, the learner can respond in context; a follow-up question/clarification is an OPTIONAL method enrichment, not printed II.B.2 source prerequisite. The distinctive method examines independently verified unknown-to-listener benign information; actual human contextual dialogue remains source relevant even if a particular gap was not achieved. This relies on an information source/verification relation rather than general art co-design or disagreement.

Real safe materials and participant consent

Offer a large safe clean art object arrangement or another accessible harmless visual/tactile fact, known by child through authentic own observation. A willing listener can choose to turn away from change without blindfolding, fear, secrets or staged rejection. Verify neutral actual partner unawareness without scripting a pretend surprise, and check child actually knows the benign fact; no private family data. Ensure learner has genuinely seen the changed large object and listener has freely agreed to ask. Prepare unrelated adult MODEL arrangement with separate props, guided DURING practice arrangements and new unknown EXIT arrangement. For PK3 recruit different willing real familiar adult and peer occasions, not one actor pretending both.

ENTRY, MODEL, DURING, novel independent EXIT and later REVISIT

ENTRY: show child a truthful new harmless visual/tactile object change, let them choose own true message to listener who did not see it. MODEL: consenting adults on separate objects honestly exchange “The blue shape moved beside the large square” and invite unscripted clarification. DURING: child originates actual informative turn through own home language/AAC/sign, real listener asks an unexpected but safe question, child responds authentically; fade leading location words and adult pointing. EXIT: genuine independently observed human communication, even on familiar safe topic/person, can support source evidence. For this DISTINCT method also check child genuinely owns an accessible benign fact the consenting listener did NOT know; a witnessed truthful information transfer strengthens inference, but a failed gap does not erase actual contextual conversation. PK3 listener adult/peer types separately across available natural opportunities, absent type NOT_OBSERVED. PK4 real conversational response and turn/closing without mandatory extra question. New unknown scenario/partner is stronger transfer only when actually witnessed, not official threshold. REVISIT: genuinely later calendar day with fresh actual true information gap, NOT_OBSERVED.

Feedback for false credit and authentic misconceptions

If child reports a change they never saw because adult told them what to say, informational authorship is absent. If partner already knew the fact, there is no information-gap METHOD evidence, though genuine observed child–human contextual conversation remains possible publisher source; offer another voluntary actual unknown benign fact without concealing this distinction or coercing a listener. If a peer or child declines before actual exchange, respect them and mark that source context NOT_OBSERVED; if they already genuinely communicated, record bounded witnessed language/turn rule evidence without compelling further turns or falsely claiming a completed knowledge gap. Adult moving a learner-controlled pointer is neutral A; writing child message or follow-up is conceptual H.

Grade-specific source and original outcome boundaries

PK3.II.B.2: A child begins appropriate context-sensitive language/style and meaningful naturally voluntary nonverbal/sign/AAC turn cues with familiar human adults and peers. Each actual familiar listener TYPE is recorded separately whenever observed across opportunities; a single witnessed setting is LIMITED source evidence, other NOT_OBSERVED, while an adult impersonating a peer is invalid. No requirement to stare at faces, speak English only, shake hands, or expose private personal details. PK4.II.B.2: Child actually engages in a reciprocal natural conversation with a REAL willing human, respecting meaningful verbal/nonverbal rules in the chosen cultural/communication context. Genuine real human partner meaning, child-created contingent reply and natural culturally appropriate conversational cue/pausing are essential; an ORIGINAL question/clarification is optional richer information-gap engagement, NOT an official requirement. A memorized line count or cartoon speech-bubble choice cannot prove authentic conversation. Partner and learner retain the right to stop. U child-owned semantic fact/turn choice; H adult-authored answer/meaning; A neutral faithful interpretation/transliteration or physical device press selected by child without inventing content; X unclear provenance. Distinguish true method gap from natural human conversation, familiar source from stronger new context, and genuinely later REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. The classroom play is instruction, not proof of real child learning.
