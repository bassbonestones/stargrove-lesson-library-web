Developmental source ladder: genuine adult/peer communication and real reciprocal exchange

PK4.II.B.2 “Child engages in conversations in appropriate ways, demonstrating knowledge of verbal and nonverbal conversational rules.” SHA b6f150a5930ad5b59eb27ffcebde9bd2aa9489d3a720121f10eb90b22c6b4939. We must not invent an official number of conversational turns or require eye contact, speech, physical contact, prescribed politeness norms, neurotypical social style or compulsory peer contact. Source action is child-origin meaning in actual interactions, not selecting a puppet conversation card.

Real people/consent: Familiar willing adults and familiar willing peers create genuine different contexts for PK3; a real willing conversational partner, adult or peer, is needed for PK4 source. If willing familiar peer is not present, PK3 peer-context source remains NOT_OBSERVED, but genuine witnessed familiar ADULT child communication should still be recorded as a limited observed source-relevant action, not misdiagnosed child failure; overall cross-context claim needs the genuinely separate real peer when available. If only a bot, a teacher script or a recorded dialogue is present, PK4 live reciprocity remains NOT_OBSERVED. Never force peer socialization; a learner can stop, a partner can withdraw and the observation remains incomplete. Use neutral open safe co-building/book/art topic, not private family, identity, home, health or other sensitive information. A child-origin home-language expression, sign and expressive AAC with semantic meaning is language; meaningful voluntary nonverbal cues can include offering a safe large object, pointing to shared project, turning toward an available communication board or indicating “your turn,” without eye-contact requirement. A sign may be linguistic; don't require a separate arbitrary physical cue if the child is already independently using meaningful conversational attention/turn signals through sign/AAC. Adult prompting where to look or speak is H, not independent.

Authentic assessment phases at each stage: ENTRY first new voluntary safe interaction before teacher MODEL, separately record actual adult and peer listener roles, first child-origin language/style/meaningful cue or real partner contingent conversational action; MODEL adult demonstrates with DIFFERENT teacher-owned partner/materials while child not graded; DURING guided supported roleplay/real participant practice, adult prompts H separated from access A; EXIT authentic safe familiar or newly changed real willing partner(s)/topic and child-origin appropriate meaningful action with responsive listener (PK3 record actual adult and peer contexts separately as available; if one unavailable mark it NOT_OBSERVED, PK4 real reciprocal exchange and verbal/nonverbal rule knowledge, no compulsory question/surprise). Changed context is stronger transfer only IF actually seen, not a universal published source gate; REVISIT genuinely other calendar date with new voluntary actual real social communication, currently NOT_OBSERVED. Provenance U child-independent, H adult supplies semantic/social action, A child-directed motor/AAC access-only, X unclear. If no partner or unsafe/coercive circumstance, NOT_OBSERVED. Child may respond with emerging words in PK3, not rigid full-sentence discourse; PK4 meaningful contingent turns and acknowledging listener's actual content, not fixed multi-turn numeric mandate. Later transfer requires real observation.

Prerequisite teaching: establish safety/consent, child can access expressive modality and understand partner's language, recognize optional familiar person and allow stop cues; teach simple communicative invitation/response with an adult first; then authentic willing peer exchange. PK4 builds from genuine relevant real human partner response and voluntary culturally appropriate turn/pause/closing, and can EXTEND to self-origin follow-up or clarification when meaningful, without imposing questions on every authentic conversation. If learner's communication mode is inaccessible to partner, supply interpreter or accessible communication aid without creating child content. Do not label sensory/attention differences as conversational deficits.

PK3 stages — source must involve familiar adult AND peer

PK3 C3-1 — safe communication with familiar adult

ENTRY: in genuine optional book/art project a familiar willing adult quietly asks open “What would you like to do?” or shares simple observation; child may independently respond with child-owned words/sign/AAC and voluntary attention gesture (e.g. points to chosen large piece). MODEL: teacher uses DIFFERENT adult partner/materials to show a friendly contextual message and offered object cue without requiring gaze. DURING: adult supports choice and child initiates safe expression, H when teacher supplies phrase. False-positive: child selects a prefilled puppet response and teacher counts actual adult exchange. Repair with other teacher demo, then fresh actual real adult interaction. Fresh EXIT: new harmless situation, authentic familiar adult listener responds to learner's spontaneous situational message and cue. REVISIT: other actual date NOT_OBSERVED. PK3 whole source not yet complete without peer context.

PK3 C3-2 — safe voluntary communication with willing familiar peer

ENTRY: new shared safe large art objects, a known peer who freely consents offers “Which piece should we use?”; assessed child independently replies or suggests meaningful next action and uses voluntary gesture/AAC to share item or signal turn. MODEL: adult with DIFFERENT consenting adult partner models a short invitation/response, not a peer's actual answer. DURING: teacher supports taking turns without compelling eye contact, touch or prescribed greeting. Misconception: peer reluctantly participates under adult pressure or adult scripts child reply; not independent. Fresh EXIT: new voluntarily consenting familiar peer and changed harmless activity where learner independently addresses/responds with real meaning and voluntary nonverbal communication cue, peer confirms receipt. REVISIT: later day, NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 C3-3 — vary style/cue across genuine adult and peer opportunities

ENTRY: separately observe child's first child-origin message and voluntary nonverbal cue to familiar adult and a consenting familiar peer in real safe collaboration; compare without ranking cultural style. MODEL: teacher uses other adult-owned project showing gentle offer/point/turn cue and meaning, never mandates eye gaze. DURING: child practices expressing relevant message differently for actual listeners, H and A logged. False-positive: child repeats the teacher's exact greeting and deliberately nods only after instruction, gets independent style/cue credit. Repair true child-choice in new real two-listener situations. Fresh EXIT: changed authentic adult and peer activities, recorded separate first child-origin meaning and context-appropriate voluntary cues. REVISIT: real later date NOT_OBSERVED.

PK3 C3-4 — full source transfer in new real relationships

ENTRY: safe new adult and peer opportunities both genuinely available and willing, record spontaneous emerging language/style and cues separately with listeners' actual response; if either missing, don't fabricate. MODEL: adult separate neutral encounter after ENTRY; DURING genuine supported practice; full cross-context EXIT when actually available: in safe authentic real adult and separate familiar consenting peer contexts, learner communicates own meaningful contextual message and natural nonverbal/sign/AAC/turn cue, observing actual individual responses. If only one genuinely willing type available, preserve LIMITED source evidence for that context and other type NOT_OBSERVED; do not force a same-day dual-listener test. A novel context can probe stronger transfer. A fictional friend cannot replace peer. REVISIT: different calendar date two genuinely safe interactions, NOT_OBSERVED. No forced sociability, universal politeness formula or exact word threshold.

PK4 stages — source is real contingent reciprocal conversation, not puppet lines

PK4 C4-1 — retrieve child-origin partner response and voluntary turn cue

ENTRY: new consenting real adult/peer raises harmless project question, learner independently expresses relevant meaning and voluntary turn/listening AAC/gesture cue; MODEL distinct teacher-owned safe chat demonstration, DURING supported response, fresh EXIT new actual partner exchange one relevant response and acknowledgment. This is prerequisite, not whole source. REVISIT later day.

PK4 C4-2 — respond to what REAL partner actually said

ENTRY: willing human partner makes previously unknown real suggestion about safe shared art; learner must acknowledge/respond to its actual content rather than recite predicted line. MODEL: teacher with another adult partner shows genuine “I heard your idea; we could…” and a voluntary listening/turn signal, DURING helps learner notice partner meaning not gaze norms. Misconception: child repeats two memorized utterances independent of partner's different reply. Repair on other real practice, new consenting partner response. Fresh EXIT: new genuine partner meaning, independent context-sensitive child reply and cue. REVISIT later actual day.

PK4 C4-3 — child asks meaningful follow-up or clarification in live dialogue

ENTRY: consenting partner makes real unexpected description/question during safe build, learner has an authentic gap/uncertainty and independently asks relevant question/clarification or responds meaningfully. MODEL: adult with different willing partner demonstrates asking for clarification without shame or fixed sentence. DURING: child may choose AAC/sign/voice, teacher never supplies exact answer during scored phase. Misconception: learner taps “why?” preset after all puppet lines with no interest or relation; repair live content-tied new question. Fresh EXIT: actual real partner's unseen message receives child-origin contingent follow-up and appropriate voluntary conversational cue. REVISIT later date.

PK4 C4-4 — sustain reciprocal shared exchange and respectfully end

ENTRY: real willing partner in new safe optional project engages with child's spontaneous information/question; observe child actually responds to partner's changing meaning across continued exchange, uses turn acknowledgment and respects partner wish to pause. MODEL: different teacher/partner demonstrate meaningful back-and-forth, safe stop, no forced eye contact. DURING: supported reciprocal exchanges, any adult coaching H. Misconception: number of scripted lines looks like conversation though child never listens/responds to actual partner. Repair real new partner with unpredictable benign reply and independent child follow-up. Fresh EXIT: new authentic voluntary conversation, actual source-relevant child contributions across partner response and voluntary turn cue; a couple contingent turns provide author sampling but no official cutoff. REVISIT later date.

PK4 C4-5 — full new conversational rule source

ENTRY: wholly new safe voluntary topic and willing responsive human, learner independently participates in authentic exchange with actual partner reaction; MODEL teacher with different partner/topics after first observation; DURING responsive practiced dialog and cue access with H/A. Extended design EXIT when safe real opportunity exists: a genuine familiar or new voluntary real human conversation in which learner responds contextually to actual partner meaning using appropriate turn/pause/closing conventions; new unanticipated partner idea and relevant learner follow-up are HIGHER-CEILING extended lesson observations when naturally elicited, not printed source minimum. If interaction does not arise, preserve honest NOT_OBSERVED, not assumed child failure. Scripted prerecorded avatar lines, choral answer, eye contact alone or copied formal pleasantry do not establish. REVISIT real different date, NOT_OBSERVED.

November 1 learning-differences remediation — real human partners and responsive communication

PK3 printed breadth references actual familiar willing ADULTS and separately real familiar willing PEERS over authentic opportunities, but each actual personally witnessed human-context communication may be meaningful partial source evidence without a mandatory same-lesson paired exit; an imaginary partner cannot fill absent context. PK4 requires genuine context-appropriate responsive conversation with real willing human, not scripted or puppeted line count; a meaningful brief two-way exchange may be source-relevant without a question/surprise, while sustained clarification is stronger optional challenge. Do not mandate eye gaze, speech-only grammar, physical contact, standard polite phrasing, fixed number of turns or compulsory peer social performance. Signs and AAC can express genuine linguistic and conversational turn cues without an extra externally imposed gesture.

Three meaningfully different teaching mechanisms

  1. Shared creative project turn taking: real adult/peer joint safe art decisions, exchanging objects and ideas with child-owned actual language and voluntary turn cues.
  2. Partner-only information gap: a truly uninformed real partner needs child-origin information or offers new facts, requiring genuine response and optionally child-authored follow-up, not scripted greetings.
  3. Meaningful repair/boundary exchange: real willing partner expresses misunderstanding, different opinion or stop request, learner practices responding constructively and independently choosing accessible conversational cues.

Source-fair contextual evidence and gradual conversational extension (2026-11-10)

The TEA 2022 PK3.II.B.2 wording describes emerging use of appropriate language, style and nonverbal cues in familiar adults AND peers communication environments; do NOT demand a fresh unmodeled adult-plus-peer double exit on one day before recording a real learner-origin adult-only or peer-only episode. Record exactly which genuine context was witnessed and the other NOT_OBSERVED, never declare child inability from absent partner. PK4.II.B.2 asks age-appropriate real conversations with verbal and nonverbal rules, NOT a required follow-up question, surprise, rigid line count, additional separate gesture, perfect speech or long duration. Genuine brief child-owned contingent human exchange may be a limited source candidate; optional more sustained creative art negotiation, novel topic and clarification can distinguish STRONGER designs and transfer. Child-selected AAC/sign including its natural communicative pause/turn convention qualifies without redundant nod; adult motor assistance for a child-selected symbol A, adult selecting actual message/turn H. No synthetic truth values prove child efficacy, consent, field completion or real learner mastery.
