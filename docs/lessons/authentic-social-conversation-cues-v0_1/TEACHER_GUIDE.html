Objective-level educator guide — Authentic human conversational language and cues

This is a source-specific overarching educator guide, NOT a counted lesson plan. Original 2022 Texas early-language source cells SHA-pinned in authoring and frozen foundation, alternate PDF concordance and qualified educator validation pending.

PK3.II.B.2 — appropriate contextual language, style and nonverbal cues with familiar adults AND peers

Published printed wording: “Child begins to use appropriate language, style, and nonverbal cues during communication with familiar adults and peers.” SHA-256 60f155c8d46353c515fbaa728cc28eec0a7cd930a821e9fe23555d5da2d95866. The child may genuinely communicate own relevant meaning/style and culturally appropriate nonverbal/turn communication in any actually observed familiar human setting; the published adult AND peer context breadth is supported by separately genuinely observed adult and familiar peer opportunities over time, NOT a compulsory both-in-one-new-EXIT barrier. If one is witnessed and the other unavailable, record authentic observed-context learning and missing second human context NOT_OBSERVED. A familiar actual adult and a familiar actual willing peer must be distinguished; a puppeteer playing the peer or adult simply pointing to a partner does not substitute. The correct style is context- and culture-sensitive, not compulsory direct gaze, touch, spoken English, formal greeting or fixed turn counts. Sign, home-language, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), personally meaningful gesture and accessible expressive mode can be valid when learner-origin meaning and listener interaction are real.

PK4.II.B.2 — reciprocal real conversational exchange, verbal and nonverbal rules

Published wording: “Child engages in conversations in appropriate ways, demonstrating knowledge of verbal and nonverbal conversational rules.” SHA-256 b6f150a5930ad5b59eb27ffcebde9bd2aa9489d3a720121f10eb90b22c6b4939. The learner engages with an ACTUAL willing human adult or peer who contributes unscripted meaningful information. Observe a genuine human partner's independently chosen message, child-origin relevant contingent reply and accessible age/culture-appropriate turn-taking, natural pause or boundary/closing. A self-authored relevant question/clarification, extended exchange or response to SURPRISING content is a stronger lesson extension, not a universal printed source prerequisite. Do not demand a fixed numerical number of conversational turns; language norms, pause durations and eye contact vary. A prerecorded conversation, a puppet, a chatbot, repeating teacher-supplied “good listening” lines or filling a transcript bubble is useful rehearsal but is NOT authentic source evidence. Partner can stop without penalty.

Three differentiated standalone methods, each with its own detailed facilitator guide

  1. Lesson 01: actual willing shared art co-decision + companion guide 01 builds genuine co-created choices with real negotiated replies.
  2. Lesson 02: real shared information gap + companion guide 02 creates a harmless unknown fact one willing human can honestly convey to another, separating a child-origin informative turn from adult composed scripts.
  3. Lesson 03: honest miscommunication repair and boundaries + companion guide 03 invites self-authored clarification of a real benign misunderstanding, respecting the partner's real independent meaning and opt-out.

Scaffolding, false positives and independent transfer

See DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE.md for PK3 C3-1–C3-4 and PK4 C4-1–C4-5. ENTRY observes child's first real response on a genuinely new harmless adult/peer communication opportunity before adult MODEL. MODEL uses two consenting adults on DIFFERENT material and a completely different original message; partner’s answer is authentic, not rehearsed by the child. DURING learner originates a real safe relevant message, listens via accessible modality and responds to actually unscripted human content; fade expected line cards, adult gestures and scripted praise. EXIT offers safe genuine familiar or newly changed listening contexts only when a real willing adult/peer is naturally available, records adult/peer evidence separately for PK3 without requiring both concurrently and observes real contingent meaningful human exchange PK4 without compulsory follow-up question. Changed new context supports stronger TRANSFER when actually witnessed; familiar source can remain limited source-relevant. Record first learner-authored message, voluntary nonverbal or AAC turn cue, whether listener genuinely responded, subsequent child response, adult prompting U/H/A/X and declined/unavailable opportunities. REVISIT must be a genuinely different calendar day with new real human context and no scripted outcome, NOT_OBSERVED until actual.

If child says own real relevant statement but adult dictates the accompanying wave/gesture, record independent language but not self-generated cue. If peer unavailable/declines, do not force one or score failure; report source component NOT_OBSERVED. A learner using gaze alternatives can communicate attention with hand, head, sign, AAC or stillness if genuinely meaningful; no compulsory eye contact. If familiar adult does entire back-and-forth, do not count child's silent presence as PK4 conversation. If learner plays with an imaginary friend, count practice but no actual human exchange.

Seven differentiated access pathways, consent and written delivery

Attention/executive: predictable child-selected partner choice, short turns, fade optional cues. Language/communication: personally meaningful home-language/sign/AAC access; translator conveys child-owned content not adult composition. Literacy/phonology: no reading/picture speech-bubble decoding requirement. Numeracy: fixed number of turns is not a source metric. Autism/sensory: genuine choice of quiet communicative interaction or pause, no forced gaze/touch. Intellectual/developmental: concrete real adult modeling on unrelated props, then independently originating small meaning. Physical/motor/hearing/vision: accessible partner distance, non-verbal signal and neutral child-directed access, qualified team review for valid meaning. Both partner and child retain right to stop. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: virtual avatars and speech bubbles can prepare but CANNOT demonstrate real willing human conversation. CO-PLAY: caregiver can be real familiar adult, not substitute for required PK3 peer. ADULT-LED: assessor must also gather actual peer opportunity for PK3. CLASSROOM: consented real peer choices and confidential individual observations, never group chorus or publicly assigned friend. No actual child outcome, qualified educator signoff, dated later REVISIT, physical print accessibility or operational four-mode parity has been shown.
