Companion 01 — Co-designed art exchange

Pair: Standalone lesson 01; objective educator guide. This is specific adult teaching guidance, NOT an additional counted plan.

Specific mechanism

Two actual consenting familiar people share real safe large art or construction materials and genuinely make independent creative decisions. Child owns a relevant message plus an optional self-authored conversational/turn cue, receives an authentic non-scripted reply and changes their contribution in response. This real co-decision differs from Lesson 02's information gap and Lesson 03's repair.

Real safe materials and participant consent

Prepare voluntary quiet art/build stations, large clean nonchoking blunt tiles, optional quiet place and child-owned AAC/sign/home language communication pathway. Across naturally available PK3 opportunities seek genuinely familiar adult and separately consenting familiar peer, each freely choosing to participate; if only one context is witnessed, record that limited real context, other type NOT_OBSERVED. No mandatory same-session two-person source gate, compulsory touch or gaze. Use separate adult-owned model supplies; new EXIT topic/partner is a stronger transfer option when safely available, not required for all source-relevant familiar human communication.

ENTRY, MODEL, DURING, novel independent EXIT and later REVISIT

ENTRY: observe a child's first actually original conversational turn about building art with a willing familiar human; PK3 record adult and peer occasions separately. MODEL: two consenting adults on different materials offer contrasting tile preferences and show independent responses without prescribing child reply. DURING: invite child to suggest own design and respond to actual real willing partner's different preference with own relevant content; adult can clarify meaning, then fade words, gestures and directed physical positions. EXIT: observe only actual freely chosen real conversation, familiar OR changed. PK3 record child-origin language/style and naturally meaningful nonverbal/sign/AAC/turn cues with real familiar adult and real familiar peer separately IF each safely occurs; one real context with other NOT_OBSERVED is limited source evidence, not child deficit. PK4 appropriately responds to natural independently chosen human content, using culturally acceptable turn/pause/closing; original follow-up or surprising topic supports stronger lesson extension but is not universal source cutoff. Both may stop. Changed partner/material tests stronger transfer IF seen, not source prerequisite. REVISIT: actual different calendar date, new real willing listener and topic, NOT_OBSERVED.

Feedback for false credit and authentic misconceptions

If partner agrees before listening and adult assigns the exact sentence to child, no authentic co-decision; model other adult-only spontaneous contrast, then use fresh materials. If child speaks without forced gaze, assess meaningful alternative turn cue and reciprocal communication, not eye position. If peer declines BEFORE any meaningful human exchange, respect no and record missing peer action NOT_OBSERVED. If a real invitation–decline–acknowledgment or earlier genuine conversation already occurred, record that limited actual communication WITHOUT implying continuing interaction or forcing more. A trusted aide physically pressing an AAC button CHOSEN by the child is access-only A if child meaning and turn are independently selected; an adult who chooses the message, reply or cue is H and cannot be credited as original child content.

Grade-specific source and original outcome boundaries

PK3.II.B.2: Child begins to use context-appropriate language/style AND personally meaningful voluntary nonverbal cues with familiar adults AND familiar peers. Both publisher-named familiar human listener TYPES matter for an overall cross-context claim, but one genuinely observed type gives narrower real source-relevant evidence, while the absent other type is NOT_OBSERVED rather than child failure; never invent a required single-session dual-exit. Adult impersonating a peer is invalid. No requirement to stare at faces, speak English only, shake hands, or expose private personal details. PK4.II.B.2: Child actually engages in a reciprocal natural conversation with a REAL willing human, respecting meaningful verbal/nonverbal rules in the chosen cultural/communication context. Real partner independently chooses genuine meaning and child responds meaningfully under appropriate conversational rules; an extra question/clarification or unexpectedly different proposal is an OPTIONAL richer lesson-level challenge, NOT a required original-source threshold. A memorized line count or cartoon speech-bubble choice cannot prove authentic conversation. Partner and learner retain the right to stop. U child-origin; H adult-authored meaning/turn choice; A neutral child-directed access including aide activation of a symbol personally chosen by child; X provenance uncertain. Grade EXIT can record familiar genuine source, whereas changed real partner/materials add STRONGER transfer only if observed; genuine delayed REVISIT cannot be invented. The classroom play is instruction, not proof of real child learning.

Seven differentiated access pathways and four written delivery modes

Attention/executive: predictable accessible turns and optional break, fade turn cards; language/communication: child-origin expressive home-language, sign or AAC with meaningful real human understanding; literacy/phonology: reading/speech-bubble decoding NOT a prerequisite; numeracy: no rigid count of lines or gaze seconds; autism/sensory: low-arousal quiet partner opportunities and no forced gaze or touch; intellectual/developmental: adult-only concrete modeling before independent actual new dialogue; physical/motor/hearing/vision: accessible materials, neutral hearing/vision/communication support, genuine learner-directed turn signal and individualized specialist verification without claiming equivalence from adult-authored words. SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: prerecorded/chatbot people can rehearse but do not qualify as real consenting human conversation for source. CO-PLAY: real caregiver may satisfy adult conversation; PK3 still needs distinct genuine familiar peer participation. ADULT-LED: actual non-scripted listener rather than teacher cue completes adult opportunity, with peer opportunity separately for PK3. CLASSROOM: actual available adult staffing supervises all children, teacher privately observes feasible focal individual genuine conversations per interval and rotates/defer; absent contexts NOT_OBSERVED, no whole-class inference from chorus or forced partner count. No actual child outcome, professional acceptance, performed later REVISIT, field print or functional four-mode parity is claimed.
