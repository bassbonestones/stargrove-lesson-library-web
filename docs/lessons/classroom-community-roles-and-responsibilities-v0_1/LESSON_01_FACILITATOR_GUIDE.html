Lesson 01 — individual facilitator guide

Distinct, full original instructional plan: LESSON_01_ACTUAL_CLASS_WAYFINDING_HELPER.md. This is the unique teacher/facilitator guide for THIS method, not a fourth lesson plan. TEACHER_GUIDE.md is the uncounted objective-wide educator synthesis.

Exact printed outcome and practical grade boundaries

PK3.I.C.2 — Child assumes roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community with adult assistance. (original research-cell SHA-256 9d1ca3a1f8863d746ba098b7554d9457dcd922cc514bb5d3195c8de0a166d9c2). Preserve: PK3 child actually ASSUMES ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES AS PART OF CLASSROOM COMMUNITY WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE. Meaningful responsibility must benefit or support real other class members rather than mere fictional role, independent teacher-only production or badge. Adult assistance is fully compatible.

PK4.I.C.2 — Child assumes various roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community. (original research-cell SHA-256 207dd56ee81635ffc4d3540a8af6223bcf5c133adca2c1edd282aef85bceb191). Preserve: PK4 child actually ASSUMES VARIOUS ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES as part of genuine classroom community; one repeated static job cannot by itself prove 'various'. Meaningful child-owned contribution to multiple distinct safe actual classroom responsibilities is required without coercive compulsory classroom labor.

Distinct child action and assessment mechanism

Actually useful safe class activity invitation and wayfinding pictorial helper, with a child-owned truthful accessible cue ready for genuine classmates to use, even before actual peer uptake; peer consultation is recorded only if witnessed. This method differs from other information and class planning roles.

Preparation: authenticity, consent, accessible physical/communication materials

Use an actual teacher-authorized safe class activity at familiar or new place with accurate accessible child-chosen cue ready for real classmates. Peers need not be observed using it immediately; record actual uptake separately. One teacher can supervise one pupil; home-only practice is precursor, not formal classroom source without real class link.

The setting must be genuine, not fabricated children's performance. Only safe, non-coercive opportunities; do not stage social conflict, engineer distress, remove needs or rank children. If real peer/real objects/working tool/appropriate group are necessary and missing, mark the corresponding objective NOT_OBSERVED, even if a fictional practice exercise works well.

ENTRY — first learner-authored meaningful opportunity

Before teacher creates sign, child chooses how to help real group find the actual safe optional class activity and makes meaningful own location picture/word/AAC choice with adult guidance if PK3.

Document exact real source and child first response before teacher cue, actual date, learner's original choice/action, real human willingness, individual access, grade and relevant outcome code(s). If only an adult guessed what the child meant, do not award child-origin evidence.

MODEL — separate adult-owned example

The SAME teacher may model an unrelated adult-owned staff-only class note on different materials; no colleague required, no adult choosing child cue.

This teacher-owned distinct safe example teaches WHY a response works, but cannot supply the learner's later answer, stimulus, motor movement, listener response or evidence for another child's actual performance.

DURING — facilitated practice with contingent corrective feedback

Help child select genuinely accurate room/place information and personally contribute to visible practical wayfinding; PK3 assistance accepted, PK4 also practices separate other actual classroom role elsewhere. Respect students' genuine use and child own safe choices.

Track U learner-origin class role, H adult chooses role/content, A neutral access/motor/AAC, X uncertain. Fade answer cues while retaining teacher safety and communication access. An independent child contribution may happen in the SAME familiar class activity; never force new location, materials or actor.

EXIT — authentic materially changed source, not memorized imitation

A real authorized current/upcoming class activity receives learner-chosen accurate accessible wayfinding cue ready for real classmates, at familiar or changed place. Teacher verifies and safely places under child-directed decision; PK3 help available/direct as appropriate. PK4 different functional role contributions must be genuinely observed, not multiple sign colors; peer uptake and new venue are optional stronger evidence.

Record separately for each printed official ID whether the precise verbs and source context were actually observed. A child's meaningful partial evidence is a candidate, not verified developmental mastery. Where the source has different PK3 and PK4 requirements, neither automatically inherits the other's threshold, and prompt allowances must be recorded truthfully.

What would be false credit and how to repair it

Teacher selects all sign meaning and child only wears badge: no owned contribution. But a real child-authored class-ready cue can be meaningful even when peer use not yet observed. Preserve agency, privacy and teacher safety.

REVISIT — later genuine day, never author-invented

Only a genuine witnessed later calendar day with actual class-use child role may be called REVISIT, familiar setting allowed; new materials/people optional. REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. No child outcome field evidence.

Seven learning-difference access profiles — source-conserving differentiation

Attention/executive: Short genuinely useful real group duty and safe choice, with meaningful break and optional alternate role.

Language/communication: Child-selected group information/role via AAC/sign/home language, real classmates' comprehension when needed. Learner-controlled home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) can preserve meaning; an adult acting as interpreter must not invent it.

Literacy/phonological: Class symbols can aid wayfinding but real performed role cannot be replaced by poster recognition.

Numeracy: No role-count quota PK3; PK4 various truly different meaningful responsibilities rather than arbitrary time cards.

Autism/sensory: No mandatory public performance or forced group service, quiet private alternate beneficial role.

Intellectual/developmental: PK3 adult-assisted duty legitimate; PK4 actual diverse child-owned roles, not teacher badges.

Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Reachable genuine supplies and child-directed neutral motor proxy for class contribution; no unsafe carrying or public labor ranking.

This method's additional access-versus-concept check: Child selects a safe accurate class-ready cue for genuine authorized classmates, even at familiar center. Adult verifies and safely posts under child's decision; child-directed AAC/motor proxy follows learner's content rather than choosing it. PK3 adult support available and direct when useful; PK4 actual distinct responsibilities required. Observed peer uptake and new location are optional stronger evidence.

Four fully written but not field-equivalent delivery modes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: future safe child-operable narrated rehearsal and custom access may be viable, but there is NO current working learner-tested application, automatic observation or equivalent validated mode. CO-PLAY: caregiver protects access, consent and responsive partner availability while child owns concept/message/meaningful action. ADULT-LED: adult demonstrates truly different own MODEL, child practices with support and later produces new unmodeled EXIT evidence. CLASSROOM: genuine individualized learner-source observations with safe, consenting relationships and group work where required; avoid group chorus/teacher action substitution. All four are written designs, not empirical evidence of real child learning, special educator approval, physical accessibility field acceptance or functional parity. COMM56-06 — “Various roles and responsibilities” is functional, not a job-title tally: An older child may make two genuinely different useful real class contributions within one integrated project/helper role: e.g. personally choose a truthful class-ready wayfinding cue and on another authentic task personally contribute a different teacher-authorized group-choice display. If the two have substantively distinct function, their role heading being the same does not invalidate variety. Conversely selecting a picture and helping hang the SAME sign are one wayfinding responsibility, not two, even with two movements. Three renamed badges remain zero real additional functions. Shared synthetic helper records role-types and functionally different responsibility-types separately, with a conservative at-least-two authentic-function sanity check that is not an official published numeric cut score, not mastery and not necessarily a one-session requirement. PK3 still may do one genuinely beneficial role with adult support/supervision.
