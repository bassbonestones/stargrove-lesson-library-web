Lesson 03 — individual facilitator guide

Distinct, full original instructional plan: LESSON_03_COMMUNITY_CHOICE_AND_ROLE_ROTATION.md. This is the unique teacher/facilitator guide for THIS method, not a fourth lesson plan. TEACHER_GUIDE.md is the uncounted objective-wide educator synthesis.

Exact printed outcome and practical grade boundaries

PK3.I.C.2 — Child assumes roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community with adult assistance. (original research-cell SHA-256 9d1ca3a1f8863d746ba098b7554d9457dcd922cc514bb5d3195c8de0a166d9c2). Preserve: PK3 child actually ASSUMES ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES AS PART OF CLASSROOM COMMUNITY WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE. Meaningful responsibility must benefit or support real other class members rather than mere fictional role, independent teacher-only production or badge. Adult assistance is fully compatible.

PK4.I.C.2 — Child assumes various roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community. (original research-cell SHA-256 207dd56ee81635ffc4d3540a8af6223bcf5c133adca2c1edd282aef85bceb191). Preserve: PK4 child actually ASSUMES VARIOUS ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES as part of genuine classroom community; one repeated static job cannot by itself prove 'various'. Meaningful child-owned contribution to multiple distinct safe actual classroom responsibilities is required without coercive compulsory classroom labor.

Distinct child action and assessment mechanism

Actual community-benefiting choice display for genuine safe authorized optional class plans plus naturally observed rotation across functionally distinct classroom roles, not merely a single voted preference or repeated cleanup sticker.

Preparation: authenticity, consent, accessible physical/communication materials

For the blank first-party choice board use ORIGINAL_COMMUNITY_CHOICE_DISPLAY_TEMPLATE.html; it is not itself a picture or an approved real class choice. A genuine teacher-approved, safe and actually available class choice, reachable display, local truth-checked large pictures/tactile cues or child-chosen sign/home language/AAC and real intended classmates. The first-party family mats teach role ideas, not ready-made verified activity cards; the optional lesson-specific blank display template requires current local cues. Another true class responsibility for PK4 can be observed on a later natural occasion; there is no labor quota.

The setting must be genuine, not fabricated children's performance. Only safe, non-coercive opportunities; do not stage social conflict, engineer distress, remove needs or rank children. If real peer/real objects/working tool/appropriate group are necessary and missing, mark the corresponding objective NOT_OBSERVED, even if a fictional practice exercise works well.

ENTRY — genuine first pupil-performed service (authored meaning separately)

Invite the child into a real willing safe helper responsibility, including an actual useful assigned pupil service with verified adult-authored words. Record precisely what the child PERFORMED, who chose informational meaning and PK3 assistance; do not force independent authored content as a prerequisite for official role.

Document real authorized source, date, child's own meaningful first response before an answer-giving model, any teacher prompt, safety/access help and class readiness. Note witnessed peer use only if truly seen; don't demand it as a prerequisite for an authorized ready role. If only an adult guessed what the child meant, do not award child-origin evidence.

MODEL — separate adult-owned example

The ONE adult/teacher shows a different unrelated adult-owned useful note/message on separate materials; never preselects the learner's two actual choices or requires a second adult.

This teacher-owned distinct safe example teaches WHY a response works, but cannot supply the learner's later answer, stimulus, motor movement, listener response or evidence for another child's actual performance.

DURING — facilitated practice with contingent corrective feedback

Help the group receive an accurate COMPLETE representation of both genuinely available optional class activities; an individual child may personally originate a consequential accurate cue for ONE option while the SAME adult or a consenting peer supplies the other true cue. That single meaningful coauthorship is valid richer informational learning when actually observed; do not require each pupil to independently author BOTH. A genuinely performed safe teacher-assigned delivery with entirely adult-written board can independently show source role but not pupil-origin content. Teacher verifies both, manages safety and retains final class decisions. As opportunities arise naturally, PK4 can contribute meaningfully in a DIFFERENT class-serving function, such as wayfinding or an approved nonprivate announcement. Distinguish distinct responsibilities by function, not two material-handling steps or repeated badges. PK3 real adult-assisted contribution suffices as bounded authentic role evidence.

Record in plain words the child's own decision and contribution, adult conceptual/answer-bearing prompts, access-only or safety help, and whether the information was actually class-ready. Adult-selected meaning is not child-origin evidence; child-selected meaning with adult-directed motor/AAC support remains learner owned. For PK3, helpful conceptual support can be legitimate adult assistance, but do not claim unprompted origin; note exactly which portion the child contributed. Fade answer-bearing conceptual support if an additional independent informational decision NATURALLY arises, without withholding adult safety or disability access. Correct misinformation using a different adult-owned example, but do not invent another class need, prescribe new room/actor or require the child to perform a second job to validate the genuine first role.

EXIT — actual source role and only optional genuinely new uncued information

Before recording partial information, confirm that the ORIGINAL pupil-origin cue itself was voluntarily expressed, safe, truthful, nonprivate, consequential and faithfully understood; this is SEPARATE from whether a LATER physical/public school service was safe or consented to. A later unsafe, unavailable or compelled role cannot earn school-service credit but cannot erase an already actually observed safe voluntary original idea. An originally coerced, private, invented, copied or adult-guessed cue must NEVER be credited as sound pupil-original information, even if the role was safely performed. One actually pupil-PERFORMED safe teacher-authorized class-serving duty (including an adult-ASSIGNED duty with verified adult-written options) already observed counts as bounded genuine source evidence without another class occasion. A child may first independently originate a safe, accurate meaningful class-option cue and later withdraw consent to post/do a public role: honor refusal, record source role NOT_OBSERVED, and preserve only the separately actually observed partial information thinking. Never require public display to document earlier voluntary meaning. Record meaningful original child informational choice as an additional learning observation, not universal role condition. Only if a genuinely different useful classroom information need naturally arises do an additional uncued semantic check; otherwise mark that extra check NOT_OBSERVED. A genuine class-ready contribution can be useful before peers are seen consulting it. PK4 variety remains NOT_OBSERVED until actual DIFFERENT functionally meaningful duties occur, not two steps of one board.

Record separately for each printed official ID whether the precise verbs and source context were actually observed. A child's meaningful partial evidence is a candidate, not verified developmental mastery. Where the source has different PK3 and PK4 requirements, neither automatically inherits the other's threshold, and prompt allowances must be recorded truthfully.

What would be false credit and how to repair it

Teacher rotates pretend paper badges 'leader', 'organizer', 'helper' during a puppet game but no actual class task occurs; labels do not establish real varied community responsibilities.

REVISIT — later genuine day, never author-invented

A genuine new safe learner-owned community contribution on a later actual calendar day; familiar class places, materials and classmates may remain. REVISIT NOT_OBSERVED. This plan is not field data or child learning evidence.

Seven learning-difference access profiles — source-conserving differentiation

Attention/executive: Short genuinely useful real group duty and safe choice, with meaningful break and optional alternate role.

Language/communication: Child-selected group information/role via AAC/sign/home language, real classmates' comprehension when needed. Learner-controlled home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) can preserve meaning; an adult acting as interpreter must not invent it.

Literacy/phonological: Class symbols can aid wayfinding but real performed role cannot be replaced by poster recognition.

Numeracy: No role-count quota PK3; PK4 various truly different meaningful responsibilities rather than arbitrary time cards.

Autism/sensory: No mandatory public performance or forced group service, quiet private alternate beneficial role.

Intellectual/developmental: PK3 adult-assisted duty legitimate; PK4 actual diverse child-owned roles, not teacher badges.

Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Reachable genuine supplies and child-directed neutral motor proxy for class contribution; no unsafe carrying or public labor ranking.

This method's additional access-versus-concept check: Distinguish actual pupil-PERFORMED assigned service using truthful adult-written information from a stronger child-ORIGIN informational decision. Neutral motor proxy may faithfully follow the child's choice, but must not invent it. A new independent semantic choice is optional when a second real class need naturally appears; otherwise NOT_OBSERVED. No staged second activity, mandatory public speech or false claim of observed peer consultation. PK4 varied duties need real different functions; adulthood safety supports remain allowed.

Four fully written but not field-equivalent delivery modes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: future safe child-operable narrated rehearsal and custom access may be viable, but there is NO current working learner-tested application, automatic observation or equivalent validated mode. CO-PLAY: caregiver protects access, consent and responsive partner availability while child owns concept/message/meaningful action. ADULT-LED: adult models unrelated safe example, child actually performs genuine age-safe class duty with support, and optional additional unmodeled informational evidence is sought only when another real purpose appears. CLASSROOM: genuine individualized learner-source observations with safe, consenting relationships and group work where required; avoid group chorus/teacher action substitution. All four are written designs, not empirical evidence of real child learning, special educator approval, physical accessibility field acceptance or functional parity.
