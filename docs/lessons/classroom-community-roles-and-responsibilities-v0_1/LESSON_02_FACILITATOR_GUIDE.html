Lesson 02 — individual facilitator guide

Distinct, full original instructional plan: LESSON_02_SHARED_INFORMATION_HELPER.md. This is the unique teacher/facilitator guide for THIS method, not a fourth lesson plan. TEACHER_GUIDE.md is the uncounted objective-wide educator synthesis.

Exact printed outcome and practical grade boundaries

PK3.I.C.2 — Child assumes roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community with adult assistance. (original research-cell SHA-256 9d1ca3a1f8863d746ba098b7554d9457dcd922cc514bb5d3195c8de0a166d9c2). Preserve: PK3 child actually ASSUMES ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES AS PART OF CLASSROOM COMMUNITY WITH ADULT ASSISTANCE. Meaningful responsibility must benefit or support real other class members rather than mere fictional role, independent teacher-only production or badge. Adult assistance is fully compatible.

PK4.I.C.2 — Child assumes various roles and responsibilities as part of the classroom community. (original research-cell SHA-256 207dd56ee81635ffc4d3540a8af6223bcf5c133adca2c1edd282aef85bceb191). Preserve: PK4 child actually ASSUMES VARIOUS ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES as part of genuine classroom community; one repeated static job cannot by itself prove 'various'. Meaningful child-owned contribution to multiple distinct safe actual classroom responsibilities is required without coercive compulsory classroom labor.

Distinct child action and assessment mechanism

Genuine accurate authorized nonprivate information/picture-message helper for actual classroom group recipients. Primary source: Texas Education Agency, 2022 Texas Prekindergarten Guidelines, original comprehensive PDF printed page 15, original PK3.I.C.2 and PK4.I.C.2 side-by-side plus adult-assigned classroom-helper child behaviors; visually verified 2026-11-12. Separate evidence dimensions: (1) real meaningful child-performed helpful class responsibility, including a safe adult-assigned task with adult assistance as the 2022 Texas printed page15 child-behavior example permits; (2) this Method02's richer learner-origin true information selection or purposeful representation, which must not be inferred from teacher-composed words; and (3) any genuinely new independent end decision, which cannot be inferred from repeating the same earlier act. No source child-role credit for merely posing/decorating while adult performs every actual service. Different from locating activity and group choice display.

Preparation: authenticity, consent, accessible physical/communication materials

A real teacher-authorized current or upcoming class information purpose, checked accurate and safe, with actual classmates who can use a ready accessible picture/tactile/sign/AAC cue. A teacher may assign a child an age-safe real delivery/display helper task while retaining responsibility for the words and safe placement: independently record whether the child actually performs useful group service, whether the child authored any informational content/representation, and whether a later separate meaning decision genuinely occurred. A visible same-session recipient is not needed; actual peer receipt/comprehension recorded only if observed. Teacher confirms authorization and handles display/safety. Familiar story theme valid; home family notice is rehearsal, not automatically classroom source. The optional original role-mat printable has a fourth-page child-facing illustrated book/leaf/art symbol starter and blank class-message frame. These samples are never self-verifying class facts: prepare truthful local facts and confirm child-selected meaning before posting. Ordinary objects, drawn symbols, sign, home language, tactile or chosen AAC are equally legitimate if meaningful and accessible; adult may safely transcribe a pupil-directed message. Earlier three printable pages are primarily adult/educator reference, not automatically child-readable picture choices.

The setting must be genuine, not fabricated children's performance. Only safe, non-coercive opportunities; do not stage social conflict, engineer distress, remove needs or rank children. If real peer/real objects/working tool/appropriate group are necessary and missing, mark the corresponding objective NOT_OBSERVED, even if a fictional practice exercise works well.

ENTRY — first learner-authored meaningful opportunity

Offer a genuine verified class need without dictating the learner's message; child chooses which verified true fact is useful or how their own meaningful accessible representation conveys it. Verify the first learner choice before adult demonstrates unrelated staff-only content. A decorative color change on a fully teacher-authored notice is practice, not an original information responsibility.

Document exact real class information purpose and child first response before teacher cue, actual date, learner's original choice/action and assent, genuine classmates as intended beneficiaries (not their prior consent to read), individual access, grade and relevant outcome code(s). Record actual recipient use or comprehension only if separately witnessed. If only an adult guessed what the child meant, do not award child-origin evidence.

MODEL — separate adult-owned example

The SAME teacher alone can demonstrate a different truthful adult-owned staff-only notice on unrelated materials, without a second colleague. Imaginary recipient reactions explicitly hypothetical, not actual school observation.

This teacher-owned distinct safe example teaches WHY a response works, but cannot supply the learner's later answer, stimulus, motor movement, listener response or evidence for another child's actual performance.

DURING — facilitated practice with contingent corrective feedback

Invite child-original choice of useful verified class information, or the learner's meaningful accessible way of conveying an actual authorized ongoing/upcoming class fact. Teacher may offer truthful options and verify content; do not count only decorating an already completed adult notice as message authorship. Use accessible sign/AAC/tactile/voice channels. A true class-ready notice is meaningful before peers consult it; actual witnessed comprehension separate. Adult safe mounting/transcription may preserve pupil meaning. PK3 teacher help allowed/available and PK4 different functionally substantive responsibilities over real occasions, not new poster topics or distinct titles alone.

Record R = actual meaningful child-performed classroom helper role observed, including a real safe adult-assigned duty; R_UNOBSERVED if the adult did all substantive service, only a costume/photo was produced, or no authentic class role opportunity occurred. Separately track Method02 information origin: U learner-origin meaningful information choice without answer cue; H learner-origin meaningful information with disclosed adult cognitive support; X no reliably learner-authored information decision (adult-written message/imaginary/uncertain, with the reason noted). X is not synonymous with no real classroom role when R is observed. Track A neutral learner-directed motor/sign/AAC/tactile access separately, and document teacher factual checks and safe mounting separately; A alone does not prove semantic authorship. Fade adult semantic answer prompts while preserving school safety, supervision and legitimate access. Use unrelated adult-owned error example, then invite an actual new learner-owned decision only where a useful authentic additional decision exists, which can occur with familiar theme/room; no compulsory new project or peer audience. If the first child-owned fact choice is only enacted or repeated later, that is one genuine role, not a second independently observed performance. A child who genuinely helps deliver the teacher's accurate notice without authoring its content still can show a real school role (R), while separate authored-information evidence is X and additional independent new decision NOT_OBSERVED.

EXIT — any truly new meaningful learner choice, familiar class context permitted

When an additional real meaningful information decision naturally arises, offer an uncued opportunity to originate a genuinely different consequential fact or communicative representation; note what was newly child-owned and the cognitive help, if any. Mere rehearsal of the same ENTRY/DURING answer is not a second independent check. If there is no additional actual useful need, record the original legitimate supported real classroom role once and mark separate end-check NOT_OBSERVED without inventing a new task. The real approved class content may be familiar; adult checks accuracy, permission and safe delivery. PK3 appropriate help available and direct when useful; older PK4 one information-helper task alone does not prove varied roles, but different real responsibilities within one project title can. Actual peer consultation and changed theme are optional stronger evidence, not mandatory.

Record separately for each printed official ID whether the precise verbs and source context were actually observed. A child's meaningful partial evidence is a candidate, not verified developmental mastery. Where the source has different PK3 and PK4 requirements, neither automatically inherits the other's threshold, and prompt allowances must be recorded truthfully.

What would be false credit and how to repair it

A puppet posts a fictional event note or teacher supplies all information while child merely points to arrow; no real responsibility or genuine recipient benefit.

REVISIT — later genuine day, never author-invented

Only an actually witnessed later-date real student-authored class contribution can be called later evidence, including familiar theme/location; new topic/recipient optional stronger transfer. Calendar plan or hypothetical peer acknowledgement NOT_OBSERVED. No child learning outcomes claimed.

Seven learning-difference access profiles — source-conserving differentiation

Attention/executive: Short genuinely useful real group duty and safe choice, with meaningful break and optional alternate role.

Language/communication: Child-selected group information/role via AAC/sign/home language; verify prospective message accessibility and record witnessed classmate comprehension separately, never infer it from availability. Learner-controlled home language, sign or augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) can preserve meaning; an adult acting as interpreter must not invent it.

Literacy/phonological: Class symbols can aid wayfinding but real performed role cannot be replaced by poster recognition.

Numeracy: No role-count quota PK3; PK4 various truly different meaningful responsibilities rather than arbitrary time cards.

Autism/sensory: No mandatory public performance or forced group service, quiet private alternate beneficial role.

Intellectual/developmental: PK3 adult-assisted duty legitimate; PK4 actual diverse child-owned roles, not teacher badges.

Physical/motor/hearing/vision: Reachable genuine supplies and child-directed neutral motor proxy for class contribution; no unsafe carrying or public labor ranking.

This method’s additional access-versus-concept check: Child meaningfully chooses truthful accessible safe nonprivate class message for actual real classmates, with teacher checking accuracy/permission and safely conveying child content; adult motor proxy can preserve origin. Familiar theme is legitimate; peer consultation separately observed. Another different responsibility function may show PK4 variety under same project title; do not count new sign color/theme alone. PK3 help permissible, not compulsory artificial prompts.

Four fully written but not field-equivalent delivery modes

SOLO — NOT IMPLEMENTED: future safe child-operable narrated rehearsal and custom access may be viable, but there is NO current working learner-tested application, automatic observation or equivalent validated mode. CO-PLAY: caregiver protects access, consent and responsive partner availability while child owns concept/message/meaningful action. ADULT-LED: One teacher can model an unrelated own information notice without extra staff, guide actual child-chosen real class-ready message, and honor source even with familiar topic and peer uptake not observed. Home caregiver equivalent meaningful precursor but not real classroom source unless genuine authorized class connection. CLASSROOM: Begin with a brief real shared information challenge in a safely supervised whole group or smaller natural groups, asking what actual current class information classmates could use without first supplying all child meaning. Show teacher-owned unrelated fact-check model. Invite several willing children over safe short creation turns to make separately identifiable useful semantic contributions to a shared real class notice or related accessible messages; maintain one-teacher sightlines with other pupils in normal safe activities. Teacher verifies truth/access, retains privacy and mounting responsibility, and may ask optional group reflection about intended meaning. Observers and chorus participants may be engaged learners but are not automatically credited original class-role evidence; note whether a pupil suggested the first true useful information, subsequently co-authored a meaningful different decision after hearing peers, or only repeated a public answer; no forced every-child same-day quota. Actual classmates are intended recipients, immediate witnessed reading optional additional observation. Record older different genuine role functions over actual occasions; no coerced public announcements, invented second staffer, sensitive records or safety duties. All four are written designs, not empirical evidence of real child learning, special educator approval, physical accessibility field acceptance or functional parity.
